## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** man of 55; soft, round-shouldered, small hands; looks harmless on purpose.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **short and slight, almost frail**; pale scalp and skin; moves softly, shuffling in felt slippers, hands hidden in his sleeves; never hurries, never stands in the middle of a room. Silhouette: a small grey shape at the edge of the frame.
- **Hair:** **shaved head**, pale scalp; no beard; thin dark eyebrows.
- **Eyes:** pale grey, heavy-lidded, always half-closed — listening.
- **Distinctive mark:** a slight tremor in the LEFT hand (from S4 on; he hides it in his sleeve).
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **slate-grey robe with sulphur-yellow lining** (the lining shows when he moves). Main colour: slate grey.
- **Signature item:** **silver brooch shaped like a hare's ear** at the right collar; from S1E12 the Helm's seal on a chain.
- **Changes:** S5 (age 63): thinner, the tremor visible, robe darker.

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** near-whisper, never hurried; mid-low, soft consonants; Highwater court accent with old-clerk precision. People lean in to hear him — that is the point.
- **Speech habits:** asks questions he knows the answer to; repeats the other person's last word as a question; never threatens directly; tender words about his son.
- **Catch-phrase:** "Nothing unheard." (house words, spoken gently) · "Steadiness."
- **Never says:** a lie that can be checked; "I don't know" — he says "Not yet."
- **Sample line:** *"Order is a kindness, my dear. The flood is not cruel; it is only untidy."*
- **Delivery for AI:** his near-whisper suits `[OS]` and over-the-shoulder shots; `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 3 in S1: E2 "steadiness", E6 genuinely shaken, E12 taking the seal.

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** the Helm's chair — and for his upstart house to be accepted by the old ones.
- **Needs (inner):** to be loved for something other than what he knows. His son is the only person who could give it, and refuses.
- **Fears:** being laughed at; not knowing; his son hating him.
- **Heroism (villain's):** he genuinely believes order is mercy, and in S1E6 he is honestly shaken to learn the Helm was murdered — he did not do it.

## Biography
Master of the Relay and the Hearings, head of a house of clerks raised to great house in CY 251. Patient, sentimental about his son Zev, merciless to anyone who threatens the order he keeps. Everyone believes he poisoned Helm Lucan; he did not (SEC-001 is Ruel's). The death-tone came "too fast" in S1E1 because a Listener in the Dry Palace sent it the moment the Helm fell — Odo was already dressed because he is always dressed.

Client of the bank; ally of Dask through bread; the Choir votes for him because the Choir owes the bank its roof. He wins on the Sunken Vote (S1E12), and his first act — Ninth Sluice declared "reclaimed land" — completes the bank's grab. He does not tell the First Cantor what Fitch saw in the Lows: information is his coin. As Helm he becomes what he always justified: Listener Law, the hunt for "the girl whose shard rang", Nan's death (S2E8, by his Listeners).
