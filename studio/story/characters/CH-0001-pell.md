## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** girl of 15 (S1–S4); small, wiry, all elbows and calves; sun- and wind-darkened skin with freckles across the nose. After the skip (S5, 23): same build, harder face, shoulders of someone who rows.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **short and wiry** (shortest of the S1 heroes); olive-brown delta skin, freckles; leans forward when she walks — a mudlark's wading gait, knees slightly bent, quick small steps; shins criss-crossed with old shell-cuts (work); nails broken short. Silhouette: small figure, one long braid swinging on the left.
- **Hair:** cropped **rust-brown** (red-brown, never auburn-bright) hair, ragged at the nape; **one long thin braid on the LEFT side**, bound at the end by a **blue tin sluice-tag** stamped "IX" (her father Dov's tag). The braid never moves to the right, never gets cut.
- **Eyes:** sea-grey, narrow when she is calculating.
- **Distinctive mark:** none on the face (keeps the prompt light). Hands always mud-dark to the wrist.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **tar-brown patched oilskin jacket** (main colour: tar-brown), mud-grey wading leggings rolled to the knee, rag-wrapped feet or bare feet; a salvage net bag on the right hip.
- **Signature item:** the **echo-shard** — cracked black stone, no bigger than a thumbnail, on a knotted cord at the throat (exactly as `cast_overview.md`; no history is given here).
- **Changes over the saga:**
  - from **S2E8**: Nan's single **spiral-shell earring in the LEFT ear** (Nan's custody passes to her at Nan's death);
  - **S4E8 → S4E11**: the shard is not on her (Ione has it); director must not show it on Pell in those episodes;
  - from **S4E12**: Ione's silver tuning fork, carried at her belt (the director fixes how it is worn; it must not hang on the shard's cord);
  - S5+: the tin tag is dented and green with verdigris; the braid is the same length (she trims the rest, never the braid);
  - after **S12E12**: voiceless; signs.

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** low for a girl (light alto), fast, clipped; Ninth Sluice accent — swallowed "t"s ("wa'er"), drops "g" ("nothin'"), flat vowels. Laughs through her nose. As an adult (S5+) the same voice, slower and lower, used to quieting crowds.
- **Speech habits:** Lows slang (see `lows_and_mids_life.md` §11); counts in floods and tides, not years; calls nobles "your dryness" to their faces; turns fear into jokes. **Whistles** two or three notes when she is nervous (Hush-Law reflex) — never the lullaby's five in public.
- **Catch-phrase:** "Water doesn't care who you are." / "Up the Steps" (her dream, said like a prayer).
- **Never says:** "my lord" / "my lady" straight; "please" to anyone above the dike; "I can't swim" (she can't, well — she never admits it).
- **Sample line:** *"Water doesn't care who you are. That's the only fair thing in this city."* · *"You want it found, you pay the mud. Mud doesn't do favours."*
- **Delivery for AI (S1):** most lines `[VO]`/`[MS]`; `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 5 in S1 — E1 "It's singing back" (Wick's line; hers is the reaction shot, no CU), E2 the net-mending talk with Nan, E5 "Your lot take ours. Did you know that?", E10 the cradle (one line), E12 on the ruins.

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** to get Wick and Nan "up the Steps" — a dry room in the Mids — before the next flood.
- **Needs (inner):** to stop believing that being alone is the same as being safe; to accept that what she carries (the tune, the stone, later her sister's fork) belongs to many people, not to her.
- **Fears:** deep water (she nearly drowned as a small child and has never said so); the Choir's robes; being the one who is left.
- **Heroism:** she keeps **going back into the water for other people** — Tibb's cradle in S1E10 is the first time, and she is terrified every time.

## Biography
Pell was born in Ninth Sluice (PL pending), the second child of Dov, a sluice-worker, and Liss, the daughter of the net-mender Nan Oshra. She was born after the Night of Still Water (EV pending, CY 295) and grew up in its shadow: half the lane's families had a name they did not say. She knows she had an elder sister "the water took before I was born"; she does not know the sister's name (SEC-004).

Her father died at the gates when she was five (CY 302, EV pending — a counterweight chain parted in a storm; proposal). Her mother "drowned in a dry year" when Pell was eight (CY 305; truth: SEC-012 — Pell does not know). From then on Nan raised her and Wick. Pell has worked the mud since she was nine: the back-canals, the silt of the failing Sluice Spire, the hours when the tide is lowest. She sells metal, buttons, lost purses and — illegally — chips of echo-stone to Mother Silt's fences. She is good at it, well liked, rude to tithe-collectors and quietly proud that Ninth Sluice children follow her.

Nan taught her one rule — never sing — and, against it, the first verse of a five-note lullaby, "for the house only". Pell hums it when she is frightened. On the lowest ebb of CY 312 (S1E1) she pulls a thumbnail of black stone out of the back-canal by the old sluice; when she hums, it hums back (SEC-002). Everything in her life after that follows from one sound.

What she inherits without knowing it: a family name, Oshra, that is older than the Concord (SEC-003); a sister who lives on the Steps (SEC-004); a mother who did not drown by accident (SEC-012). Where the shard came from is `[SEALED — ask lead]`.

## Love line — L4 "Kites over the Wall" (Revision r7)
**Age gate (hard rule, DEC-71):** Pell is 15 in S1 (born CY 297). In **S1–S4** she carries **no romantic beat, no hint, no look held too long** — with Lio, Hollis, Esk or anyone. Lio and Pell in S1 are friends of 15 and 17 and nothing else. She turns 18 in the skip; romance is allowed from S5 (23) but not used there. No retroactive framing: nobody ever says "since we were children"; flashbacks to her under 18 are never shot or scored as romance.

- **Partner:** **Esk** (CH-0040), first met as adults in **S7** (CY 322): she is 25, the voice of the Hum; he is 23, kite-master and envoy of the uplands, "the Dry Queen's nephew". What they share before a word is spoken: **Kes Mabry** — his guardian, and the sergeant who opened the gate of her district.
- **The wall:** she believes the Dry Queen sent the wave that killed her sister (S4E11). In S7E6 she learns the envoy is the Dry Queen's **son**. Love across the saga's deepest quarrel — the delta's song and the upland's thirst — and the one line that ends alive.
