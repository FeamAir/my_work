## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** young woman of 18 (20 at death); tall, straight-backed, a singer's chest and long neck; pale, indoor skin.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **tall and slender**, very upright (a singer's posture, trained since childhood); pale indoor skin — paler than any Lows face; walks slowly and evenly, as in a procession. From S1E10 her right hand rises to her throat when she is moved (habit, not a sign). Silhouette: tall, straight, long silver fall of hair.
- **Hair:** **long, straight, pale-silver hair** to the waist, worn loose with two thin side-plaits joined at the back. The silver is the weekly "Cantor's rinse" (SEC-004): **dark roots must be visible in S3 (Pale Crag confinement) and S4**, growing to a hand's width by S4E1; from S4E1 she lets them show.
- **Eyes:** dark brown (note for art: the rinse makes this contrast striking; nobody in House Ilvane comments on it).
- **Distinctive mark:** a faint red line across the throat after S1E10 (the Great Song), hidden by the band.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** House Ilvane white with pale-violet trim, under a novice's undyed-linen Choir stole; soft white boots. Main colour: **white**.
- **Signature items:** (1) **violet silk throat-band** — becomes **black** from S1E11 (after the voice is lost); (2) **silver tuning-fork pendant** on a fine chain at the breastbone, which she strikes on her knuckle to take her pitch.
- **Changes over the saga:** S1E11 band turns black; S3 roots; S4 plain grey-white travelling coat over the white (in hiding); S4E8–E11 she wears Pell's shard; the fork passes to Pell at S4E12.

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character (S1E1–E10):** clear, light, high-mid; precise diction of Pale Crag; a singer's breath control — she never runs out of air mid-sentence. **From S1E10: no voice.** Communicates in Choir hand-sign (a formal sign language Cantors use when hoarse), later adapted with Pell's street gestures. Interpreted by: **Brother Elm** (S1E11 – first lessons), **Cressa** (S2 onward, at home), **Pell** (S4, without needing words). Inner thoughts as `[VO]` in her own pre-E10 voice, used sparingly (≤3 per episode).
- **Speech habits (before E10):** formal, complete sentences, scriptural cadence ("Hear, and be still"); asks questions as statements. **Signing style (after E10):** small, exact, close to the body, like someone who was taught to be silent in temples; when angry her signs get bigger, not faster.
- **Catch-phrase:** "What is the song for?" (first spoken, later her first full sentence in sign).
- **Never says:** "commoner" or "Lowfolk" (she says "the people of the Ninth"); never lies outright — she withholds.
- **Sample line:** *"If the song is a gift, then whom is it given to?"* · (signed, subtitled) *"I gave it away. It was mine to give."*

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** to be worthy of the song and to save the failing Sluice Spire.
- **Needs (inner):** to learn that obedience and goodness are not the same thing; later, that a name given and a name stolen can both be true.
- **Fears:** that the song is for sale (it is); that she is not who she was told she is (she is not).
- **Heroism:** she **gives up the thing she loves most for strangers** — her voice (S1E10) and then her breath (S4E11).

## Biography
Officially the only daughter of Lord Aurin Ilvane and Lady Cressa, granddaughter of First Cantor Vesna; raised at Pale Crag and in the Ilvane townhouse, trained for the Choir from early childhood. She is the most gifted novice of her year and the only Ilvane who walks into the Lows "to look at what the songs are for".

In truth she was born **Ama**, of the Ninth, elder daughter of Dov and Liss (SEC-004), and taken from a rescue boat on the Night of Still Water (CY 295, EV pending) by the Gleaner Sister Venn; she was installed in Cressa's nursery after Cressa's own baby died. Her silver hair is a rinse; nobody at Pale Crag has seen its real colour since she was two. As a baby she had already learned two notes of her mother's lullaby — the two notes she hums back to Pell at the Sluice Spire in S1E5 without knowing why.

In S1 she serves the Sluice Spire nightly, notices that the Choir's novices include "foundlings blessed by the Lady" (SEC-005), hears her grandmother commit the Choir's vote to Fennick, and on the third Still Day climbs the Sluice Spire against orders and sings a Great Song alone. A low hum from a rooftop joins her. She wakes mute.

**Keeps acting after death:** her tuning fork (Pell's from S4E12; gives the Great Chorus its pitch in S12E12); her **journal**, published by Cressa in S5E6 with the Gleaner rolls and Silt's ledger (the proof that the ear is learned, SEC-005); the question of who sent the wave (SEC-024 — `[SEALED — ask lead]`).
