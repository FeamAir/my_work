# Second circle — the Lows

Version 1.0 · 2026-11-22 · author: writer-characters · Canon: v0.1 (+ world package r2) · Spoiler level: **internal**
Format: one machine block + short dossier (function · secret · link to the past · look anchors · voice line). Places (PL) and events (EV) are pending; names of places are given in prose.
**New-character proposals** in this file (need keeper IDs/approval): CH-0070 Sal Tern, CH-0073 Gant, CH-0074 Nim. Teens (Mags 15, Lio 17, Patch 12, Tibb): no romance or sexual content.
Lowfolk naming: one-syllable given name + "of the <sluice>"; a few families keep an old private surname (Oshra, Tern).

Silhouettes (build, skin, gait, marks of work and war) for every entry are summarised in `_anchors_matrix.md` §2a; investor rule — traits come from the world and biography, never from morality.

**Mags** (15) — Pell's mudlark friend and rival; the loudest laugh in the back-canal. *Function:* the Lows' first loss — drowns in the S1E10 flood. *Secret:* she steals for Mother Silt — and (proposal) in S1E2 she sells word of Pell's "singing stone" to Silt's fence Nim for one copper, which is how the Listeners get the lane, not just the log. Pell learns it in S2 from Nim, after Mags is dead (an episode puzzle with no one left to forgive). *Link:* her copper is the first small sale that moves the top (Fitch's hunt). *Look:* frizz of black hair tied up with a strip of red rag; a man's oversized waistcoat full of pockets. *Voice:* hoarse, cackling. *"Finders keep, losers weep, and Listeners pay double."* First seen E1 (proposal — mudlarking beside Pell).

**Lio** (17) — Pell's friend, a street whistler, the best ear in the Ninth. *Function:* sings aloud at Ruel's rally (S1E2), a night on the tide-post (E3), and vanishes; returns in S6 as **the Pearl Pretender**, claiming Ashare blood. *Secret (SEC-016):* the claim was invented by Quell salvage-brokers and Piers Lantry to sell "Lirenne salvage bonds"; **Lio believes it**. *Link:* a whistler's tune on a Quell deck (S2E2) is the only trace of him between S1E3 and S6E1. Dying (S8E6) he asks the crowd to raise Lirenne anyway — the brokers' slogan becomes the movement's aim. *Look:* S1 — lanky, **black hair with a white streak over the LEFT brow**, patched sky-blue scarf; S6 — the streak kept, a pearl-and-teal coat (Ashare colours) and a fake spiral-shell brooch; he draws the spiral the wrong way (S6E3). *Voice:* light tenor, beautiful whistle; after the tide-post he never sings again until S6. *"I didn't sing for them. I sang because the crowd was singing inside and nobody let it out."* Relation to Pell lives in CH-0001.

**Corr the Reedmaster** (50) — reedmaster (gang boss) of the Ninth Sluice treadmill walkers. *Function:* S1E5 *"They always break the week before the Still. Funny, that."*; in the E10 flood pockets the **clean-sawn axle-pin** (SEC-028); runs the walkers' secret union (proposal: needs its own SEC id); blackmails the Pumpwrights in S5E8 so their barges hold the district on the Night of a Thousand Voices; the treadmill strike prices the S6 election; brings the walkers into the chorus (S11). Main from S11. *Link to the past:* walked the wheel himself on the Night of Still Water; the pumps "broke" that night too (proposal — the Guild's racket is older than Dorran). *Look:* barrel-bodied, huge thighs; **LEFT hand lost in a pump-gear** at thirty (he still walks the wheel); bald, sun-black scalp, **iron-grey beard plaited into one rope**, a reed whistle on a thong (the walkers keep step by it — Hush-Law culture); thighs like pilings. *Voice:* bass, slow, every sentence a step. *"A wheel doesn't care who walks it. Neither do the men who own it. So we'll care for ourselves."*
