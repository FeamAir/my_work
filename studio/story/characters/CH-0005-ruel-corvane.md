## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** man of 48; big-chested, sailor's shoulders, beginning to thicken.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **big and heavy-chested, thickening at the middle** (a man who eats well and rows hard); deep sea-weathered tan; rolling sailor's gait on land; fills a doorway. Silhouette: the widest figure in the S1 cast, long coat flaring.
- **Hair:** **salt-and-pepper (dark, grey-streaked) hair in a short sailor's queue**, and a **grey-streaked dark beard**, trimmed square.
- **Eyes:** warm hazel, laugh-lines.
- **Distinctive mark:** a white rope-scar across the back of the right hand.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **deep-blue admiral's long coat** with silver braid and the **silver cormorant clasp** at the collar. Main colour: deep blue (long coat — Maren's blue is the *short* riding coat; see the report).
- **Signature item:** **black pearl ring on the LEFT hand** — the Admiral's Pearl, a Corvane heirloom, hinged and hollow (as `cast_overview.md`; no further history here). He shakes hands **left-handed** with men he means to charm.
- **Changes:** S1E8 stabbed — bloodied coat; S1E9 wake — the ring still on his hand; it is gone after S1E11.

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** warm baritone, round, big; carries across a quay without effort; Gullmouth coast accent (open vowels).
- **Speech habits:** sea idioms ("hard over", "make fast", "a following sea"); calls everyone by name, Lows people included; jokes before bad news.
- **Catch-phrase:** "We go under and come up." (house words — he says them like a promise).
- **Never says:** "the Lows" with contempt; "I'm sorry"; the name of Helm Lucan after E1.
- **Sample line:** *"I don't drink before a storm."* (E1, `[ECHO: SEC-001]`) · *"Forgive what I did for them."* (last words, E9, `[ECHO: SEC-001]`)

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** the Helm's chair — to save the delta from the bank.
- **Needs (inner):** to admit that he has become what he fights; he never does.
- **Fears:** the Ninth drowning again — he rowed the boats on the Night of Still Water.
- **Heroism:** the "hero of the Still Water boats" — he went into the flood in CY 295 when no lord did. His crime and his heroism have the same root.

## Biography
Head of House Corvane and Lord Admiral of the Concord fleet; one daughter, Maren (Maren's mother and the rest of the family: writer-houses). On the Night of Still Water he took Corvane boats into Ninth Sluice, unasked, and pulled hundreds out; the Lows have loved him since. A reformer in speech — pumps for the Lows, an open Tithe Map — and, like his house, ruthless in practice; deep in debt to Lantry for the fleet refit.

In the season before the saga, relay-keeper **Bede Lark** sold him early news: the new Tithe Map would let Ninth Sluice flood on the Still Days. Ruel bought poison from **Sister Marl**, the Choir apothecary — paid by a Lantry sight-draft on the Corvane refit credit, forty marks, "by the Lord Admiral's own hand", dated Longdark 4 — and on Longdark 6 pricked Helm Lucan with the needle in his hollow pearl during a left-handed clasp (**SEC-001**). He declined the tithe cup ("I don't drink before a storm"). The Helm died mid-sentence on "…and the Ninth Sluice—". The proof is the draft, not a confession.

He did not know that Bede Lark had sold the same news to Ursel Lantry — and that the bank now knew he knew. He is stabbed on the Steps on Still 1 (E8) and dies on the *Patient Gull* on Still 2 (E9). His sister Ysa is already aboard (SEC-023).

**Keeps acting after death:** the ring (Maren takes it at the wake, S1E11; wears it from S6E12); the burned ledger page and its twin; the debt to Lantry; his last words; what his sister did to protect him (SEC-023); his crime made public (S6).

## Love line — L1 "The Second Lamp" (Revision r7)
*Age gate: Ruel 48, Jaro 36 in S1 — both adults; the line is live in S1 (DEC-71). Rating TV 16+: the camera never goes past the cabin door.*

- **Partner:** Captain-Lord **Jaro Quell** (CH-0019). Lovers since **CY 305** — the year the wrecker silver begins to pay the Corvane fleet (SEC-031). The money was always the shadow of the love; the cover for both is one sentence: *"an old friendship of sailors"*. Ruel has been a widower since CY 300.
- **Why it is hidden (never who — always votes and silver):** a candidate for Helm whose swing vote shares his cabin hands his rival a challenge; clan silver spent on a lover's fleet can depose Jaro at any Hull Moot. So: no signed letters, no shared roof. The signal is a **second stern lamp** on the *Patient Gull* — one lamp: the Admiral is aboard; two: come.
- **Motivation (what the love does inside him):** Jaro is the one man he never charms. He keeps him **out of the crime** — the needle never comes near him — and **out of the truth**: Ruel tells his sister, not his lover. The love is one more thing he keeps behind his back, which is exactly his flaw ("to admit that he has become what he fights; he never does"). His last unsent note, dated **Longdark 5** — between the poison draft (Longdark 4) and the Helm's death (Longdark 6) — begins *"If I asked you to sail tomorrow and not ask why—"* and stops.

- **Speech with Jaro (rules for script writers):** in the gallery Ruel **does not speak** — the hand is his line. The joke-before-bad-news habit turns, with Jaro only, into a plain warning (*"A storm is coming. Be ready too."*). In public he never names the tie; the Chronicler never states the cause (romance_lines §0.8).
- **Behaviour — the hand rule:** Ruel shakes **left-handed** with men he means to charm. With Jaro, **never**: he gives the right hand, the ringed left goes behind his back. This must hold in every shot of the two of them (E4, E6, E9). The scarred right hand (rope-scar) is the hand he gives.
