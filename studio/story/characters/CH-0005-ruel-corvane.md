- **Age band and build:** man of 48; big-chested, sailor's shoulders, beginning to thicken.
- **Hair:** **salt-and-pepper (dark, grey-streaked) hair in a short sailor's queue**, and a **grey-streaked dark beard**, trimmed square.
- **Eyes:** warm hazel, laugh-lines.
- **Distinctive mark:** a white rope-scar across the back of the right hand.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **deep-blue admiral's long coat** with silver braid and the **silver cormorant clasp** at the collar. Main colour: deep blue (long coat — Maren's blue is the *short* riding coat; see the report).

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** the Helm's chair — to save the delta from the bank.
- **Needs (inner):** to admit that he has become what he fights; he never does.
- **Fears:** the Ninth drowning again — he rowed the boats on the Night of Still Water.
- **Heroism:** the "hero of the Still Water boats" — he went into the flood in CY 295 when no lord did. His crime and his heroism have the same root.

## Biography
Head of House Corvane and Lord Admiral of the Concord fleet; one daughter, Maren (Maren's mother and the rest of the family: writer-houses). On the Night of Still Water he took Corvane boats into Ninth Sluice, unasked, and pulled hundreds out; the Lows have loved him since. A reformer in speech — pumps for the Lows, an open Tithe Map — and, like his house, ruthless in practice; deep in debt to Lantry for the fleet refit.

In the season before the saga, relay-keeper **Bede Lark** sold him early news: the new Tithe Map would let Ninth Sluice flood on the Still Days. Ruel bought poison from **Sister Marl**, the Choir apothecary — paid by a Lantry sight-draft on the Corvane refit credit, forty marks, "by the Lord Admiral's own hand", dated Longdark 4 — and on Longdark 6 pricked Helm Lucan with the needle in his hollow pearl during a left-handed clasp (**SEC-001**). He declined the tithe cup ("I don't drink before a storm"). The Helm died mid-sentence on "…and the Ninth Sluice—". The proof is the draft, not a confession.

He did not know that Bede Lark had sold the same news to Ursel Lantry — and that the bank now knew he knew. He is stabbed on the Steps on Still 1 (E8) and dies on the *Patient Gull* on Still 2 (E9). His sister Ysa is already aboard (SEC-023).

**Keeps acting after death:** the ring (Maren takes it at the wake, S1E11; wears it from S6E12); the burned ledger page and its twin; the debt to Lantry; his last words; what his sister did to protect him (SEC-023); his crime made public (S6).
