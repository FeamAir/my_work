## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** woman of 42; long-limbed, lean from thin years, a climber's hands.
- **Hair:** **shaved head**, sun-dark scalp, with **a thin blue kite-line tattoo running from the LEFT temple back over the crown** (proposal — keeps her from reading as "the other shaved head" beside Odo in S3–S5).
- **Eyes:** amber-brown, wind-creased.
- **Distinctive mark:** **one copper ring in the LEFT ear**.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **sand-orange layered coat** (three wind-layers, the outer one fraying), leather wrist-guards, cord sandals. Main colour: sand-orange.
- **Signature item:** **kite-spar staff** — a tall staff of bound kite-spars with a furled signal-kite at the top.
- **Changes:** S7+: a grey-green mourning cord tied on the staff (her people's dead of the famine).

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** dry, low alto, carries in wind; Ossari accent — hard "k" and "ts", short vowels, sentences that end on a falling tone; slower in the Concord tongue than in her own.
- **Speech habits:** plain, concrete nouns (water, wells, children, days); never uses an honorific for a Concord lord; counts in wells and dry years.
- **Catch-phrase:** "Count the wells." / (S3 on) "Your stones drink our rivers."
- **Never says:** "please"; "queen" about herself (that is the Concord's name for her); whether she breached the weir — she does not deny it.
- **Sample line:** *"You call it a river. Up here we call it the thing that leaves."*
- **Delivery for AI:** wide shots on the rim, back to camera with the staff — iconic; `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 3 per season (S3 onwards).

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** water for the plateau — the Falls Spire silent for one season so the Shelf lakes and wells can refill (S3); then to hold the rim.
- **Needs (inner):** to let someone else carry part of the weight — including her son, whom she gave away to keep a peace.
- **Fears:** the empty well; being the leader who chose the wrong children to save.
- **Heroism:** she **takes the blame of her people on herself** — sometimes blame that is not hers to take.

## Biography
War-leader of the Ossari kite-clans, chosen by the clan council in the dust years (writer-houses: council, clans, date). Her people's rivers and wells have been dying for generations; the Concord says the stone is old and the rains are fickle. She knows the falls swell on days without rain and cannot prove why (SEC-008, believed from S3, proven S7E10).

Esk is **her son**, given to the Stair as a hostage under a border truce as "her nephew" (SEC-019); his official mother is her sister Dakka (proposal). She has watched his kite from the rim for five years.

In S4 her people build a weir at the lip of the falls; in council (E9) she threatens to breach it; on the third Still Day it goes, and the wave runs down the Aun. She **does not deny it** `[SEALED — ask lead]`, and takes the Weir Terms.
