## 0. Bodies and peoples (investor's instruction, 2026-11-22)
People in the saga differ as in life — build, height, age, skin, faces of different peoples, scars, marks of illness and work, missing limbs, deafness or muteness, posture and gait. A recognisable **silhouette** is the strongest consistency anchor for AI generation. Rules used in every dossier:
- each main hero and each second-circle character differs by **silhouette + at least 2 anchors**;
- every physical trait is **explained by the world or the biography** (pumps and treadmills, war, floods, salt, lime, class, indoor or outdoor life) — never random;
- **no caricature, no stereotype:** weight, disability or a scar never marks a character's morality. Heavy or disabled characters are spread across all sides (Silt, Ruel, Dorran, Cressa, Juno, Gant; Jaro's hand, Corr's hand, Silt's knees, Itsek's leg, Rook's eye, Sabin's muteness, Bede's half-deafness, Wick's deaf ear).

**Peoples of the saga (proposal for the canon keeper and art team; not tied to virtue or class):**
| People | Skin and faces (range) | Typical marks of life |
|---|---|---|
| Delta folk (the Lows and most of the Mids) | olive to deep brown, every mix — the delta is a crossroads | shell-cuts on shins, rope-calluses, damp coughs, salt-cracked hands |
| Old Steps houses | wide range; the Ilvane pale from indoor life and in-marriage | soft hands, straight backs |
| Gullmouth / Corvane coast | sea-weathered tan to brown | rope-scars, rolling gait |
| Shoals islanders (Quell) | deep brown, often freckled across the nose | missing fingers (ropes, wrecks), squint lines |
| Polder folk (Dask, Moll) | fair to ruddy, broad-boned | clay-grey hands, strong backs, sunburn |
| Uplanders below the Stair (Rusk) | ruddy, light to light-brown, high-coloured cheeks | powder burns, pike wounds |
| Ossari (kite-clans) | brown to deep bronze, wind-creased eyes | climbing scars, kite-cord scars, thinness of the dry years |

## 3. Conflicts inherited from the frame files (for the lead / director)
1. **Grey coats:** Hollis (grey clerk's coat, `cast_overview.md`) vs Odo (slate grey) — both S1–S5 main faces. Mitigated here as "light ash-grey" vs "slate + sulphur lining". Alternative: Hollis in warm taupe/dun. Director's call.
2. **Corvane blue:** Ruel (deep-blue coat) vs Maren (deep-blue riding coat) — both S1 main faces (Ruel to E9). Mitigated: long vs short cut, different hair. Alternative: Maren in a sea-grey officer's jacket with blue facings in S1, the Corvane blue only from S2 (when she becomes the house).
3. **Shaved heads:** Odo vs Tsura — both main faces S3–S5. Mitigated: Tsura's skin tone, kite-line tattoo, copper ring and orange coat. Director to confirm the tattoo.
4. **Brown hair family:** Pell (rust-brown), Maren (umber), Elm (ash-brown), Wick (mouse-brown). Distinct by style and by the anchors (braid / high tail / tonsure / cap); Wick's cap covers his hair in most shots.
