## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** woman of 73; small, very straight; moves slowly, never hesitates.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **small and straight**; only her hands are ever seen — pale, age-spotted, long fingers heavy with rings; she moves slowly and never hesitates; stands perfectly still while the Voice speaks. Silhouette: a small veiled figure, one ringed hand raised.
- **Hair:** **white**, long, almost never seen — under the veil.
- **Face:** **always veiled** — a **violet veil** from crown to collarbone; the face is never shown (no face reference — production gift).
- **Distinctive mark / signature item:** **silver rings on every finger of the signing hand (the RIGHT)**; when moved, those fingers **tap five beats** on whatever is under them (S1E12 on the Ring's rail).
- **Usual clothes and colours:** Choir robe of undyed linen under a white-and-violet First Cantor's mantle.
- **Changes:** S10 prisoner — the mantle taken; veil and rings kept.

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** none. **Mute for forty years** (the cost of a Great Song). **Never lip-sync.**
- **Interpreted by:** **Brother Elm** (S1–S8E9) — he reads her hand-signs aloud in his own voice; then **Daro Vey** (S8–S10E9, voices her last words). Subtitles carry the *literal* sign gloss only when a scene needs the audience to see that the Voice has softened her (S1E8: Elm's eyes say he has softened it).
- **Signing style:** formal Choir sign, small and slow, the ringed hand doing most of the work; never repeats a sign; the five taps are not a sign — they are the one thing she does not control.
- **Sample line (via the Voice):** *"The Choir owes the bank its roof."* (S1E8) · *"Hear, and be still."*
- **What she never "says":** her own name; "I".

## Biography
Head of the Choir and the real power of House Ilvane; mother of Lord Aurin; official grandmother of Ione. Kind to children, merciless to the realm. Her Choir is short of Cantors and growing shorter, because the ear is learned only from the forbidden Lows lullaby (SEC-005) — the Choir cannot make its own singers and gleans the Lows' children under seven. In CY 305 she ordered the death of Liss of the Ninth, who had found Sister Venn (SEC-012). Brother Elm, her Voice, was planted in the Choir by the Quiet (SEC-012), and sometimes mistranslates her on purpose. She votes the Choir for Fennick (S1E7) because the Choir owes the bank its roof, and as the speaker for the drowned casts the Sunken Vote that makes Odo Helm (S1E12).
