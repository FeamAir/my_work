## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** S1–S4: food, adventure, to be taken seriously by Pell. S5+: to protect Pell from outside the law — he joins the watch so someone inside it is on her side.
- **Needs (inner):** to be his own man, not Pell's little brother — which he becomes only by walking into her cause.
- **Fears:** being left behind; water at night.
- **Heroism:** he **stands between his sister's people and the soldiers** — in S5 and at the Ninth Bridge.

## Biography
Born four years before his mother drowned; he does not remember his father. Grew up in Nan's stilt-house, mudlarking at Pell's heels, the comic eyes of the Lows. On the lowest ebb of CY 312 he is the only one who sees the stone hum back (S1E1). On the Steps day (S1E2) he steals a cadet cap from the stiff young officer at Ruel's side — Maren — and never gives it back. His best friend is Patch, a debt-walker boy on Corr's treadmill. In the flood (E10) he goes over the roofs with Nan; on the ruins (E12) he is the one holding baby Tibb.

After Nan's death he is raised by Pell, the Undertow and the war. In the five-year skip he joins the city watch — the very force that hunts the Hum — as Pell's eyes inside it.
