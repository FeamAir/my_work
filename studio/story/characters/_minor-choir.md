Better."*). (Death date: before Longdark 31; keeper to fix with S1E7's dates.)

**Sister Venn** (68) — the old Gleaner-mistress. *Function:* the **robed sleeve** that lifts the baby out of frame in the S1E1 pre-title (no face); found by Ione at Pale Crag in S3E12 — tells her she was gleaned as a baby from Ninth Sluice; key witness S3–S4; her **confession in S8E8** carries two further clues (`[SEALED — ask lead]`). *Secret:* took baby "Ama" from a rescue boat on the Night of Still Water (SEC-004); in CY 305 Liss found her — and was drowned (SEC-012). *Link:* she has gleaned children for fifty years and remembers every one by the note they hummed back. *Look:* tiny, bent, **deep grey Gleaner's hood never lowered**, the **silver Gleaner's bell** at her belt; spotted, very clean hands. *Voice:* thin, kindly, terrifying. *"They all hum, child. Every one of them hums when you ring. Yours hummed two notes I had never heard."*

**Cantor Sabin** (66) — the underpaid, **mute** old Cantor of the failing Sluice Spire. *Function:* Ione serves the Sluice Spire under him (S1E3, proposal); seen with a **new purse** in E9; on the third Still Day the Spire is not retuned; in the flood (E10) he forces a **breath-tone** into the Spire and **sings out** — dies on the stair (RULE-001 shown). *Secret (SEC-015):* took the purse to skip the Still-Day song (Ursel's money; S2E6 traces it to Fennick's steward). *Link:* thirty years singing a Spire the Fennicks would not pay to retune; the purse was the first money anyone had given him. *Look:* skeletal, **throat wrapped in a yellowed linen band**, Choir robe grey with age; the new purse has **copper Lantry thread** (a fair clue). *Voice:* mute; crude, tired Choir sign; one breath-tone. *(signed)* "They pay me to sing. Now they pay me not to. Both coins spend."

**Archivist Ferro** (55) — keeper of the Choir's open archive. *Function:* gives Pell the **Spiral Book fragment** (S5E10). *Secret (proposal, new SEC):* has secretly **copied forbidden Ashare texts** for thirty years, sewing the copies into the bindings of hymn-books. *Link:* proposal — first seen S2 with Elm (Elm's ally). *Look:* stooped, **bald crown with a fringe of grey hair**, **two pairs of spectacles** worn at once, ink to the elbows. *Voice:* whispery, eager, delighted by old words. *"Burned books are the loudest. Everyone remembers what was in them — wrongly."*

Main from S8 (replaces Elm inside the Choir). *Function:* catches Elm mistranslating (S7E7); after Elm's death the only person who can voice Vesna's signs (S8–S10); voices the First Cantor's last words (S10E9). Survives. *Secret:* **a gleaned child himself** (like Ione) — Lows-born, taken under seven; he does not remember from which sluice (SEC-005 instance; lead: does his origin need its own secret?). *Link — proposal for S1E5:* he is the junior novice beside Sedge when Pell and Ione find the "tested" child; **he** hands the child back on Ione's order and looks at Pell's lane as if he knows it — a scene that gains meaning in S8. *Look:* tall, rawboned, **white-blond hair cut in a straight fringe**, grey novice's robe; carries a **Gleaner's bell** (S1) → an enforcer's iron-shod staff (S7+). *Voice:* S1: polite, flat, too careful; S8+: a firm baritone that never lets Vesna's words be softened. *"I say what she signs. Exactly what she signs. Someone in this Choir has to."*

I only sort them."*
