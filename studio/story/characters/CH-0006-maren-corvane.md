## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** young woman of 19; tall, lean, sailor-strong; sun on her cheekbones.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **tall, lean, long-legged**; warm brown skin, darker than her father's (her mother's side — writer-houses); quick upright stride, hands behind her back like a captain on deck. Silhouette: tall, short coat, high curly tail.
- **Hair:** **dark umber-brown curls tied high at the crown with a silver cord** — a short, bouncing tail.
- **Eyes:** grey-blue, steady.
- **Distinctive mark:** none in S1; from S2E11 a thin powder-burn scar through the LEFT eyebrow (proposal — the Sluice Gates).
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **deep-blue SHORT riding coat** (cut at the hip — never her father's long coat), white shirt, grey breeches, sea boots. Main colour: deep blue (see the report: a Corvane-blue conflict with Ruel in S1E1–E9 only).
- **Signature items:** (1) **brass spyglass on her belt (right hip)**; (2) from **S6E12** her father's **black pearl ring, worn openly on the LEFT hand**. From S1E11 to S6E12 the ring is hidden (never on screen on her).
- **Changes over the saga:** S2 exile: salt-faded coat; S5 admiral's insignia; S6E12 the ring; S7+ Helm's grey-blue mantle over the coat (the director decides the Helm's regalia).

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** clear mezzo, crisp, commanding; Gullmouth accent like her father but harder, no warmth wasted.
- **Speech habits:** short sentences; orders, not requests; naval terms used exactly; she never fills a silence. Grows shorter each season.
- **Catch-phrase:** "Hold the line of the ships." (S1–S2) → later "Protect the name." (S1E11, `[ECHO: SEC-010]`)
- **Never says:** "I don't know"; "please" to her own captains; her father's crime aloud.
- **Sample line:** *"I wanted the sea. The sea doesn't vote."* · *"Every crew comes home or I don't."*
- **Delivery for AI (S1):** `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 4: E2 to Ruel ("I wanted the sea"), E9 at his deathbed, E11 reading the page, E12 refusing to swear.

## Goals
- **Wants (S1):** to protect her father's name. (S2–S5) to win the war her father's death started. (S6–S8) to hold the delta together.
- **Needs (inner):** to admit that the name she protects is a lie — which she never quite does.
- **Fears:** losing a crew; being her father; disorder ("the mob").
- **Heroism:** she **never abandons her crews** — and that loyalty is what turns into her danger.

## Biography
Only child of Ruel Corvane; a junior naval officer by choice, raised on decks more than in halls. She wanted the sea, not politics, and is pulled off her ship to be her father's aide during the election (S1E2) — where a Lows boy steals her cadet cap (Wick; pays S9E10). She takes a ledger copy from Hollis to her father (E7) and watches him burn one page. After his murder she stays by him on the *Patient Gull* (E9). In E11 Hollis gives her the page's twin: her father poisoned the Helm (SEC-001). At the wake she slips the black pearl ring from his finger and hides it with the page — her first compromise. Her aunt Ysa confirms her right to cast the Corvane vote "on one condition", spoken off screen (SEC-023; the condition is obedience — pays S2). In the Ring she votes for Corvane's cause, loses on the Sunken Vote, refuses to swear to Odo, and sails with the fleet.

### L2 — "The Clerk and the Admiral" (with Hollis Kett, CH-0003) — the great tragic love of the saga
- **The shape:** she trusts his columns before she trusts him. He is the only person who never tells her what she wants to hear. Each time she must choose between him and "the name", she chooses the name — and each time she loves him more for having said no. It is SEC-010's pattern written as a love story.
- **Why it is hidden:** heir (later head) of Corvane, and a charity clerk of the bank — then a fugitive, then her own spy in the enemy's treasury. In each of his positions a known tie to her would kill him.

### L1 reaches her — the Captain-Lord
S2E9 she learns of the wrecker silver and thinks she has understood the friendship; **S2E10** Ysa lays down two things — the boatswain's discharge and the packet: *"There were two things to bury."* Maren learns SEC-046. From then on Jaro is the nearest thing to family she has left (he shelters her in S2E12). In S3–S5 she reads his "both sides" as a friend cooling; she does not know Odo holds the letters.

### L3 — "The Paper Marriage" (with Lord Zev Fennick) — S7, a marriage of state against feeling
- **Terms (public):** S7 (CY 322), she is 29, he 24. Betrothal S7E2, marriage **S7E3** in the Ring, without a song. The Relay is sworn to the Helm for her wars and stays in Fennick's keeping; the consort keeps the Listening House and the Hearings; **they do not share a roof**. Corvane and Fennick have been enemies since CY 251 — the marriage closes the Helm's War on paper. She has bought every vote with something; Fennick she buys with her hand. The secret terms (proposed SEC-048) are not in my brief.
- **What it is:** never a love and never becomes one. Both are honest about what it is, and that honesty is the nearest thing to warmth in it — two people who each lost a father to the chair.
- **Beats:** S7E3 Jaro stands witness and does not object · S7E4–E5 the consort, as Master of the Hearings, rules against her convenience (the Kilbride suit) · *beyond this dossier's arc limit, for reference only:* S9E12 crowned with a consort and **no heir**; S10–S11 the cadet line (Corvane of the Roads) becomes the lever against her; the marriage ends with her death (S12E11); Zev survives.

### How the lines change her speech and behaviour
- **With Hollis she lets the silence stand** — the one person for whom "she never fills a silence" becomes a kind of trust: she waits for his figure. Her orders do not soften; she **orders and loves in the same sentence** (S4E12). Through S6 she never says the words of love on screen; the second column of the S2 letters says them for her (proposal for the S2 outline: the column is written in his hand, answered in hers as "received" — her only reply).
- **The page and the ring** are the props of the line: hidden together in S1E11; the page she asks him to cut in S6E7; the ring goes on at S6E12.
- **With Zev:** plain, contractual, courteous; no endearments, no lies. Their scenes are played across a table or a Ring rail, never across a doorway.
- **Staging:** letters, hands, the ring; two-shots from behind. Her S1 `[LIPSYNC-CU]` budget is unchanged (the E7/E11 seed exchanges are two-shots).
