# Second circle — the Ossari (people of the kite-clans)

Version 1.0 · 2026-11-22 · author: writer-characters · Canon: v0.1 (+ world package r2) · Spoiler level: **internal**
Coordination: `writer-houses` writes Ossari power (clans, council, war-host, saltpeter trade); this file writes the people. Ossari names follow the ts/k/d palette.
**New-character proposals** in this file: CH-0071 Dakka Den, CH-0072 Itsek.
Production note: Ossari in the field wear wind-hoods and goggles of smoked horn — easy to show masked or at distance; kites and gliders carry most of their screen presence.
Ottar (CH-0062), Kes's Ossari-born corporal, is filed in `_minor-mids.md` (he serves Rusk).

Silhouettes (build, skin, gait, marks of work and war) for every entry are summarised in `_anchors_matrix.md` §2a; investor rule — traits come from the world and biography, never from morality.

**Kadu** (30) — Ossari field captain. Main from S10 (the Ossari alliance). Survives. *Function:* first seen in S3 (the lead places the episode) — a field captain with a **key tattoo**; his family has kept the **Vanth "river book"** since CY 141 — one of the three records that prove, side by side with Dorran's gauges and the Choir's song logs, that the detuned song pulls water from the uplands (SEC-008, S7E10); stands beside Pell in hiding (S10). *Secret:* **descends from House Vanth**, destroyed in CY 141 for singing the Falls Spire less (SEC-008 note). *Link:* the oldest grudge in the saga, carried by its calmest man. Proposal: Ottar is his cousin, given as a hostage-child twelve years before Esk. *Look:* compact, scarred, **long black hair in a single rope braid threaded with copper wire**, the **key tattoo on the RIGHT forearm** (design: art team; meaning: lead's S3 outline), sand-orange and dun wind-coat, horn goggles on the brow. *Voice:* slow, low, very few words; Ossari accent with old, formal Concord words nobody uses any more (the book taught him the language). *"My family kept a book for a hundred and eighty years so that one day someone would believe it. Read."*

**Dakka Den** (38; NEW — proposal) — Tsura's younger sister; **Esk's official mother** ("the Dry Queen's nephew"). *Function:* the person who carries the lie of SEC-019 in the clans; proposal: first seen in S2E1 at Tsura's side on the rim, reading Esk's kite *aloud* — the line that tells the audience how much both women care about one hostage boy. *Secret:* SEC-019 — she agreed to call her sister's son her own so that the truce would take a lesser hostage; proposal: in S7E6 she is the one who says it aloud, in council, to stop Esk being traded again. *Link:* her own children died in the dust years (proposal), which is why the lie was easy to believe. *Look:* **many thin plaits tied off with knots of kite-string**, a sand-orange coat patched with dun, a copper bead at the throat. *Voice:* warm, fast, laughing Ossari speech; poor Concord. *"Everyone up here has a sister's child at the Stair, or a child at a sister's. We are a people who lend each other."*

**Itsek** (47; NEW — proposal) — Ossari envoy; leads the embassy to Highwater in S2 ("why the Ossari sent envoys"); in S3 he is the envoy who throws the motif line at a Concord lord: *"Who pays for the dry?"* *Secret (proposal, linked to Brenna's secret):* his clan has bought Rusk saltpeter back from Brenna's quartermasters for ten years; he knows the Stair's powder stores better than the Stair does. *Link:* the S3 Battle of the Stair — Ossari guns firing Rusk powder — runs through his ledgers. *Look:* elderly-lean; **LEFT leg in a leather-and-spar brace** from a glider crash in his youth, walks with a cane; **shaved sides with a grey crest**, a **long envoy's scarf of knotted signal-cord** (every knot a treaty), smoked-horn goggles pushed up. *Voice:* courteous, dry, perfect Concord with an Ossari "k". *"Your Spires answer you. Our wells do not answer us. Explain the difference, and I will go home."*
