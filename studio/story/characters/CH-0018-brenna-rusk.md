## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** woman of 57; tall, rawboned, a soldier's back; big hands.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **very tall, rawboned, heavy-handed**; ruddy upland skin, broken veins on the cheeks; heavy armoured stride, takes stairs two at a time even at fifty-seven. Silhouette: the tallest woman in the cast, black and square.
- **Hair:** **steel-grey, cut straight at the jaw**, with a single **black-iron ring** clasping a lock at the RIGHT temple.
- **Eyes:** pale grey-green.
- **Distinctive mark:** a notch missing from the top of the LEFT ear.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **black lacquered half-cuirass** over a black wool coat, **rust-red lining and sash** (Rusk colours inverted from Kes's tabard). Main colour: black.
- **Signature item:** the **Key of the Stair** — a long iron key on a chain at her belt; she never lets anyone else carry it.
- **Changes:** S3 — in full plate at the gate; the key is on her body when the Stair falls (what happens to it: lead's choice).

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** loud, flat, rough contralto; upland accent like Kes's but with a lady's grammar.
- **Speech habits:** says exactly what she thinks; military metaphors; impatient with the Steps' politeness; laughs once, loudly.
- **Catch-phrase:** "Shut, and stand." (house words)
- **Never says:** "neutral" without spitting; the word "saltpeter" in Highwater.
- **Sample line:** *"The Steps vote. The Stair bleeds. Write that down, Admiral, and then ask for my voice."*
- **Delivery:** `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 2 in S1 (E4 with Ruel, E12 vote) and ≤5 in S2–S3.

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** to keep the Stair standing and neutral; to keep her soldiers paid in a poor house.
- **Needs (inner):** to admit that her neutrality has a price list.
- **Fears:** the Stair falling on her watch; poverty.
- **Heroism:** she **holds the gate** until the Stair falls around her.

## Biography
Lady of the Stair and head of House Rusk; widow (family: writer-houses). She runs a fortress-town that is rich in honour and poor in coin, and pays her pikes by selling Ossari saltpeter — on the side and against Concord law — back up the escarpment, through the clan of the envoy Itsek (proposal). Her quartermasters learn from her and sell it twice (houses_overview: Rusk scheme). She holds Esk as a hostage under a border truce and raises Ottar, an older Ossari hostage-child, as a soldier.

In S1 she is Ruel's guest at the vote-courting (E4) and casts Rusk's vote for Corvane (E12). In S2 she tries to keep the Stair out of the Helm's War. In S3 the Ossari guns at the Battle of the Stair fire Rusk's own powder; she dies holding the gate (E11), and the Stair falls.
