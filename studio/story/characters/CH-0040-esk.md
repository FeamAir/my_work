## Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** boy of 13 (S1–S4), thin, light, made for climbing; after the skip a young man of 21 — wiry, long-armed.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** S1–S4: **small for thirteen, thin, light**; brown upland skin; climbs everything; S5+: **wiry, long-armed**, still light-footed. Silhouette: a boy on a roof-edge with a kite-line.
- **Hair:** **tight black curls dusted with orange clay** (Ossari custom; the clay fades in captivity S1–S2 and is back from S3).
- **Eyes:** amber-brown — his mother's (nobody comments; a fair clue for SEC-019).
- **Distinctive mark:** kite-cord scars across both palms.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** S1–S2: a **dun-brown hostage's jacket** (Rusk issue, too big) with an **orange Ossari sash**; S3+: Ossari wind-coat in sand-orange and dun. Main colour S1–S2: dun-brown.
- **Signature item:** a **knotted kite-cord wound round the LEFT wrist** — from S3E8 it is the cord of "stone-song days" (one knot per day the falls swelled without rain). And his **kite** (red-and-sand diamond kite with a tail of knotted rags).
- **Changes:** S5+ adult refs; the cord is now long enough to wrap the forearm.

## Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** S1–S4: light, quick boy's voice with a strong Ossari accent (hard "k", clipped endings); S5+: a quiet young tenor, slow in the Concord tongue.
- **Speech habits:** precise, literal; counts things; translates Ossari proverbs badly on purpose to make Kes laugh.
- **Catch-phrase:** (S1E12 kite-word, subtitled) "Home." · (S3 on) "Your stones drink our rivers."
- **Never says:** "mother" about Tsura in public.
- **Sample line:** *"The falls got fat again, Sergeant. No rain. I tied a knot."*
- **Delivery:** child — back to camera with the kite is ideal; `[LIPSYNC-CU]` ≤2 in S1 (E4 with Kes), ≤5 per season S3+.

## Goals
- **Wants (outer):** to go home; later, to make the Concord believe what his cord shows.
- **Needs (inner):** to be a son, not a hostage — and to forgive the mother who gave him.
- **Fears:** being the price of a peace again.
- **Heroism:** he **keeps the record nobody else will keep**, and later carries the pitch when every other line is cut (S11).

## Biography
Given to the Stair as a hostage under a border truce, as "the Dry Queen's nephew" (SEC-019: he is her son). He has lived with House Rusk since he was ten (given in CY 309 after the Rim Skirmish — r3, DEC-20) and in S1 is in Highwater with the Rusk delegation. He flies a kite from the barracks roof (S1E4); Kes teases him; he teaches her to read kites. In the S1E12 tag his kite answers the signal-fires on the escarpment: "home".

His knotted cord of "stone-song days" (S3E8) is the first data anyone gathers on the thirst (SEC-008). In S9 he is suspected of a betrayal he tried to prevent; in S11, with the Relay in the Sovereign's hands, his kite-lines carry the pitch from boat to boat.
