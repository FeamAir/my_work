**12 seasons · 4 acts · 145 episodes** (12 per season, 13 in S12). Format and running-time rules: `series_bible.md` §11. Continuity rules: §12.

**Mystery balance** (`research/recommendations.md` §4): S1–S6 each closes ≥1 old mystery and opens ≥2; S7–S12 each closes ≥2 and opens ≤1. IDs refer to `sealed/master_mystery.md`.

**Protagonist line:** Act I is led by **Ione** (with Pell); Ione dies at the end of S4 → **Pell** leads Acts II–IV; **Maren** grows from ally to the opposite pole and leads the "other half" of Acts III–IV. One planned time skip (S4→S5, five years).

## ACT I — THE SILENT SPIRE

### S1 — *Dry Tithe* (CY 312, Longdark 6 → Still 5; ~35 days)
**Conflict:** the old Helm dies; a 30-day election to the Still Days between reformist Lord Admiral Ruel Corvane and Odo Fennick, master of the Relay — while the bank quietly plans to let the slum district of Ninth Sluice drown for profit. **Big event:** the Still Days flood of Ninth Sluice (E10) — Ione's Great Song saves thousands and takes her voice. **Big loss:** Ruel Corvane, stabbed on the Steps (E8), dies E9. **Closes:** who poisoned the Helm (SEC-001 — Ruel himself, E11). **Opens:** why Pell's echo-shard answers her lullaby (SEC-002); who ordered Ruel's murder (SEC-013); who sabotaged the Still Day retuning (SEC-015). **Ends:** Fennick wins on the Sunken Vote; Maren refuses to kneel and sails; Ossari fires on the escarpment. Full outline: `season01_outline.md`.

### S2 — *The Purchased Seat* (CY 313, Firstwater → Saltdry)
**Conflict:** "the Helm's War" — Maren's Corvane fleet and Jaro Quell's islanders blockade Highwater against Helm Odo, Dask grain and Lantry money; Lady Brenna Rusk tries to keep the Stair neutral. In the drowned Lows, Pell, Wick and Nan hide Hollis and his ledger copy in the Undertow; mute Ione learns sign, learns politics, and secretly protects them. Odo hunts "the girl whose shard rang". **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Sluice Gates** (E11) — fleet against city locks, ended by Lady Ysa Corvane opening Gullmouth to Fennick and Dask barges blocking the canal. **Big loss:** **Nan Oshra** killed by Fennick's Listeners (E8) — her last words: *"It's not in the blood. It's in the mouth."* **Closes:** who sabotaged the retuning (SEC-015, E6); who ordered Ruel's murder — Ursel Lantry with Garrick Dask (SEC-013, E11). **Opens:** what Nan meant; why an infant grave at Pale Crag bears Cressa Ilvane's name a year before Ione's birthday; why the Ossari sent envoys. **Ends:** Maren defeated and in exile in the Shoals; Brenna Rusk receives the Dry Queen's ultimatum; kites over the Stair.

### S3 — *The Stair* (CY 313 Ember → CY 314 Rains)
**Conflict:** Ossari invasion. Tsura Den, the Dry Queen, demands that the Falls Spire fall silent for one season so the Aun can flow back to her starving plateau; the Concord sees only an invader. A forced truce: Odo pardons Maren to get her fleet. Kes and the hostage boy Esk at the Stair; Tsura's own POV in the upland camps; Pell goes with the Quiet into the Sunk to learn what her lullaby is; Ione traces the Gleaners. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Stair** (E10–11) — pike squares, cannon in the rain, signal-kites and gliders over the falls, the first truly massive battle. **Big loss:** **Lady Brenna Rusk**, holding the gate (E11); the Stair falls. **Closes:** the lullaby is the missing tone of the Spires — Pell sings it from the Sunken Spire's crown at low tide and the dead Spire *answers* (SEC-002, E10); Kes's guilt (SEC-009, E9). **Opens:** Ione learns from old Sister Venn that she was "gleaned" as a baby from Ninth Sluice; Ossari prisoners say "your stones drink our rivers". **Ends:** the Ossari hold the escarpment rim; Ione looks at her dark roots in a basin of water.

## ACT II — THE LOW NOTE

### S4 — *Still Days* (CY 314, Silting → Still)
**Conflict:** occupation and an armed truce on the escarpment; Odo rules Highwater by "Listener Law". Ione investigates her own birth with her grieving official mother Cressa (new POV) and Mother Silt (new POV). The Choir, now knowing Pell has the ear, takes her to Pale Crag; Ione breaks her out (E8). **Big event:** the Dry Queen breaches the upland dam to force terms; the flood wave runs down the Aun to Highwater during the Still Days, when the Spires are being retuned. **Big loss:** **Ione** — mute, she sings the Crown Spire with Pell (high part + low note) and "sings out" (E11). Their duet holds the wave: the first proof that two voices are stronger than one. **Closes:** Ione is Ama, Pell's elder sister, taken on the Night of Still Water (SEC-004, E10). **Opens:** why the Choir steals children with the ear; what the duet means (why two voices worked). **Ends:** Pell carries her sister's tuning fork down the Spire; Chronicler: *"Five years passed in which nobody sang."*

### S5 — *A Thousand Voices* (CY 320, Highsun → Still)
**Conflict:** Pell, 23, leads "the Hum" — illegal singing circles in the Lows; Wick, 19, a soldier in the city watch; Dorran Strand's pumps promise a world without songs; Odo, ageing and paranoid; Maren, pardoned admiral, building power; Hollis, Odo's treasury secretary, a double agent; Brother Elm leaks Choir secrets. **Big event:** **the Night of a Thousand Voices** (E11) — thousands sing the Sluice Spire during a storm; it answers as no Spire has in 300 years; the Listeners fire on the singers; Hollis kills Odo. **Big loss:** **Odo Fennick** (villain) and hundreds of singers. **Closes:** the ear is not hereditary — Cressa publishes Ione's journal and the Gleaner records (SEC-005, E6); what really happened at the Breaking (SEC-003, E10 — Spiral Book fragment + flashback: the Nine massacred the people's chorus; Pell's ancestress Oshra carried the last verse). **Opens:** why it rained on the uplands the night of the Thousand Voices; who writes the anonymous *Chronicle* pages that know too much (Chronicler thread).

### S6 — *Ledger of the Drowned* (CY 321)
**Conflict:** a new Helm election and the bank's last gamble; the dry-note crash; a "Pearl Pretender" (Lio, Pell's lost friend) claims Ashare blood and splits the Lows. **Big event / battle:** the bank run and the **Counting House fire** (E9); the **Battle of the Shoals** (E11) — Quell against a mercenary fleet that changes sides mid-battle. **Big loss:** **Ursel Lantry** (E10); Hollis presumed dead in the fire (E9; status sealed); Cressa murdered by the Choir (E12). **Closes:** Account Nine and the scale of the dry-note fraud (SEC-007, E7); Hollis's father forged the Dry Charter and was killed by Ursel (SEC-006, E8). **Opens:** is the Pretender real?; is Hollis alive? **Ends:** Maren elected Helm with the votes Pell bought for her by a promise — repeal of the Hush Law.

## ACT III — THIRST

### S7 — *Thirst* (CY 322)
**Conflict:** famine on the plateau; Tsura returns with the whole upland in arms; Helm Maren repeals the Hush Law only in Highwater; Garrick Dask (new POV) plays grain against both sides; Nell Tern (new POV) at Maren's side. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Hundred Locks** (E11) — the largest land battle of the saga, fought across canal locks. **Big loss:** **Kes Mabry** (E11). **Closes:** the detuned song pulls water from the uplands — the Spires are why the Ossari starve (SEC-008, E10, proven by Dorran's river gauges against the Choir's song logs); Esk is Tsura's son, not her nephew (SEC-019, E6). **Opens (1):** can the song be sung without draining anyone? **Ends:** Maren learns SEC-008 — and orders the Spires sung harder.

### S8 — *The Spiral* (CY 323)
**Conflict:** the Ossari besiege Highwater; inside, Pell, Daro Vey (new POV, Choir enforcer, himself a gleaned child) and Brother Elm break into the Choir's sealed archive under the Choir Spire. **Big event / battle:** the **Siege of Highwater** (E10–11), ended by Maren's terrible stroke: she sings two Spires against the Ossari camps and drowns thousands. **Big loss:** **Brother Elm**, executed by the Choir (E9); Lio the Pretender (E6). **Closes:** the Pretender's claim is false (SEC-016, E6); Vesna ordered the death of Pell's mother, and Elm was a Quiet child planted in the Choir (SEC-012, E8); who really built the Spires, and what the true song is (SEC-014, E10 — answer sealed). **Opens (1):** Lirenne could be raised — and whoever holds it holds the Sunken Vote.

### S9 — *The Ninth Vote* (CY 324 → CY 325 Still)
**Conflict:** Pell's movement tries to raise the Sunken Spire to claim the ninth vote and check the Helm; Maren, now feared and adored, sees a rival. Tibb (13, new POV — the baby Pell saved in S1E10) is a chorus runner. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Ninth Bridge** (E10) — the chorus columns ambushed on their way to the coast. **Big loss:** **Wick** (E10); Mother Silt earlier (E5). **Closes:** who betrayed the chorus's route (SEC-010, E11 — sealed); Tibb is the daughter of Nell Tern's drowned sister — Nell is Lows-born (SEC-017, E7). **Opens (1):** who is "Quire", the masked forger whose counter-chronicle pamphlets appear in E12? **Ends:** Maren dissolves the Conclave and is crowned **Sovereign** on the Still Days — the Chronicler announces a new count of years (**SY**).

## ACT IV — THE CHORUS

### S10 — *Sovereign* (SY 1)
**Conflict:** the Sovereign Years: Maren's order, efficient and merciless; Pell in hiding with the Ossari alliance (Kadu, new POV); Quire's pamphlets; Maren breaks the Choir. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Polders** (E11) — Maren floods the Dask polders to starve the alliance. **Big loss:** **Vesna Ilvane** (E9), **Garrick Dask** (E11). **Closes:** Vesna's true motive (SEC-020, E9); Pell completes the song from the Spiral Book (SEC-021, E10) — answers sealed. **Opens (1):** can Lirenne be raised before the Long Ebb?

### S11 — *Lirenne* (SY 2)
**Conflict:** the Long Ebb — a once-in-a-century storm season; Spires fail one after another; the alliance sails for the Sunk. Corr the Reedmaster (new POV) brings the treadmill walkers into the chorus. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of Lirenne** (E11–12) — the greatest naval battle of the saga while thousands sing on boats; the Sunken Spire rises from the sea. **Big loss:** **Jaro Quell** (E8), **Tsura Den** (E10). **Closes:** Quire's identity (SEC-011a, E6 — sealed); Lirenne can be raised — and is (E12). **Opens (1):** what will Maren do with the Crown Spire?

### S12 — *The Low Note* (SY 2–3, 13 episodes)
**Conflict:** Maren holds Highwater and threatens to silence every Spire and drown the delta rather than surrender it to "the mob". **Big event:** the **Great Chorus** — ten thousand voices across the delta, every Spire sung by crowds linked by the Relay; the cost of the song shared by many, so nobody is taken. **Big loss:** **Maren Corvane** (E11). Pell's voice (E12). **Closes:** the last questions — Maren's choice; the Chronicler's identity and the frame of the whole narration (SEC-011b, E13 — sealed). **Epilogue (E13, ~35 min):** decades later; the Chronicler's sign-off changes for the first time. A new count of years begins.
