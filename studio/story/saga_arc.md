# Saga Arc — THE LOW NOTE

Version 1.1 · 2026-11-22 · author: lead-screenwriter · status: **draft**

> **Revision r2 (2026-11-22, stress-test pass):** season paragraphs rewritten where logic, motive or pace failed (S2–S4, S6–S12); the S4 "dam" is now the Ossari **weir**, and who breached it is a secret (SEC-024); the middle of the saga (S6–S9) gets its own engine — a hidden crime revealed in S7, a failed First Raising in S9, a second-layer mystery that runs S8→S10; six new secrets SEC-023…029 are scheduled (answers of SEC-024…027 stay in the sealed file); every season now lists its **hand-off image**; new sections at the end: **Intrigue ladder**, **Motif matrix**, **Thread ledger** (thread → planted → returns → revealed). No death, battle or reveal episode from v1.0 has moved.
Spoiler level: **internal / partly sealed.** This file names *what* is revealed and when; the full answers are in `sealed/master_mystery.md`. Not for public material.

**12 seasons · 4 acts · 145 episodes** (12 per season, 13 in S12). Format and running-time rules: `series_bible.md` §11. Continuity rules: §12.

**Mystery balance** (`research/recommendations.md` §4): S1–S6 each closes ≥1 old mystery and opens ≥2; S7–S9 each closes ≥2 and opens ≤2 (r2: the middle needs live questions of its own, each answered within two seasons); S10–S12 each closes ≥2 and opens ≤1. IDs refer to `sealed/master_mystery.md`.

**Protagonist line:** Act I is led by **Ione** (with Pell); Ione dies at the end of S4 → **Pell** leads Acts II–IV; **Maren** grows from ally to the opposite pole and leads the "other half" of Acts III–IV. One planned time skip (S4→S5, five years).

## ACT I — THE SILENT SPIRE

### S1 — *Dry Tithe* (CY 312, Longdark 6 → Still 5; ~35 days)
**Conflict:** the old Helm dies; a 30-day election to the Still Days between reformist Lord Admiral Ruel Corvane and Odo Fennick, master of the Relay — while the bank quietly plans to let the slum district of Ninth Sluice drown for profit. **Big event:** the Still Days flood of Ninth Sluice (E10) — Ione's Great Song saves thousands and takes her voice. **Big loss:** Ruel Corvane, stabbed on the Steps (E8), dies E9. **Closes:** who poisoned the Helm (SEC-001 — Ruel himself, E11). **Opens:** why Pell's echo-shard answers her lullaby (SEC-002); who ordered Ruel's murder (SEC-013); who sabotaged the Still Day retuning (SEC-015); who silenced the apothecary (SEC-023). **Lower layers move the top (r2):** a relay-keeper sells one leak twice and causes two murders; the Pumpwrights' yearly "breakage" doubles the dead (SEC-028); gate passes are sold at the evacuation gate. **Ends:** Fennick wins on the Sunken Vote; Maren refuses to kneel and sails; six days upriver, Ossari fires on the escarpment. **Hand-off image:** Esk's kite at the Stair → S2E1: Tsura on the rim, reading it. Full outline: `season01_outline.md`.

### S2 — *The Purchased Seat* (CY 313, Firstwater → Saltdry)
**Conflict:** "the Helm's War" — Maren's Corvane fleet and Jaro Quell's islanders blockade Highwater against Helm Odo, Dask grain and Lantry money; Lady Brenna Rusk tries to keep the Stair neutral. In the drowned Lows, Pell, Wick and Nan hide Hollis and his ledger copy in the Undertow (Widow Pruitt teaches Hollis how a debtor is "drowned on paper"); mute Ione learns sign, learns politics, and secretly protects them. Odo hunts "the girl whose shard rang". **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Sluice Gates** (E11) — fleet against city locks. Maren loses it three ways at once: Lady Ysa Corvane opens Gullmouth to Fennick, Dask barges block the canal, and her own guns fall silent early — her captains' padded powder accounts (the house's small fraud costs it the war; the root of the future Sovereign's hatred of "small thieves"). **Big loss:** **Nan Oshra** killed by Fennick's Listeners (E8) — her last words: *"It's not in the blood. It's in the mouth."* **Closes:** who sabotaged the retuning (SEC-015, E6 — Hollis matches the purse to Fennick's steward; the dead Helm's sealed will surfaces with the unsigned Tithe Map); who silenced Sister Marl (SEC-023, E10 — Ysa: *"I have been burying your father for a year"*); who ordered Ruel's murder — Ursel Lantry with Garrick Dask (SEC-013, E11). **Opens:** what Nan meant; why an infant grave at Pale Crag bears a date five months before Ione's birthday; why the Ossari sent envoys. **Ends:** Maren defeated and in exile in the Shoals; Brenna Rusk receives the Dry Queen's ultimatum; kites over the Stair. **Hand-off image:** the ultimatum in Brenna's hand → S3E1: Tsura dictating it.

### S3 — *The Stair* (CY 313 Ember → CY 314 Rains)
**Conflict:** Ossari invasion. Tsura Den, the Dry Queen, demands that the Falls Spire fall silent for one season so the Shelf lakes and the upland wells can refill; the Concord sees only an invader. A forced truce: Odo pardons Maren to get her fleet. Kes and the hostage boy Esk at the Stair — Esk keeps a knotted cord of "stone-song days", the days the falls swell without rain; Tsura's own POV in the upland camps; Pell goes with the Quiet into the Sunk to learn what her lullaby is; Ione traces the Gleaners until Vesna confines her at Pale Crag "for her nerves" (no rinse — her roots grow out). **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Stair** (E10–11) — pike squares, cannon in the rain, signal-kites and gliders over the falls, the first truly massive battle. The Ossari guns fire Rusk's own powder: Brenna's quartermasters have been selling the saltpeter twice (r2 — a barracks racket decides a battle). **Big loss:** **Lady Brenna Rusk**, holding the gate (E11); the Stair falls. **Closes:** the lullaby is the missing tone of the Spires — Pell sings it from the Sunken Spire's crown at low tide and the dead Spire *stirs* (SEC-002, E10) — and the Quiet elders, instead of rejoicing, cover her mouth; Kes's guilt (SEC-009, E9). **Opens:** Ione learns from old Sister Venn that she was "gleaned" as a baby from Ninth Sluice; Ossari prisoners say "your stones drink our rivers"; what are the Quiet afraid of? **Ends:** the Ossari hold the escarpment rim and the Falls Spire. **Hand-off image:** Ione's dark roots in a basin of water → S4E1: the same basin; Cressa pours the rinse away.

## ACT II — THE LOW NOTE

### S4 — *Still Days* (CY 314, Silting → Still)
**Conflict:** occupation and an armed truce on the escarpment. The Ossari silence the captured Falls Spire and build a **weir** at the lip of the falls; upland wells rise for the first time in a generation, the Aun drops, the Dask polders crack — the delta says "the Dry Queen stole the river". Odo rules Highwater by "Listener Law". Ione investigates her own birth with her grieving official mother Cressa (new POV) and Mother Silt (new POV), whose purge of her own child-selling under-boss exposes the Gleaners' "ear-money" (SEC-029, E6). The Choir, now knowing Pell has the ear, takes her to Pale Crag — where the First Cantor asks to see her alone (E7); Ione breaks her out (E8). Tsura threatens in council to breach the weir (E9). **Big event:** on the third Still Day, while Highwater's Spires are silent for retuning, the weir goes; the flood wave runs down the Aun. **Big loss:** **Ione** — mute, she sings the **Crown Spire** on breath alone with Pell (high part + low note) and "sings out" (E11). Their duet holds the wave: the first proof that two voices are stronger than one. **Closes:** Ione is Ama, Pell's elder sister, taken on the Night of Still Water (SEC-004, E10); ear-money (SEC-029). **Opens:** why the Choir steals children with the ear; what the duet means; and — unnoticed by anyone on screen — Concord-stamped barrel staves in the wreck of the weir (SEC-024). **Ends (E12):** the Dry Queen "does not deny" the wave and gets her terms — the **Weir Terms**: the Ossari keep the rim, the Falls Spire is sung only half the year; Pell carries her sister's tuning fork down the Spire; Chronicler: *"Five years passed in which nobody sang."* **Hand-off image:** the tuning fork in Pell's fist → S5E1: the fork struck on a tide-post by an adult hand.

### — five-year skip —

### S5 — *A Thousand Voices* (CY 320, Highsun → Still)
**Conflict:** Pell, 23, leads "the Hum" — illegal singing circles in the Lows; Wick, 19, a soldier in the city watch; Dorran Strand's pumps promise a world without songs; Odo, ageing and paranoid; Maren, pardoned admiral, building power; Hollis, Odo's treasury secretary, a double agent; Brother Elm leaks Choir secrets. The Choir is visibly short of Cantors — two Spires are sung in shifts by exhausted children. **Big event:** **the Night of a Thousand Voices** (E11) — it begins at the tide-post; thousands sing the Sluice Spire during a storm; it answers as no Spire has in 300 years; the Listeners fire on the singers; Hollis kills Odo. The district stands through the night only because the Pumpwrights' barges hold the water — Corr the Reedmaster's price for his silence about the sawn pump-pins of CY 312 (SEC-028, E8: the treadmill walkers blackmail the Guild, and that makes the great night possible). **Big loss:** **Odo Fennick** (villain) and hundreds of singers. **Closes:** the ear is not hereditary — Cressa publishes Ione's journal, the Gleaner rolls and Silt's ledger (SEC-005, E6), and with it the Choir's real weakness: only the forbidden lullaby makes a singer, so the Hush Law has been starving the Choir itself; the broken pumps (SEC-028, E8); what really happened at the Breaking (SEC-003, E10 — Spiral Book fragment + flashback: the Nine massacred the people's chorus; Pell's ancestress Oshra carried the last verse). **Opens:** why the falls ran thin and the upland wells rose on the night of the Thousand Voices; who writes the anonymous *Chronicle* pages that know too much. **Hand-off image:** the Helm's seal lying unclaimed on the floor of the Ring → S6E1: eight hands and one empty glove.

### S6 — *Ledger of the Drowned* (CY 321)
**Conflict:** a new Helm election and the bank's last gamble; the dry-note crash; a "Pearl Pretender" (Lio, Pell's lost friend) claims Ashare blood and splits the Lows — and behind him, new paper: "Lirenne salvage bonds" sold from barges by Quell brokers and Piers Lantry (SEC-016). **The lowest floor sells to the highest (r2):** the Choir cannot man its Spires; great houses with failing Spires need the Hum's singers; Corr's walkers can stop every pump in the city. Pell and Corr trade songs and treadmills for votes — the first time the Lows hold something the Steps must buy. **Big event / battle:** the bank run and the **Counting House fire** (E9; Zev Fennick is the last man seen going in); the **Battle of the Shoals** (E11) — Quell against a mercenary fleet that changes sides mid-battle when its notes become worthless. **Big loss:** **Ursel Lantry** (E10); Hollis presumed dead in the fire (E9; status sealed); Cressa murdered by the Choir (E12). **Closes:** Account Nine and the scale of the dry-note fraud (SEC-007, E7); Hollis's father forged the Dry Charter and was killed by Ursel (SEC-006, E8); Ruel's crime becomes public (SEC-001). **Opens:** is the Pretender real?; is Hollis alive? **Ends:** Maren elected Helm with the votes Pell bought for her by a promise — repeal of the Hush Law; she puts on her father's black pearl ring. **Hand-off image:** the ringed hand closing on the Helm's seal → S7E1: the same hand signing the repeal "within sight of the Crown Spire" — read out by a crier at the tide-post.

## ACT III — THIRST

*Act III engine (r2): the war is the surface. Underneath run three questions that belong to this act alone — who sent the wave that killed Ione (S7), what the tenth stave is (S8→S10), why the people's own song drowned them (S9→S10).*

### S7 — *Thirst* (CY 322)
**Conflict:** famine on the plateau — Helm Maren, paying Dask for its vote with river water, breaks the Weir Terms of CY 314 (the Falls Spire silent half the year) and has it sung all year round; Tsura returns with the whole upland in arms; Helm Maren repeals the Hush Law only in Highwater; Garrick Dask (new POV) plays grain against both sides; Nell Tern (new POV) at Maren's side; Zev Fennick opens the Relay's logs to Dorran. From E1 the Chronicler's voice is hoarser. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Hundred Locks** (E11) — the largest land battle of the saga, fought across canal locks. **Big loss:** **Kes Mabry** (E11). **Closes:** Esk is Tsura's son, not her nephew (SEC-019, E6); **who sent the wave of CY 314 (SEC-024, E8 — shown to the audience inside Garrick's POV; no hero learns it yet; answer sealed)**; the detuned song pulls water from the uplands — the Spires are why the Ossari starve (SEC-008, E10, proven by three records side by side: Dorran's river gauges, the Choir's song logs, and the Vanth "river book" that Kadu's family has kept since CY 141). **Opens:** can the song be sung without draining anyone?; (dramatic irony) when will Pell learn what the audience now knows? **Ends:** Maren learns SEC-008 — and orders the Spires sung harder. **Hand-off image:** the falls roaring at night under a clear sky → S8E1: the same falls from above, the Ossari host crossing by torchlight.

### S8 — *The Spiral* (CY 323)
**Conflict:** the Ossari besiege Highwater; inside, Pell, Daro Vey (new POV, Choir enforcer, himself a gleaned child) and Brother Elm break into the Choir's sealed archive under the Choir Spire. **Big event / battle:** the **Siege of Highwater** (E10–11), ended by Maren's terrible stroke: she sings two Spires against the Ossari camps and drowns thousands. **Big loss:** Lio the Pretender (E6) — dying, he asks the crowd to raise Lirenne anyway, and the fraud's slogan becomes the movement's aim; **Brother Elm**, executed by the Choir (E9). **Closes:** the Pretender's claim is false (SEC-016, E6); Vesna ordered the death of Pell's mother, and Elm was a Quiet child planted in the Choir (SEC-012, E8) — and Pell learns whose stone she has worn since she was fifteen, and where she found it (first step of SEC-027); who really built the Spires, and what the true song is (SEC-014, E10 — answer sealed). **Opens:** Lirenne could be raised — and whoever holds it holds the Sunken Vote; the archive's song has **ten staves, and the tenth is written backwards** — what is it? **Hand-off image:** a spiral shell set on Lirenne on Pell's chart of the Sunk → S9E1: the same chart on Maren's table; her hand removes the shell.

### S9 — *The Ninth Vote* (CY 324 → CY 325 Still)
**Conflict:** Pell's movement tries to raise the Sunken Spire to claim the ninth vote and check the Helm; the salvage-bond holders pay for the boats; Maren, now feared and adored, sees a rival. Tibb (13, new POV — the baby Pell saved in S1E10) is a chorus runner. **Midseason catastrophe (r2, E5): the First Raising.** At the lowest tide the chorus sings everything the Quiet have kept, "the last verse" included. The Sunken Spire does not rise; twenty miles away the Storm Spire falls dumb for an hour and the surge takes the staging camp on the Shoals. **Mother Silt** dies getting the children out and leaves Tibb her "book of sold men". Jaro Quell breaks with the movement; the Quiet split; Maren, who watched from her flagship, has seen with her own eyes that the people's song can drown people. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Ninth Bridge** (E10) — the chorus columns, marching to try again, are cut off by the spring tide and slaughtered by Dask levies. **Big loss:** **Wick** (E10) — among the dead Maren finds a boy wearing her own old cadet cap. **Closes:** Tibb is the daughter of Nell Tern's drowned sister — Nell is Lows-born (SEC-017, E7), and it is Nell, who handed over the tide-table and then learned who was marching, who testifies who betrayed the route (SEC-010, E11 — sealed); what Maren's ring is (second step of SEC-027, E12 — sealed). **Opens:** why did the holy song drown its singers?; who is "Quire", the masked forger whose pamphlets — *on the Sovereign's side* — appear in E12? **Ends:** Maren dissolves the Conclave and is crowned **Sovereign** on the Still Days — the Chronicler announces a new count of years (**SY**). **Hand-off image:** Quire's first sheet nailed to the tide-post → S10E1: Tibb's hand tears it down and carries it to Pell.

## ACT IV — THE CHORUS

### S10 — *Sovereign* (SY 1)
**Conflict:** the Sovereign Years: Maren's order, efficient and merciless to great thieves and small; Pell in hiding with the Ossari alliance (Kadu, new POV), doubting her own song; Quire's pamphlets argue the Sovereign's case better than she does; Maren breaks the Choir, seizes its archive and its school, and keeps the old First Cantor a prisoner. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of the Polders** (E11) — Maren floods the Dask polders to starve the alliance. **Big loss:** **Vesna Ilvane** (E9), **Garrick Dask** (E11 — drowned in his own fields). **Closes — the saga's double turn (answers sealed):** who the First Cantor really is and why she chose the lie (SEC-026 + SEC-020, E9); what the tenth stave is, why the First Raising killed, and what truly happened in the last hour of Lirenne (SEC-025 + SEC-021, E10) — after which Quire changes sides; Pell learns who sent the wave that killed her sister (SEC-024, E11, from Silt's book). **Opens (1):** can Lirenne be raised — the right way — before the Long Ebb? **Hand-off image:** a storm front on the sea horizon, seen from the drowned polders → S11E1: the same front from the crown of the Crown Spire.

### S11 — *Lirenne* (SY 2)
**Conflict:** the Long Ebb — a once-in-a-century storm season; Spires fail one after another for want of singers; the alliance sails for the Sunk. Corr the Reedmaster (new POV) brings the treadmill walkers into the chorus; with the Relay in the Sovereign's hands, Esk's kite-lines carry the pitch from boat to boat. **Big event / battle:** the **Battle of Lirenne** (E11–12) — the greatest naval battle of the saga while thousands sing on boats; the Sunken Spire rises from the sea. **Big loss:** **Jaro Quell** (E8 — he comes back to the side he left), **Tsura Den** (E10 — her last words to Pell are about the weir). **Closes:** Quire's identity (SEC-011a, E6 — sealed; the answer is not what the clues of S9–S10 seem to say); Lirenne can be raised — and is (SEC-018, E12). **Opens (1):** what will Maren do with the Highwater Spires — now that she holds the Choir's archive and its children? **Hand-off image:** every echo-stone in Highwater ringing low → S12E1: Maren in the empty Ring, listening; the ringed hand closes.

### S12 — *The Low Note* (SY 2–3, 13 episodes)
**Conflict:** Maren holds Highwater and threatens to silence its Spires and drown the delta rather than surrender it to "the mob" — a threat the audience now knows can be carried out, and has been once before. **Big event:** the **Great Chorus** — ten thousand voices across the delta, every Spire sung by crowds linked by kite and Relay (Zev opens it); the cost of the song shared by many, so nobody is taken; then the crowds at each Spire decide, by singing or by silence, what the song shall be from now on. **Big loss:** **Maren Corvane** (E11). Pell's voice (E12). **Closes:** Maren's choice; the last step of SEC-027 (E11); the Chronicler's identity and the frame of the whole narration (SEC-011b, E13 — sealed). **Epilogue (E13, ~35 min):** decades later; the tide-post of Ninth Sluice; the Chronicler's sign-off changes for the first time. A new count of years begins.
