> **v1.1 (same day) — the investor's hook rule, passed on by the director:** viewers decide in seconds. A wordless visual hook in the first 5–10 s; by 0:30 the viewer must want to know "what is this / why"; by 3:00 the hero's personal stake and the first mystery; no logo, title or slow exposition before the hook; the title comes after the cold open. Consequences against the outline's minute marks: (1) the pre-title's seven images are re-ordered — the cradle is the first frame; (2) the Chronicler's tapestry moves from 1:15 to 5:55, *after* the heroine, her stake and the stone — it now answers a question instead of delaying one; (3) the stone answers Pell **at the find**, at 3:00, not later at home; (4) the tithe-collector becomes the stake (six coppers by dusk) and returns at dusk. No fact, clue, death or fixed line of the outline is lost: table in `continuity_check.md` §2. Minute-by-minute retention check: §2 below.

**Episode:** pilot, ~29 min of story · **In-world time:** one day — CY 312, Longdark 6 (Hornday), dawn ebb → dark (date ruling: `continuity_check.md` §1, R-1) · pre-title: CY 295, the night of Still 3.
**Lines:** P (Pell) / I (Ione) / H (Hollis). **Clock stated:** thirty days to the Still Days. **Clock inside the episode:** six coppers by dusk.
**The pilot's promise to the viewer:** a flood in the first second; a stone that sings back by the third minute; a world law *seen*, not explained (a girl sings, the sea lies flat); a ruler who dies mid-sentence.
**Episode puzzle (fair, closed inside the episode):** the transposed figure at the next desk (Sc 14) — both numbers are on screen before Hollis reacts.

### Sc 1 — PRE-TITLE: the Night of Still Water `$`
- **Place:** PL-0008 Ninth Sluice lanes → PL-0021 evacuation gate (Lows side) → open flood water. **Time:** night, CY 295, Still 3. **No dialogue. No Chronicler. No score until the hum. The very first frame of the series is image 1 — nothing precedes it.**
- **Characters:** CH-0004 Kes at seventeen (bk — hands only) · CH-0023 Liss (bk — silhouette, left braid; humming voice) · the baby (voice; a wrapped shape) · CH-0056 Sister Venn (bk — a robed sleeve) · a crowd (silhouettes).
- **What happens (seven images, cut on sound):**
  1. **0:00–0:08 — THE HOOK.** A baby's cradle, afloat, turning slowly down the middle of a *street* — black water to the door-lintels, a lantern still burning on a post beside it.
  2. 0:08–0:18 — Lanterns along the plank-walks go out one by one, from the far end toward us. The water climbs a doorpost's ladder of tide-marks, past the highest.
  3. 0:18–0:35 — A crowd in the dark against a great gate. A pair of young soldier's hands — no face — drag the bar **shut**. Fists on iron from the wrong side.
  4. 0:35–0:50 — Silence under water. Then, above it, a woman's voice hums five notes ♪. Silhouette on a roof-ridge: one long braid on the left.
  5. 0:50–1:00 — A rescue boat with one lantern. A wrapped baby in it hums **two** of the notes back.
  6. 1:00–1:08 — A robed sleeve reaches in and lifts the baby out of frame.
  7. 1:08–1:15 — The boat's lantern goes on without it, small on the black water. Superimposed, no cut to black: *The Night of Still Water.*
- **Purpose:** the hook; the world's cost before the world's rules; the image the whole season walks toward (E10 repeats it shot for shot).
- **Seeds:** SEC-004 (two notes; the sleeve) · SEC-009 (M-HND) · M-LUL first statement — the first music of the series · M-BRD · the cradle (mirror for E10; SEC-017 is *not* planted here — no knot on this cradle).
- **Transition:** MATCH CUT — the lantern on black water → the sun on wet silt. The same street, the same doorpost. Daylight. Where the water was, there is mud. Stamp: *Seventeen years later.*
- **Length:** 1:15.

### Sc 2 — The same lane: six coppers by dusk
- **Place:** PL-0008 Ninth Sluice lane, outside PL-0019 Nan's stilt-house (the same doorpost as Sc 1, the tide-mark of that night far above the others) · one six-second rise to PL-0004 Highwater (the three tiers; the Spires). **Time:** dawn, Longdark 6, CY 312.
- **Characters:** CH-0001 Pell · CH-0007 Wick · CH-0015 Nan `[OS]` (inside) · the tithe-collector (light, unnamed — slate tabard with a sulphur stripe, a box chained to his wrist) · CH-0048 Corr and treadmill walkers above (bk).
- **What happens:**
  - A fist knocks on the doorpost — on the old tide-mark. The tithe-collector: Last Song, three coppers a month, two months owing. **Six coppers by dusk, or he takes it in goods.** He looks past the door at the nets.
  - Wick, eleven, hanging upside down from the rope-bridge: Nan has drowned. Twice. The second time she took the money with her. Nan `[OS]`, scolding from inside, spoils it.
  - Pell (fifteen; tar-brown oilskin, cropped rust hair, a thin left braid with a tin tag "IX") comes out with a rake and a rope and does not waste a word on him: dusk.
  - The camera rises with her glance, six seconds, once: the stilt-town in its own shadow below the waterline; the brick dike-line above it; granite terraces above that; and over all of it black towers, one of them humming audibly **flat**. Back down. The silt of the canals lies open where there has always been water. Overhead a great wheel turns — walkers' backs, a spoken one-pitch step-count, a reed whistle.
  - Pell, FIXED LINE (fingerprint) `[MS]`: *"You want it found, you pay the mud. Mud doesn't do favours."* — and she is down the ladder.
- **Purpose:** the heroine and her stake inside the first two minutes; the world in six seconds, as a glance and not a lecture.
- **Seeds:** M-PAY (the dry-tithe; "Last Song" — the Ninth's tier today) · RULE-003 shown (the flat hum — why the ebb is so low: no free coincidence) · the tide-mark of CY 295 · M-BRD · the "IX" tag · the Lows' sound (knock, planks, whistle, the count).
- **Transition:** Wick drops off the bridge into the mud after her.
- **Length:** 0:50 (ends 2:05).

### Sc 3 — The back-canal: the find and the answer ♪ `$`
- **Place:** PL-0020 the Ninth Sluice back-canal, by the old sluice. **Time:** dawn ebb.
- **Characters:** CH-0001 Pell · CH-0007 Wick · CH-0049 Mags (light — far off in her own lane; a red rag in her hair) · other mudlarks (bk).
- **What happens:**
  - Lanes are owned. Mags, across the silt, the loudest laugh on the canal: Pell's lane is all buttons. Wick answers for both of them.
  - The old sluice sill is bare — for the first time anyone has seen. Past it, a sump pool of standing black water. Pell ties the rope round her waist and puts the other end in Wick's fists. She will not step past the sill. She works the pool with the rake at arm's length, and her face is not the face from Sc 2.
  - The rake snags. She has to lean out over the water. **Frightened, without knowing she does it, she hums — mouth shut, three notes ♪.** Wick: *"Pell. Mouth."* — outdoors, in sight of a Spire, that is the tide-post.
  - **(3:00)** The rake comes up with a rotted cord, and on it a black stone no bigger than a thumbnail, cracked across. As it breaks the surface it **hums back** — low, under the floor of the ear — and the whole black pool shivers into rings.
  - Pell, hauling herself back onto the sill, hears nothing but her own heart. **Only Wick has seen.** He looks from the rings to her, and for once has no words.
  - An unlicensed echo-chip: a crime, and six coppers at Tuck's. Pell palms it before Mags can see what, or where. *"Buttons!"* she calls back. Wick: there was no tone for it to ring to. Pell: chips ring; that is what chips are for.
- **Purpose:** the inciting object and the first mystery, together, at the three-minute mark; Pell's competence and her one fear, shown and not named (pays E10); the Hush Law as a boy's whisper.
- **Seeds:** SEC-002 ♪ (first clue) · M-SHD · SEC-027 ("no bigger than a thumbnail") · SEC-012 (the *place* — plainly: the back-canal, by the old sluice; pays E2's question) · RULE-006 · DEC-45 guard: Mags sees neither the find nor the spot, and is too far to hear · Tuck named (E2) · the stone is worth exactly the debt.
- **Transition:** a two-finger whistle from the plank-walks — rising three: all clear, tide turning. They climb.
- **Length:** 2:10 (ends 4:15).

### Sc 4 — Nan's rule
- **Place:** PL-0019 Nan's stilt-house (interior). **Time:** morning.
- **Characters:** CH-0015 Nan · CH-0001 Pell · CH-0007 Wick.
- **What happens:**
  - Nan (tiny, hunched, indigo headscarf; one spiral-shell earring, left — unremarked) mends nets and scolds without looking up. Silt-bread. The net-chest sits on the floor like something that does not matter. Pell eats with one fist closed.
  - Wick is bursting. Pell's foot finds his shin under the table. Nan, of the canal: the old sill bare? Not in her floods. Not since— and she bites the thread instead of finishing.
  - Through the wall the Sluice Spire's morning song starts, thin and flat. Wick, mouth full, cheerfully joins it on one loud note.
  - **Nan's hand is over his mouth before the note is finished.** Nobody moves. The Spire sings on outside.
  - Nan `[LIPSYNC-CU]`, FIXED LINE: *"Never sing, chick. Hum if you must, and only for the house."*
  - She shoulders a bale of mended net — the chandler's on the dike pays today, back by dusk — and goes. Pell opens her fist. The stone has left its shape in her palm. She knots the cord round her own neck, under the oilskin. Wick: *"It answered you."* Pell: *"It's six coppers."*
- **Purpose:** the Hush Law as a grandmother's terror; the family; the stone kept from Nan (E2 needs her to see it for the first time); the stake and the mystery tied into one object — she means to sell the thing the series is about.
- **Seeds:** RULE-006 · M-MTH (SEC-003, pays E8) · SEC-012 (her fear reads as the law) · the net-chest (pays E3, E7, E11) · the earring (visible only; its line is E2 — SEC-026) · M-SHD at her throat.
- **Transition:** the stone against her skin, black, close → a black tower woven in thread. A dry page turns.
- **Length:** 1:40 (ends 5:55).
