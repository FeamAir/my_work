### Sc 1 — PRE-TITLE: "The Night of Still Water" `$`
- **Place:** PL-0008 Ninth Sluice lanes → PL-0021 evacuation gate (Lows side) → open flood water. **Time:** night, CY 295, Still 3. **No dialogue. No Chronicler. No score until the hum.**
- **Characters:** CH-0004 Kes at seventeen (bk — hands only) · CH-0023 Liss (bk — silhouette, left braid; humming voice) · the baby (voice; a wrapped shape) · CH-0056 Sister Venn (bk — a robed sleeve) · a crowd (silhouettes).
- **What happens (seven images, cut on sound):**
  1. Black water climbs a stilt-lane, rung by rung up a doorpost's tide-marks.
  2. Lanterns along the plank-walks go out one by one, from the far end toward us.
  3. A crowd in the dark against a great gate; a pair of young soldier's hands — no face — drag the bar across. The bar drops.
  4. A cradle turning slowly in the current.
  5. A woman's voice, somewhere above the water, hums five notes ♪. Silhouette on a roof-ridge: one long braid on the left.
  6. A rescue boat with one lantern. A wrapped baby in it hums **two** of the notes back.
  7. A robed sleeve reaches in and lifts the baby out of frame. The lantern goes on without it.
- **Card (white on black, silence):** *The Night of Still Water. Seventeen years ago.*
- **Purpose:** the hook; the world's cost before the world's rules; the image the whole season walks toward (E10 repeats it shot for shot).
- **Seeds:** SEC-004 (two notes; the sleeve) · SEC-009 (M-HND) · M-LUL first statement — the first music of the series · M-BRD · the cradle (mirror for E10; SEC-017 is *not* planted here — no knot on this cradle).

- **Length:** 1:15.

### Sc 3 — The lowest ebb `$`

### Sc 4 — The back-canal: the find

### Sc 7 — The stone answers ♪
- **Place:** PL-0019. **Time:** morning, minutes later.
- **Characters:** CH-0001 Pell · CH-0007 Wick.
- **What happens:**
  - Pell washes the stone in the water-bowl and picks at the rotted cord. Busy hands; without noticing she hums — mouth shut, three notes of something old ♪.
  - The stone hums back. Low, under the floor of the ear. The water in the bowl shivers into rings.
  - Pell hears nothing; she is already talking about what Tuck pays for a chip. **Only Wick sees.** He looks from the bowl to her and for once has no words.
  - Wick: there was no tone to ring to. Pell: chips ring, that is what chips are for. She knots the stone onto her own neck-cord, under the oilskin, to sell.
- **Purpose:** the series' central wonder, small and domestic, witnessed by a child nobody will believe.
- **Seeds:** SEC-002 ♪ (first clue) · M-SHD at her throat · Tuck named (E2).
- **Transition:** the stone's low hum does not quite end — it becomes the scratch of a pen on vellum.
- **Length:** 1:25.

### Sc 17 — The death-tone `$` `LA`
- **Place:** PL-0011 (the hall's licensed echo-stone in its brass cage on the wall) → the Counting House steps, over the city. **Time:** dusk.
- **Characters:** CH-0003 Hollis · CH-0011 Ursel · CH-0055 Fossett · the hall.
- **What happens:**
  - The evening bell has barely stopped when the caged stone on the wall begins to ring: the Relay's **death-tone** — three long tones on the Crown's pitch, and a silence as long as a tone.
  - **Forty pens stop in the air.** A drop of ink hangs and falls. Nobody breathes.
  - One pen goes on. Ursel, at the high desk, finishes her line, blots it, and begins the next.
