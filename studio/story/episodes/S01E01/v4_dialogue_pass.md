## 4. The Chronicler without a voice

### 4.1 In S1E01: one written page, in the ending

**What it is.** A page of a bound book (typeset, ruled, archival; **no handwriting**), designed as a card in the same graphic family as the map. It fades up over the main theme ≈1 s after the black that follows the low note. It holds ≈20 s and is not read aloud. SFX: one dry page turn. The text is the Chronicler's fixed wording, letter for letter, for every sentence that no character now speaks:

```text
THE CHRONICLE OF THE DRY YEARS

This is the three hundred and twelfth year of that Concord: three hundred and twelve years, each with its tithe, and each balance carried forward.
By law the Helm reads the map aloud, and signs it; and the balance of that year was read on the sixth of Longdark.
Helm Lucan Dask died on the sixth of Longdark, with the Tithe Map unsigned.
By law a Helm is chosen on the Still Days. There were thirty days, and a debt carried forward.

…and the water kept its counsel.
```

**Why a page and why there:**
- The ECHO wordings (ex-L033, L047, L105) and the sign-off (ex-L034) **still exist in E1, word for word**, so later episodes can quote them.
- The SEC-011b clues of S01E01 ("carried forward", "the balance of that year") stay **in the record's own words and nowhere else**. No character says "carried forward" in E1. This keeps the S01E04 (Hollis) and S10E06 (first other mouth) steps intact.
- At the very end, after we have *seen* the reading break off, the cup refused and the stone answer, the official record says only what it always says: true facts with every cause left out. The bible's theme "history is written by the dry" becomes visible on the first viewing without a single spoken word of exposition. It also leaves the viewer a clock: **thirty days**.
- It costs no story time; it sits inside the ending.
- Fans will screenshot it. The investor's "viewers keep their own tables" starts here.

**Spoken by a character instead (exact fixed wording kept, as a recitation):** ex-L029 and ex-L031 verbatim, ex-L032 verbatim, all by Nan (Sc 4). ex-L030 is said in shortened form; the full sentence is not needed in E1 (it is not `[ECHO]`). ex-L044–L046 (the tiers, eight Spires) are paraphrased in Corr's and Pell's words (Sc 6). ex-L104 is also on the page.

**Literal truth.** Nan quotes the Choir's record and adds nothing false of her own. Her own lines are questions, deflections and "That's what they found." (§6.4). Corr's and Pell's paraphrases are true as of CY 312 (`world.md` tiers of song; Ninth = Last Song since CY 295).
