## 1. Great houses (living)

### HS-001 House Corvane — "the Sentinel"
- **Seat / Spire:** Gullmouth, Sentinel Spire. **Colours:** deep blue, silver. **Sigil:** a black cormorant diving into a silver wave. **Words:** *"We go under and come up."*
- **Power:** the navy (most of the Concord fleet), harbour tolls, the river-mouth customs.
- **Character:** proud old sea-war house; reformist in rhetoric, ruthless in practice; loves the Lows as sailors' recruiting ground. Absorbed founding House Ostry by marriage (~CY 120).
- **Relations:** old rival of Fennick (law vs. sea); uneasy friendship with Quell (sailors); in debt to Lantry for the fleet refit.
- **Role in saga:** S1 Lord Admiral **Ruel Corvane** runs for Helm and dies; his daughter **Maren** carries the house through civil war, exile and power — the saga's great tragic arc. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-001, Maren's late-saga turn]**
- **Scheme:** Corvane captains sell "storm-berths" (priority anchorage in storms) to merchant ships; the fleet's powder accounts are padded. *Consequence (r2):* the guns run dry at the Sluice Gates (S2E11).
- **Heirloom (r2):** "the Admiral's Pearl" — the head of the house's black pearl ring, hinged and hollow, "won at Lirenne" by the founding admiral. The family does not know more than that. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-027]**

### HS-002 House Dask — "the Granary"
- **Seat / Spire:** Dask Barrows in the Polders, Sheaf Spire. **Colours:** ochre, earth brown. **Sigil:** a bronze sickle over three furrows. **Words:** *"Every mouth owes us."*
- **Power:** grain (most of the delta's bread), dike-labour, the polder levies (big but poorly trained infantry).
- **Character:** cold, patient, transactional; marries for land; never forgets a debt. Conquered founding House Moll (CY 88) — the Moll polders are still restless.
- **Relations:** supplies everyone, trusts no one; secretly tied to Lantry via grain-futures.
- **Role in saga:** the dead Helm **Lucan Dask** (E1) was theirs; his nephew **Garrick Dask** is the swing vote and the saga's great survivor-traitor; POV in Act III. Dask polders are flooded deliberately in S10.
- **Scheme:** cornering grain before flood seasons; selling "relief bread" to the Lows at triple price through Undertow middlemen. *Consequence (r2):* Dask's vote is always for sale for bread or water — to Odo in S1, to Maren in S7; when the Ossari weir dries the polders in CY 314 the house is desperate. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-024]**

### HS-003 House Ilvane — "the Songblood"
- **Seat / Spire:** Pale Crag cloister, Pale Spire. **Colours:** white, pale violet. **Sigil:** an open white hand holding a spiral stone. **Words:** *"Hear, and be still."*
- **Power:** the ear — most Cantors come from Ilvane or are trained at Pale Crag; dominates the Choir; sells "priority songs" in storms.
- **Character:** serene, pious, inbred by policy ("keep the blood true"), silver-haired by birth **(belief)**. The family head is **Lord Aurin Ilvane**; real power is his mother, **First Cantor Vesna Ilvane**.
- **Relations:** controls two votes in practice (Ilvane + Choir) — but in S1 they split; contempt for Fennick, need for Lantry's money.
- **Role in saga:** home of **Ione** (POV, S1–S4). The house's founding legend and its bloodline are the centre of the saga's lie. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-004, SEC-005]**
- **Scheme:** the Choir's debts to Lantry are paid by secret sale of storm-priority (*"the Choir owes the bank its roof"* — why it votes Fennick in S1); "gleaning" of children with the ear; Gleaners are paid by the head ("ear-money"). *Consequence (r2):* the Choir is short of Cantors and the shortage worsens every year — by S5 some Spires are sung in shifts by children; by S6 great houses must hire singers from the Lows. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-005, SEC-029]**

### HS-004 House Rusk — "the Stair"
- **Seat / Spire:** the Stair (fortress-town on the Red Escarpment), Falls Spire. **Colours:** rust red, black. **Sigil:** a red iron gate in a black cliff. **Words:** *"Shut, and stand."*
- **Power:** the best standing army (pike-and-shot), the only easy road from the uplands, saltpeter trade.
- **Character:** soldier house, plain-spoken, poor in coin, rich in honour; a "sword house" among the founders that won its Spire by destroying House Vanth in the First Upland War (CY 141).
- **Relations:** despises the Steps' politics; long border history with the Ossari (war, trade, hostages).
- **Role in saga:** **Lady Brenna Rusk** and sergeant **Kes Mabry**; the Battle of the Stair (S3) is the first great land battle; the Stair falls.
- **Scheme:** the Rusk quartermasters sell Ossari saltpeter twice (to the Concord and back to the smugglers). *Consequence (r2):* at the Battle of the Stair the Ossari guns fire Rusk powder (S3). After the Stair falls, Rusk keeps its Conclave vote "in exile" at Highwater (the Spire is lost, the seat is not — a standing quarrel), and its quartermasters keep their trade.

### HS-005 House Quell — "the Shoals"
- **Seat / Spire:** Brackwater on the barrier islands, Storm Spire. **Colours:** sea-green, bone. **Sigil:** a green eel coiled through a ring. **Words:** *"Calm is bought."*
- **Power:** salt pans, fishing fleets, privateers, wreckers, smuggling routes to the outside world.
- **Character:** half lords, half pirates; elect their own captain-lord among the island clans; loyal to whoever pays and respects them.
- **Relations:** natural ally of Corvane at sea, natural enemy of the Relay (they live on unrecorded trade).
- **Role in saga:** **Captain-Lord Jaro Quell** (POV from S2) — the rogue who keeps changing sides and keeps being right.
- **Scheme:** false lights — wrecking ships on purpose and "salvaging" them; the Storm Spire is sung *softer* for ships that did not pay the calm-fee. *Consequence (r2):* Quell salvage-brokers, with Piers Lantry, float "Lirenne salvage bonds" and a pretender to sell them (S6) — which splits the Lows' vote and later pays for the Raising. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-016]**

### HS-006 House Fennick — "the Listeners"
- **Seat / Spire:** the Listening House in Highwater, Sluice Spire (failing). **Colours:** slate grey, sulphur yellow. **Sigil:** a grey hare with raised ears. **Words:** *"Nothing unheard."*
- **Power:** the Relay (all fast messages), the Hearings (courts), the city watch's informers ("Listeners").
- **Character:** clerks who became lords (raised to great house in CY 251 when Harrow died out); despised as upstarts, feared as the ears of the realm.
- **Relations:** client of Lantry, enemy of Corvane, useful to everyone.
- **Role in saga:** **Lord Odo Fennick** — S1 villain-POV, Helm from S1E12, killed S5. His son **Zev** inherits a broken house (POV from S6).
- **Scheme:** falsified or delayed relay messages (markets move on them); selling court verdicts; the Sluice Spire is under-sung to save Cantor fees. Below the lord's sight, relay-keepers sell messages early on their own account. *Consequence (r2):* one keeper's double sale in CY 312 sets off both murders of S1.

### HS-007 House Lantry — "the Counting House"
- **Seat:** the Counting House on the Steps, Highwater. No Spire. **Colours:** black, copper. **Sigil:** a copper balance whose one pan holds water. **Words:** *"All water finds its level."*
- **Power:** dry-notes (the paper money of the realm), loans to every house, the Purchased Seat (bought Harrow's vote, CY 252).
- **Character:** merchant dynasty led by **Dame Ursel Lantry**, "the Counting Widow"; polite, exact, never raises its voice.
- **Relations:** creditor of all; holds Corvane's fleet debt, the Choir's debt, Fennick's loyalty.
- **Role in saga:** the S1–S6 money plot: flood speculation and the dry-note fraud; collapses in S6. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-007]**
- **Scheme:** issuing notes against land the bank expects to be "let go" in floods; Account Nine.

## 2. Extinct and fallen houses (for history and fan maps)

### HS-008 House Ashare — "the Tide-Kings" (extinct OS 700)
- **Seat:** Lirenne (now under the Sunk), the Sunken Spire. **Colours:** pearl, deep teal. **Sigil:** a silver spiral shell. **Words (recorded):** *"Many mouths, one water."*
- **Public history:** Keepers of Lirenne for 700 years, later hereditary "Tide-Kings"; ended with **Queen Neveh**, who "went mad and sang her city into the sea". Their spiral sign survives as the folk symbol of the Lady Below.
- **Role in saga:** the deepest layer of the mystery; the Sunken Vote; a pretender claims their blood in S6. **[SEALED-REF: SEC-002, SEC-003]**
