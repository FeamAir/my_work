## 0. Principles (director's frame, 2026-11-22)

- **Season 1: 7 point-of-view heroes** from every layer of society.
- **Every season adds new main heroes** — replacements for the dead and *unexpected promotions* of second-circle characters. Over the saga: **25 main heroes** (table §3).
- **A wide second circle (~40)**, each with a secret and a link to the past; the surprise links between seasons run through them (§4).
- **AI limit:** at most **8–10 main faces on screen per season** (with full reference sheets). Second-circle characters get lighter "costume + anchor" references; veiled, masked, helmeted or voiceless characters are favoured. Season-by-season face budget is in `saga_arc.md`.
- **Every main hero has two visual anchors** (hair detail + signature item), a distinct voice and a distinct heroism.
- Minors at the saga start (r3, complete): Pell (15), Wick (11), Esk (13), Zev (14), Daro (16), Lio (17), Mags (15), Patch (12), the baby Tibb; Ione is 18. **No romance or sexual content** with anyone under 18 *in the season being written*; Tibb is a minor through the whole saga. **r7:** love in this saga is adult (18+ in the season's year), rated TV 16+, and always causes a decision — a vote, a silence, a betrayal, a document; lines and age gate in §6 and `romance_lines.md`.

## 1. Season-1 point-of-view heroes (7)

### Pell [CH-0001] — the mudlark *(poor; lead of the saga from Act II)*
15, a mudlark of **Ninth Sluice** in the Lows: wades flooded lanes salvaging metal and lost things, sells to the Undertow, raises her brother Wick with their grandmother Nan. Quick, funny, rude, allergic to authority, physically brave and secretly terrified of deep water. **Wants:** to get Wick and Nan "up the Steps" — out of the Lows before the next flood. **Hides:** that she hums Nan's forbidden lullaby, and that an echo-shard she found in the back-canal *answers* when she does (SEC-002; where it came from: SEC-027, sealed). Her family history reaches back 312 years (SEC-003) and seventeen years (SEC-004). **Anchors:** cropped rust-brown hair with one long braid on the **left** bound by a blue tin sluice-tag; cracked black echo-shard, no bigger than a thumbnail, on a cord; from S2E8 also Nan's single spiral-shell earring (left). Mud-grey wading leggings, patched oilskin. **Voice:** fast, low, Lows slang, whistles instead of singing (Hush Law). Line: *"Water doesn't care who you are. That's the only fair thing in this city."* **Heroism:** she keeps going back into the water for other people. **Fate:** survives the saga; pays with her voice in the finale. **Love (r7):** none before she is adult — S1–S4 carry no romantic beat for her. From S7 (she is 25): Esk, the Dry Queen's son (L4); in S6 she refuses Lio's suit. The line ends alive.

### Ione Ilvane [CH-0002] — the novice Cantor *(great house; lead of Act I)*
18, novice of the Choir, "daughter" of Lord Aurin Ilvane, granddaughter of First Cantor Vesna. Gifted, disciplined, devout, and the only Ilvane who walks into the Lows to look at what the songs are for. **Wants:** to be worthy of the song and to save the failing Sluice Spire. **Hides:** doubts about the Choir's "priority songs"; later, the truth of her birth (SEC-004). Loses her voice in S1E10 singing a Great Song to save Ninth Sluice; from then on speaks in hand-sign (subtitled / interpreted). **Anchors:** long pale-silver hair (kept silver by a weekly "Cantor's rinse" — note for art: roots must be visible dark in S3–S4 captivity); a violet silk throat-band (black after she loses her voice); a silver tuning-fork pendant. **Voice:** clear, formal, precise until E10; after — sign + inner VO. **Heroism:** gives up the thing she loves most for strangers. **Fate:** dies S4E11 singing the **Crown Spire** with Pell against the flood wave — the saga's first protagonist change. **Keeps acting after death:** her tuning fork (Pell's from S4E12), her journal (published S5E6), the question of who sent the wave (SEC-024, sealed). **Love (r7):** Brother Elm loves her and never says it (L5; seed S1E11, feeling from S2 when she is 19); her journal's missing pages are his (SEC-049).

### Hollis Kett [CH-0003] — the clerk *(middle class)*
26, junior clerk of the Lantry Counting House, raised as a charity apprentice of the bank after his father drowned. Precise, ironic, cowardly by habit and brave by accident; solves paper puzzles (forged notes, false ledgers) — the "episode detective" of S1–S6. **Wants:** a quiet promotion and a dry room. **Hides:** that he skims nothing — and is ashamed of how much he could. Discovers Account Nine (the bank's flood speculation) and his own father's signature on the CY 295 Dry Charter (SEC-006). **Anchors:** round spectacles with a cracked **left** lens; ink-stained right fingers; long grey clerk's coat; brass ledger-clip in the breast pocket (Ursel's gift, S1E1). **Voice:** dry, quick, ledger metaphors ("carried forward", "balance"). **Heroism:** tells the truth when a lie would pay. **Fate:** presumed dead in the bank fire S6E9. Status after that is sealed (SEC-011). **Love (r7):** Maren Corvane (L2, SEC-047) — seeds only in S1; by letter in S2; her purser in S3; lovers from S4E12. He makes her father's crime public rather than cut one page (S6E7). His brass clip was his father's (line A, SEC-050).

### Kes Mabry [CH-0004] — the sergeant *(soldier / Mids)*
34, pike sergeant of House Rusk, commoner, twenty years' service; in S1 commands the Rusk guard of the Conclave delegation in Highwater, later at the Stair. Dry humour, protective of her soldiers, contemptuous of politics. **Wants:** to bring her people home alive and retire to a dry farm. **Hides:** she was the young soldier who shut the evacuation gate of Ninth Sluice on the Night of Still Water (CY 295) on orders — thousands drowned behind it (SEC-009). In S1E10 she opens the gate against orders. **Anchors:** short black hair with shaved sides; burn scar along the **right** jaw; dented half-helm with a painted white stripe; rust-red Rusk tabard. **Voice:** low, gravelly, upland accent. **Heroism:** holds the line for others. **Fate:** dies S7E11 at the Battle of the Hundred Locks.

### Lord Ruel Corvane [CH-0005] — the candidate *(politics)*
48, Lord Admiral, head of House Corvane, candidate for Helm: charismatic reformer who promises pumps for the Lows and an open Tithe Map. Loved by sailors and the Lows. **Wants:** the Helm's chair to save the delta from the bank. **Hides:** he poisoned the old Helm to force an early election (SEC-001). Murdered on the Steps in S1E8, dies S1E9 — the saga's first great shock; his crime is revealed S1E11 and becomes his daughter's inheritance. **Anchors:** grey-streaked dark beard; deep-blue admiral's coat with silver cormorant clasp; black pearl ring on the **left** hand. **Voice:** warm baritone, sea idioms. **Keeps acting after death:** the ring (a Corvane heirloom, "the Admiral's Pearl"; hinged and hollow), the burned ledger page, the debt to Lantry, his last words, and what his sister did to protect him (SEC-023). **Love (r7):** Jaro Quell, since CY 305 (L1, SEC-046) — the second stern lamp of the *Patient Gull*; the one person he did not tell. His letters keep acting after his death (Ysa → Odo → Zev → Jaro).

### Maren Corvane [CH-0006] — the heir *(great house / politics; the saga's tragic pole)*
19, Ruel's daughter, a junior naval officer who wanted the sea, not politics. Brilliant tactician, loyal, proud. **Wants (S1):** to protect her father's name. **Hides (from S1E11):** that she buried the proof of his crime — the page and the ring, which she takes from his hand at the wake. Over the saga she becomes the realm's strongest leader — and its greatest danger (SEC-010). **Anchors:** dark curly hair tied high with a silver cord; brass spyglass on her belt; from S6E12 her father's black pearl ring, worn openly. Short deep-blue naval jacket (r3 — her father wears the long coat). **Voice:** crisp, commanding, short sentences. **Heroism:** never abandons her crews. **Fate:** dies S12E11. **Love (r7):** Hollis Kett (L2, SEC-047) — whenever she must choose between him and the name, she chooses the name. From S7E3 married by contract to Lord Zev Fennick (L3, SEC-048); a consort and no heir.

### Lord Odo Fennick [CH-0010] — the Listener *(villain POV)*
55, master of the Relay and the Hearings, candidate for Helm and, from S1E12, Helm. Soft-spoken, patient, sentimental about his son, genuinely believes order is mercy. **Wants:** the chair, and for his upstart house to be accepted. **Hides:** that he did *not* kill the old Helm (everyone thinks he did) — and what he is willing to do to keep power once he has it. **Anchors:** shaved head; silver brooch shaped like a hare's ear; slate-grey robe with sulphur-yellow lining. **Voice:** near-whisper, never hurried. **Fate:** killed S5E11.

## 2. Key antagonists and figures (S1 main faces: Vesna, Ursel, Nan)

**First Cantor Vesna Ilvane [CH-0012]** — 73, head of the Choir, real power of House Ilvane. Mute for forty years (the cost of a Great Song); speaks only through her young "Voice", Brother Elm, who reads her hand-signs aloud — no lip-sync ever (production gift). Kind to children, merciless to the realm. **Wants:** the song kept "pure" — and the lie kept. **Hides:** almost everything (SEC-005, SEC-012; her life before the Choir is `[SEALED — ask lead]`). Small tell for directors: when moved, her ringed fingers tap five beats. **Anchors:** violet veil, white hair, silver rings on every finger of the signing hand. Casts the Sunken Vote in S1E12. **Fate:** dies S10E9.

**Dame Ursel Lantry [CH-0011]** — 62, "the Counting Widow", head of the bank. Courteous, grandmotherly, terrifying. **Wants:** the bank to outlast every house. **Hides:** Account Nine (SEC-007) and the death of Hollis's father (SEC-006); she raised Hollis. **Anchors:** black and copper gown, pince-nez on a copper chain, walking stick with a scale-shaped handle. **Fate:** dies S6E10 when the bank falls (POV in S6).

**Nan Oshra [CH-0015]** — 70, Pell's grandmother, net-mender, elder of a Quiet cell. Sharp-tongued, tender, frightened of the Choir for reasons she never explains. Teaches Pell the lullaby's verses one by one. **Hides:** SEC-002, SEC-003, part of SEC-004; she had an elder sister, Ves, taken by the Gleaners in CY 244 — "I never learned which robe she wears". **Anchors:** a single spiral-shell earring (left; "it had a mate"), indigo headscarf. **Fate:** killed S2E8; her last words drive Act II.

**Tsura Den, the Dry Queen [CH-0013]** — 42, war-leader of the Ossari kite-clans. Seen only as kites on the escarpment at the end of S1; POV from S3. Practical, proud, desperate: her people's rivers are dying. **Anchors:** shaved head with one copper ring in the **left** ear, sand-orange layered coat, a kite-spar staff. **Fate:** dies S11E10.

**Mother Silt [CH-0014]** — 54, boss of the Undertow, rules from a barge-palace in the Lows; clouded eyes, misses nothing. Sells everyone, protects children — and in S4 learns that those two rules cannot both be kept. Keeps a "book of sold men". Second circle S1–S3, main from S4. **Fate:** dies S9E5 in the surge after the First Raising, getting the children out; the book goes to Tibb.
