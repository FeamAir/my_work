# Season 1 — *Dry Tithe* — Episode Outline

Version 1.0 · 2026-11-22 · author: lead-screenwriter · status: **draft** · Canon: v0.1 (world from `series_bible.md`)
Spoiler level: internal (clue tags reference `sealed/master_mystery.md`; do not copy clue explanations into public material).

**Season question:** *Who will hold the Helm — and who will pay for it?*
**Season mystery (closed E11):** who poisoned Helm Lucan Dask (SEC-001).
**In-world span:** CY 312, Longdark 6 → Still 5 (~35 days). Election by law on the Still Days.
**POV lines:** Pell (P) · Ione (I) · Hollis (H) · Kes (K) · Ruel (R) · Maren (M) · Odo Fennick (O). Max 3 lines per episode.
**Main faces:** Pell, Ione, Hollis, Kes, Ruel, Maren, Odo, Ursel, Nan (+ Vesna, veiled).
**Key locations (13):** see `series_bible.md` §6. New locations per episode ≤2.
Tags: `[LONG-24]` battle/disaster episode, `[LONG-28]` finale, `♪` mystery leitmotif cue, `[ECHO: SEC-…]` line with a future second meaning.

### E1 — "The Ninth Sluice" · pilot ~30 min · Longdark 6–7 · lines P / I / H
- **Cold open:** the Chronicler [VO] over the Breaking tapestry — the "mad" Sunken Queen, the Nine, the founding of the Concord; sign-off "…and the water kept its counsel." (clues SEC-003, SEC-011b)
- **P:** Pell mudlarks the flooded lanes of Ninth Sluice with Wick; finds a black echo-shard; at home in Nan's stilt-house she hums Nan's lullaby and the shard hums back — only Wick sees ♪ (SEC-002). Nan's rule established: never sing.
- **I:** Ione sings the Crown Spire at the Dry Palace tithe-reading (iconic crown shot, back to camera). Below, Lord Admiral Ruel Corvane clasps Helm Lucan's hand left-handed; a drop of blood; Ruel refuses the tithe cup (SEC-001). Helm Lucan collapses mid-reading of the Tithe Map.
- **H:** Hollis at the Lantry Counting House balances "Account Nine"; Ursel Lantry gives him a pencil and a kind word. The relay rings the **death-tone**; the hall goes silent.
- **Cliffhanger:** across the delta every echo-stone rings the death-tone — Pell's shard rings a different, lower note ♪. Wick: "Pell… it's singing back."

### E2 — "Candidates" · ~21 min · Longdark 8–10 · lines R / O / P
- **Hand-off:** the death-tone's morning: the Conclave declares the election for the Still Days (30 days).
- **R:** Ruel declares — pumps for the Lows, an open Tithe Map; the Lows cheer him as the hero of the Still Water boats. Maren, called from her ship, reluctantly becomes his aide.
- **O:** Odo Fennick declares "steadiness"; at home, gentle with his son Zev; at the Listening House, his captain Fitch reports an echo-shard "ringing wrong" somewhere in the Lows.
- **P:** Pell tries to sell the shard to Mother Silt's fence; Nan drags her home; over the net-mending Pell says she had a sister "the water took before I was born"; Nan says their mother Liss "drowned in a dry year" (SEC-004, SEC-012). Nan's family name: Oshra (SEC-003). Episode puzzle: who stole the street's dry-tithe box (Pell solves it — a Listener's bribe).
- **Cliffhanger:** Fitch, on a rooftop, watches Pell slip the shard back on its cord.

### E3 — "The Hush Law" · ~21 min · Longdark 11–14 · lines P / I / H
- **Hand-off:** Fitch's Listeners sweep Ninth Sluice for "wrong-ringing" stones.
- **P:** Pell's friend Lio, a street whistler, sings aloud — once — for a crowd; arrested; a night on the **tide-post** (shown by water rising, sound, Pell's face; 16+). Pell and Wick sneak him water. Lio vanishes from the Lows afterwards.
- **I:** Ione's hair is given the weekly "Cantor's rinse" (SEC-004). Vesna (through Brother Elm's voice) assigns her to help the failing Sluice Spire nightly; among Vesna's novices are Lows "foundlings blessed by the Lady" (SEC-005). Ione's first hoarseness — the cost shown.
- **H:** Episode puzzle: a forged dry-note in the morning count; Hollis traces it to a rival desk (Fossett). Promoted to the private office. Ursel, in passing: "Account Nine closes on the third Still Day." (SEC-007, SEC-015)
- **Cliffhanger:** Hollis opens Account Nine's ledger: every entry is a street of Ninth Sluice.

### E4 — "Ledger and Lantern" · ~21 min · Longdark 15–18 · lines R / K / H
- **Hand-off:** Hollis reading the Ninth Sluice street names, one by one.
- **R:** Ruel courts votes — Captain-Lord Jaro Quell (calm-fees joke: *"Calm is bought."*) and Lady Brenna Rusk; Ione carries his messages to her father Aurin Ilvane; she sees Ruel twist his pearl ring — hinged (SEC-001).
- **K:** Kes Mabry introduced commanding Brenna's guard; teases the Ossari hostage boy Esk who flies a kite from the Rusk barracks roof; she avoids looking at the old Ninth Sluice evacuation gate (SEC-009). Line: *"Who pays for the dry? Whoever's standing lowest."*
- **H:** in the archive Hollis finds the CY 295 Dry Charter signed by a clerk "T. Kett" — "a common name" (SEC-006). That night he sees Garrick Dask leave Ursel's office by the back stair (SEC-013).
- **Cliffhanger:** Garrick to Odo, in the Ring's corridor: "Dask votes for bread. Who has the most bread, Odo?"

### E5 — "Mudlark" · ~21 min · Longdark 19–21 · lines P / I (two lines — the first crossing)
- **Hand-off:** at dawn Ione, disguised in a plain cloak, pays a mudlark to guide her through the Lows to the Sluice Spire — the mudlark is Pell.
- **P + I:** a walk through the Lows: treadmill pumps, the dry-pledge racket, Mother Silt's barge, the lime-pit workers whistling. Class collision played with humour and edge. At the Sluice Spire, Ione sings and fails; Pell hums without thinking and the Spire steadies for three seconds ♪ (SEC-002). Ione hums two notes back, stops, puzzled (SEC-004). Neither understands.
- **Episode puzzle:** a child missing from the lanes — Pell and Ione find her with a Gleaner (Sister Venn's novices) "for testing"; Ione orders the child returned. Pell: "Your lot take ours. Did you know that?"
- **Cliffhanger:** Fitch's Listeners close the lane; Pell runs, Ione is recognised and escorted home — and tells nobody what she heard.

### E6 — "Skin, Not Mouth" · ~21 min · Longdark 22–24 · lines O / H / R (midseason turn)
- **Hand-off:** Fitch reports to Odo: a Cantor of Ilvane was in the Lows with "the wrong-ringing girl".
- **O:** Dr Ambry, the Helm's physician, declares to the Conclave that Lucan was poisoned — "by the skin, not the mouth"; the taster Juno lived (SEC-001). Every eye on Odo; Odo, privately, is genuinely shaken — *he didn't do it*.
- **R:** Ruel publicly accuses Fennick; the Ring erupts; Ruel's popularity soars.
- **H:** Hollis confirms the scheme: Lantry has pre-sold Ninth Sluice land as "to be reclaimed" — the bank expects it to flood during the Still Days, with 20,000 people in it (SEC-007). He copies the ledger.
- **Cliffhanger:** Fossett, in the doorway, watching Hollis copy.

### E7 — "Echo" · ~21 min · Longdark 25–30 · lines H / P / M
- **Hand-off:** Hollis flees the Counting House with his copy under his coat.
- **H + P:** chased through the Mids by Lantry men, he falls into the Lows; Pell and Wick hide him in Nan's stilt-house (Nan furious, then kind). Episode puzzle: Hollis and Pell decode a relay tone sequence (Bede Lark) proving Fennick's Listeners are hunting the shard.
- **M:** Maren meets Hollis secretly and takes the ledger copy to her father. In his cabin Ruel reads it, goes grey, burns **one** page (SEC-001). Sister Marl, the Choir apothecary, is found dead with a Corvane silver mark in her hand.
- **Cliffhanger:** Ione, through Elm's voice at dinner, hears Vesna's sign: the Choir will vote for Fennick.

### E8 — "Still Day One" · ~21 min · Longdark 31 → Still 1 · lines K / I / P
- **Hand-off:** the first bell of the Still Days; every Spire falls silent in turn for retuning — the city holds its breath.
- **K:** Kes's guard in the Ring as the vote is postponed by a challenge (Corvane vs. Fennick precedence). **I:** Ione sings in the retuning chorus of Cantors at the Choir Spire. **P:** in the Lows the Still Days rite — commoners kneel, hands over mouths; Wick: "Why do we cover our mouths?" (SEC-003). Nan teaches Pell the lullaby's second verse: "older than the Concord".
- **Cliffhanger:** the night procession down the Steps — a masked man stabs Ruel; Kes kills the assassin; a Quell token falls from his hand (red herring; SEC-013).

### E9 — "The Steps" · ~22 min · Still 2 · lines R / M / O
- **Hand-off:** Ruel carried up the Steps bleeding; the city learns in minutes through the relay.
- **R + M:** Ruel's last hours on the *Patient Gull*: with Maren and Ione; his last words to Maren: *"Forgive what I did for them."* `[ECHO: SEC-001]` He dies — the season's big loss. The black pearl ring stays on his hand at the wake (Maren removes it in S6).
- **O:** Odo contests Maren's right to cast the Corvane vote; quietly he is offered the bank's help to "settle the Lows". Kes reports the assassin wore Polder clay on his boots (SEC-013). Cantor Sabin of the Sluice Spire seen with a new purse (SEC-015).
- **Cliffhanger:** on the third Still Day the Sluice Spire is not retuned — its hum stops. In Ninth Sluice, water begins to seep through the sluice-gate seams.

### E10 — "Still Water" · `[LONG-24]` · Still 3 night · lines P / I / K
- **Hand-off:** the seep becomes a spray; the old sluice-gate cracks.
- **The flood of Ninth Sluice** (disaster set piece: wide shots of rising water, lantern lines, rooftops; personal threads only): Pell, Wick and Nan escape over roofs; Pell's friend **Mags** drowns; Pell pulls a baby (Tibb) from a floating cradle tied with a navy knot (SEC-017). Old Cantor Sabin, voiceless, tries to sing and "sings out" — dies on the stair (RULE-001 shown).
- **K:** Kes, ordered to keep the evacuation gate to the Mids shut, opens it: *"Not twice."* (SEC-009) Thousands pour through.
- **I:** Ione defies Vesna, climbs the Sluice Spire and begins a Great Song alone; the water flattens; a low hum joins her from a nearby roof — Pell ♪ (SEC-002); the Spire holds long enough. Ione turns, sees the girl, and collapses.
- **Cliffhanger:** dawn: Ione wakes and opens her mouth — no sound comes.

### E11 — "Ink" · ~22 min · Still 4 · lines H / M / I
- **Hand-off:** Ione's silent scream; Elm signs to her for the first time — the beginning of her new language.
- **H:** in the drowned stilt-house Hollis retrieves the second ledger copy Pell hid in Nan's net-chest; the counting house's double entry preserved the page Ruel burned: Ruel's payment to Sister Marl "for the pearl's filling" — Ruel poisoned the Helm (SEC-001 **revealed**); the taster Juno confirms the handshake. Ursel finds Hollis's empty desk and murmurs: "His father had a beautiful hand." (SEC-006)
- **M:** Hollis gives Maren the page. She reads, and hides it — her first compromise (SEC-010 pattern). She will vote.
- **I:** Ione, mute, signs to her father to keep his vote for Corvane. Recall montage of the SEC-001 clues (6 shots).
- **Cliffhanger:** Vesna's Voice announces: in a tie, the First Cantor will speak for the drowned.

### E12 — "The Sunken Vote" · `[LONG-28]` · Still 5 · lines O / M / P
- **Hand-off:** the Ring at dawn; eight seats, one empty chair draped in teal for the Sunken Vote.
- **O + M:** the vote: Corvane (Maren), Quell, Ilvane (Aurin, defying his mother), Rusk — against Fennick, Lantry, the Choir, Dask. **4–4.** Vesna, veiled, rises and casts the Sunken Vote: **Odo Fennick is Helm.** His first act: Ninth Sluice declared "reclaimed land" (the bank's grab completed). Maren refuses to swear, walks out of the Ring, and sails with the Corvane fleet. Kes is stripped of her rank for opening the gate and sent to the Stair with Esk.
- **P:** Pell, Wick, Nan and the baby on the ruins of their home at low tide; Hollis, now a fugitive, joins them. Pell hums; far out on the Sunk, the crown of the Sunken Spire rings faintly ♪ (SEC-002, SEC-018). Nan weeps and says nothing.
- **Final image:** at the Stair, Kes sees Ossari signal-fires and kites along the whole escarpment; Esk's own kite answers with a signal (subtitle: "home") (SEC-008, SEC-019).
- **Chronicler sign-off:** "And so the Concord had its Helm, and the Lows had their silence. …and the water kept its counsel."
- **Hand-off to S2:** S2E1 cold open answers the final image from the other side — Tsura Den on the escarpment, reading Esk's kite.
