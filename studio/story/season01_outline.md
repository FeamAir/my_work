## The first three episodes — retention design

| | What the viewer gets | The question that brings them back |
|---|---|---|
| **E1** | a flood in the first second; a world rule seen, not explained (a girl sings, the sea lies flat); a ruler dies mid-sentence; a stone that sings back | *What is that stone — and what was the Helm about to say about the Ninth Sluice?* |
| **E2** | the hero-candidate in the slums, a theft played for laughs, a secret policeman on the roof; the grandmother knows the stone | *What does Nan know? Will the boy who sang be punished?* |
| **E3** | the price of singing shown on a friend's body (the tide-post); the price of the song shown in Ione's throat; and the engine of the season: a ledger in which every line is a street of the heroine's district, with a closing date | *They are going to drown twenty thousand people on a date. Who stops it?* |

By the end of E3 the viewer holds: four heroes on three floors of the city, one magic rule with its cost, one murder mystery, one ticking date, and one motif (five notes). Nothing in E1–E3 requires a lore explanation to be felt.

### E2 — "Candidates" · ~21 min · Longdark 8–10 · lines P / R / O
- **Pays:** E1's last second — the same room, the stone still ringing (r4: in Pell's open hand, a Listeners' warning whistle far off — the whistle passes the lane; two of the six coppers are still owing). Nan comes in on the sound, sees the stone, goes white. She does not ask what it is. She asks **where** Pell found it — "the back-canal, by the old sluice" — and takes Pell's face in both hands. (SEC-012, SEC-027; reads as fear of the Hush Law.)
- **R (+P crossing):** Ruel declares on the Steps — pumps for the Lows, an open Tithe Map — and then does what no candidate does: comes down to Ninth Sluice by rowboat, the hero of the Still Water boats. The Lows roar. Maren, pulled off her ship to be his aide, hates every minute; Wick steals her cadet cap (running gag; pays in S9). Kes Mabry — first face shot — commands the Rusk guard that keeps the quay: one dry line, one look at the crowd's hungry children. (Light first appearances, r3: Corporal Ottar in Kes's file; Midshipwoman Nell Tern at Maren's elbow; in the crowd Sal Tern with a baby on her hip.)
- **O:** Odo Fennick declares "steadiness"; at home, gentle with his son Zev, who will not touch the Relay keys; at the Listening House, Captain Fitch reports a stone "ringing wrong" somewhere in the Ninth during the death-tone — keeper Bede Lark logged it.
- **P:** Pell tries to sell the stone to Mother Silt's fence, Tuck (*"Calm is bought, mudlark. So is everything."*); Nan drags her home. Behind them Mags, who saw the stone sing, sells Tuck the news of it and the name of the lane for one copper (r3; SEC-045 — pays S2E8–E9). Over the net-mending: Pell had a sister "the water took before I was born"; their mother Liss "drowned in a dry year" (SEC-004, SEC-012); the family name is Oshra — "an old Lows name, it means nothing" (SEC-003). Nan's single spiral-shell earring: "It had a mate." (SEC-026) That night Nan lays salt and a shell at the back-canal. Episode puzzle: who stole the street's dry-tithe box — Pell solves it (a Listener's bribe; the Listeners are buying eyes in the Ninth).
- **Cliffhanger:** at Ruel's rally Pell's friend Lio, drunk on the crowd, **sings** — aloud, in sight of a Spire. Listeners' whistles from three sides. On a roof, Fitch's eye goes past the singer to the cord at Pell's neck.

### E3 — "The Hush Law" · ~21 min · Longdark 11–14 · lines P / I / H
- **Pays:** the whistles close; Lio is taken in front of the crowd; Ruel's boat is already gone. Fitch's Listeners sweep Ninth Sluice for "wrong-ringing" stones.
- **P:** Lio's night on the **tide-post** (shown by water rising, sound, Pell's face; 16+). Pell and Wick sneak him water. At dawn he is alive, and will not speak to them. He vanishes from the Lows afterwards (pays S6). Pell hides the stone in Nan's net-chest.
- **I:** Ione's hair is given the weekly "Cantor's rinse" — by her mother Cressa's own hands, seen in the mirror (r3; SEC-004; pays S4E1). Vesna (through Brother Elm's voice) assigns her to help the failing Sluice Spire nightly — its keeper is old Cantor Sabin, voiceless, with two tired singers under him (r3); among Vesna's novices are Lows "foundlings blessed by the Lady" (SEC-005). Ione's first hoarseness — the cost shown.
- **H:** Episode puzzle: a forged dry-note in the morning count; Hollis traces it to a rival desk (Fossett). Promoted to the private office (in the corridor: Piers Lantry, the grandson who does not want the bank — r3). Ursel, in passing: "Account Nine closes on the third Still Day." (SEC-007, SEC-015)
- **Cliffhanger:** Hollis opens Account Nine's ledger: every entry is a street of Ninth Sluice — and each is marked "to be reclaimed". He counts on his fingers from the fourteenth of Longdark to the third Still Day: twenty-five days.

### E4 — "Ledger and Lantern" · ~21 min · Longdark 15–18 · lines R / K / H
- **Pays:** Hollis reading the Ninth Sluice street names aloud, one by one — over shots of those streets, and of Pell in one of them.
- **R:** Ruel courts votes — Captain-Lord Jaro Quell (who quotes his house words with a grin: *"Calm is bought."*) and Lady Brenna Rusk; Ione carries his messages to her father Aurin Ilvane; she sees Ruel twist his pearl ring — the pearl is hinged, and hollow (SEC-001, SEC-027).
- **K:** Kes's world: the Rusk barracks; she teases the Ossari hostage boy Esk — three years a hostage, since the Rim Skirmish (r3) — who flies a kite from the roof; a Hadden officer, rust-red coat with one sand-orange sleeve, laughs with the boy in his own tongue, and Corporal Ottar understands the joke and does not laugh (SEC-044); she avoids looking at the old Ninth Sluice evacuation gate (SEC-009); she catches one of her guardsmen, Ansel Fray, selling a gate pass — a real one, with the quartermaster's office stamp on a pass that only the warden's clerk may issue (r3; SEC-030) — and lets him off with a blow — "next flood I'll hang you" (pays E10). Line: *"Who pays for the dry? Whoever's standing lowest."*
- **H:** in the archive Hollis finds the CY 295 Dry Charter signed by a clerk "T. Kett" — "a common name"; alone, he says "carried forward" (SEC-006, SEC-011b). That night he sees Garrick Dask leave Ursel's office by the back stair (SEC-013).
- **Cliffhanger:** Garrick to Odo, in the Ring's corridor: "Dask votes for bread. Who has the most bread, Odo?"

### E5 — "Mudlark" · ~21 min · Longdark 19–21 · lines P / I (two lines — the first crossing)
- **Pays:** Odo's answer, seen from below: at dawn Dask grain barges under Fennick's yellow seal slide through the Ninth Sluice canal; the price of bread in the Lows doubles before breakfast (the Chronicler gives the price to the copper). Pell is shouting at a baker when a stranger in a plain cloak offers her a silver mark to be guided to the Sluice Spire — Ione.
- **P + I:** a walk through the Lows: the treadmill pumps (Corr the Reedmaster: *"They always break the week before the Still. Funny, that."* — SEC-028), the dry-pledge racket, Mother Silt's barge, lime-pit workers whistling. Class collision played with humour and edge. At the Sluice Spire, Ione sings and fails; Pell hums without thinking and the Spire steadies for three seconds ♪ (SEC-002). Ione hums two notes back, stops, puzzled (SEC-004 — the two notes of the pre-title). Neither understands.
- **Episode puzzle:** a child missing from the lanes — Pell and Ione find her with a Gleaner novice "for testing"; the novice is counting coins (SEC-029). Ione orders the child returned. Pell: "Your lot take ours. Did you know that?"
- **Cliffhanger:** Fitch's Listeners close the lane; Pell runs, Ione is recognised and escorted home — and tells nobody what she heard.

### E6 — "Skin, Not Mouth" · ~21 min · Longdark 22–24 · lines O / H / R (midseason turn)
- **Pays:** Fitch reports to Odo: a Cantor of Ilvane was in the Lows with "the wrong-ringing girl". Odo decides *not* to tell the First Cantor — information is his coin.
- **O:** Dr Ambry, the Helm's physician, declares to the Conclave that Lucan was poisoned — "by the skin, not the mouth"; the taster Juno lived (SEC-001). Every eye on Odo; Odo, privately, is genuinely shaken — *he didn't do it*, and now needs to know who did. Kes holds the door of the Ring when it erupts (K bridge).
- **R:** Ruel publicly accuses Fennick; the Ring erupts; Ruel's popularity soars. (On a rewatch: watch his left hand.)
- **H:** Hollis confirms the scheme: Lantry has pre-sold Ninth Sluice land as "to be reclaimed" — the bank expects it to flood during the Still Days, with 20,000 people in it (SEC-007). He copies the ledger — twice, a clerk's habit.
- **Cliffhanger:** Fossett, in the doorway, watching Hollis copy.

### E7 — "Echo" · ~21 min · Longdark 25–30 · lines H / P / M
- **Pays:** Fossett shouts; Hollis flees the Counting House with the copies under his coat.
- **H + P:** chased through the Mids by Lantry men, he falls into the Lows; Pell and Wick hide him in Nan's stilt-house (Nan furious, then kind). Episode puzzle: with relay-keeper Bede Lark's bought codebook, Hollis and Pell decode a tone sequence proving Fennick's Listeners are hunting the stone. Bede, taking his coin: *"Old stones ring low. We throw them back."* — and, counting it: he has sold news to an admiral and a bank this season, "the same news, twice" (SEC-002 rule; SEC-001/013 chain). Pell hides the second copy in the net-chest.
- **M:** Maren meets Hollis secretly and takes a ledger copy to her father. In his cabin Ruel reads it, goes grey, burns **one** page (SEC-001). Sister Marl, the Choir apothecary, is found dead with a Corvane silver mark in her fist (SEC-023).
- **Cliffhanger:** Ione, through Elm's voice at dinner, hears Vesna's sign: the Choir will vote for Fennick.

### E8 — "Still Day One" · ~21 min · Longdark 31 → Still 1 · lines K / I / P
- **Pays:** Ione follows Vesna from the table and demands why. Vesna signs; Elm voices: *"The Choir owes the bank its roof."* (Elm's eyes say he has softened it.) Then the first bell of the Still Days: the Spires will fall silent in their appointed order — the coast today, Highwater on the third day — and the city holds its breath.
- **K:** Kes's guard in the Ring as the vote is postponed by a challenge (Corvane vs. Fennick precedence). **I:** Ione stands in the muster of Cantors at the Choir Spire as the retuning parties are sent out to the Storm and Sentinel Spires — and counts how few they are (r3: Highwater's own Spires are retuned on Still 3). **P:** in the Lows the Still Days rite — commoners kneel, hands over mouths; Wick: "Why do we cover our mouths?" (SEC-003). Nan teaches Pell the lullaby's second verse: "older than the Concord. Nine verses — and one you don't need." (SEC-025)
- **Cliffhanger:** the night procession down the Steps — a masked man stabs Ruel; Kes kills the assassin; a Quell token falls from his hand (red herring; SEC-013). On his left hand, a knot tattoo nobody yet reads (r3: Orm Hask).

### E9 — "The Steps" · ~22 min · Still 2 → dusk of Still 3 · lines R / M / O
- **Pays:** Ruel carried up the Steps bleeding; the city learns in minutes through the Relay.
- **R + M:** Ruel's last hours on the *Patient Gull*: with Maren and Ione; his last words to Maren: *"Forgive what I did for them."* `[ECHO: SEC-001]` He dies — the season's big loss. His sister Lady Ysa is already aboard — come from Gullmouth "by the night barge", a day sooner than any message could have fetched her (SEC-023). The black pearl ring is on his hand at the wake.
- **O:** Odo files a Challenge of the Voice against Maren: she is nineteen, and a bearer under twenty-one must be confirmed by the eldest adult of her house (r3) — which puts the Corvane vote in Lady Ysa's hand; quietly he is offered the bank's help to "settle the Lows". Kes reports the assassin wore Polder clay on his boots and a Lantern Company buckle under his coat (SEC-013; r3). Cantor Sabin of the Sluice Spire seen with a new purse (SEC-015). Guildmaster Dorran Strand, in the Ring's gallery, petitions for more pumps and is not heard (cameo, r3). One line sets the danger (r3): *"The Sluice has been singing on two voices and a mute since the summer. Miss it one night in this weather and you won't have days."*
- **Cliffhanger:** dusk on the third Still Day. By the order of the Still, the Sluice Spire falls silent for its retuning — and the song that should wake it does not come. In Ninth Sluice, water begins to seep through the sluice-gate seams.

### E10 — "Still Water" · `[LONG-24]` · Still 3 night · lines P / I / K
- **Pays:** the seep becomes a spray; the old sluice-gate cracks.
- **The flood of Ninth Sluice** (disaster set piece: wide shots of rising water, lantern lines, rooftops; personal threads only — and the pre-title of E1 happening again, shot for shot): the three treadmill pumps stand jammed, walkers hauling at a dead wheel; Corr pockets a clean-sawn axle-pin (SEC-028). Pell, Wick and Nan escape over roofs; Pell's friend **Mags** drowns; Pell — terrified of deep water — goes in and pulls a baby (Tibb — about a year old, sitting up and howling; r3) from a floating cradle tied with a navy knot (SEC-017); the baby's mother, Sal Tern, is not seen again. Old Cantor Sabin, voiceless, forces a breath-tone into the Spire and "sings out" — dies on the stair (RULE-001 shown).
- **K:** at the evacuation gate to the Mids the holders of bought passes are let through — the warden's clerk counting them, Ansel Fray on the bar (r3) — and the rest held; Kes, ordered to keep it shut, looks at her own hands on the bar (the pre-title's hands) and opens it: *"Not twice."* (SEC-009) Thousands pour through.
- **I:** Ione defies Vesna, climbs the Sluice Spire and begins a Great Song alone; the water flattens; a low hum joins her from a nearby roof — Pell ♪ (SEC-002); the Spire holds long enough. Ione turns, sees the girl, and collapses.
- **Cliffhanger:** dawn: Ione wakes and opens her mouth — no sound comes.

### E11 — "Ink" · ~22 min · Still 4 · lines H / M / I
- **Pays:** Ione's silent scream; Elm signs to her for the first time — the beginning of her new language (and of the series' sign glossary).
- **H:** in the drowned stilt-house Hollis retrieves the second copy from Nan's net-chest. It holds the page Ruel burned: **a Lantry sight-draft on the Corvane refit credit — forty marks to Sister Marl, apothecary, "by the Lord Admiral's own hand", dated Longdark 4**, two days before the Helm died. Why is an admiral's private draft filed under Account Nine? Because the bank was watching him. Hollis lays the chain out like a column: the draft; Marl's trade and her death; Ambry's "by the skin"; Juno, fetched from the kitchens, who remembers the left-handed clasp and the Helm sucking his knuckle; Ione's sign for *hinge*. No line of it says poison; the sum does — Ruel poisoned the Helm, to stop the map that would have drowned the Ninth, and the Ninth drowned anyway (SEC-001 **revealed**). Ursel finds Hollis's empty desk and murmurs: "His father had a beautiful hand." (SEC-006)
- **M:** Hollis gives Maren the page. She reads; at the wake she slips the ring from her father's finger and hides both — her first compromise (SEC-010 pattern; SEC-027). Lady Ysa confirms Maren's right to cast the Corvane vote "on one condition", spoken off screen — and the Corvane seal stays on Ysa's hand (r3; SEC-023; pays S2).
- **I:** Ione, mute, signs to her father to keep his vote for Corvane. Recall montage of the SEC-001 clues (6 shots).
- **Cliffhanger:** Vesna's Voice announces: in a tie, the First Cantor will speak for the drowned.

### E12 — "The Sunken Vote" · `[LONG-28]` · Still 5 (+ tag, Firstwater 6) · lines O / M / P
- **Pays:** the Ring at dawn; eight seats, one empty chair draped in teal for the Sunken Vote.
- **O + M:** the vote: Corvane (Maren), Quell, Ilvane (Aurin, defying his mother), Rusk — against Fennick, Lantry, the Choir, Dask. **4–4.** Vesna, veiled, rises; her ringed fingers tap five beats on the rail (SEC-026); she casts the Sunken Vote: **Odo Fennick is Helm.** His first act: Ninth Sluice declared "reclaimed land" (the bank's grab completed). Garrick Dask inherits his uncle's estate — "everything but one sealed chest" (pays S2E6). Maren refuses to swear, walks out of the Ring, and sails with the Corvane fleet. Kes is stripped of her rank for opening the gate and sent upriver to the Stair with the hostage Esk.
- **P:** Pell, Wick, Nan and the baby on the ruins of their home at low tide; Hollis, now a fugitive, joins them. Pell hums; the stone answers; **cut** to the Sunk — the crown of the Sunken Spire, bare at low tide, rings faintly ♪ (SEC-002, SEC-018; the audience hears it, the characters cannot). Nan weeps and says nothing.
- **Chronicler bridge + tag (r2):** *"Six days upriver, the new year had already begun."* Map stamp: the Stair, Firstwater 6, CY 313. Kes and Esk arrive under escort; along the whole escarpment, Ossari signal-fires and kites. Esk's own kite goes up and answers (subtitle: "home") (SEC-008, SEC-019).
- **Chronicler sign-off:** "And so the Concord had its Helm, and the Lows had their silence. …and the water kept its counsel."
- **Hand-off to S2:** S2E1 cold open answers the final image from the other side — Tsura Den on the rim, reading Esk's kite.
