## Part 1 — The 17 rulings that block Season 1

### DEC-03 — Corvane Sea Right and confirmation of a voice-bearer under 21
- **Ruling: A — both rules.** On screen in S1 only the Ring law is *spoken* (S1E9: Odo's Challenge of the Voice — Maren is 19; a bearer under 21 needs the confirmation of the eldest adult of the house). The Sea Right is *seen* in S1E11 (the Corvane seal stays on Ysa's hand when she confirms) and is first named in S2.
- *Why.* The Ring law gives S1E9 a rule the viewer can follow in one line; the Sea Right is the lever of S2 and should not cost S1 a second explanation.
- **Keeper.** Approve RULE-015 and RULE-018. HS-001: approve the amend. SEC-023: add to the S1E11 clue "Ysa confirms — and keeps the seal". Ysa's condition itself stays unspoken in S1 (pays S2E10).

### DEC-04 — Retuning schedule of the Still Days
- **Ruling: A — approved as written** (Still 1 Storm, Sentinel · Still 2 Sheaf, Pale · Still 3 Highwater's three: Choir at dawn, Crown at noon, Sluice at dusk · Still 4 Falls and the Lament for the Drowned · Still 5 the Joining). One clarification becomes part of the rule: **"retuning" = the Spire is let fall silent for some hours, then woken by the retuning song; a Spire in mid-retuning does not answer an ordinary solo tide-song.**
- *Why.* A public, fixed order turns the Still Days into a clock the audience can read; the clarification is what makes a *skipped* retuning (S1) deadly and what later scenes on a Still 3 need.
- **Foundation repair (mine).** `season01_outline.md` E8 had Ione singing in a retuning at the Choir Spire on Still 1 — against this schedule. Corrected: on Still 1 she stands in the **muster of Cantors** at the Choir Spire as the retuning parties leave for the coast, and counts how few they are. E9/E10: the Sluice Spire falls silent at **dusk on Still 3** and its retuning song never comes.
- **Keeper.** Approve RULE-019 with the clarification sentence; `world.md` §7.8 accordingly. EV-0304/0305: Sluice Spire silent from dusk, Still 3. Restricted proviso: sealed §10.

### DEC-05 — RULE-003 and the speed of the S1E10 flood
- **Ruling: B + the line from A.** RULE-003 now reads: *"A fully silent Spire lets the sea in within days — within hours on a storm tide, or where the Spire is already failing."* S1E9 carries the line (Kes's guard-captain or Corr, writer's choice): *"The Sluice has been singing on two voices and a mute since the summer. Miss it one night in this weather and you won't have days."*
- *Why.* The law stays true for healthy Spires (S4, S12 need "days"), and the Ninth drowns in a night for a stated reason the viewer has already seen (E3, E5: the Spire is failing).
- **Keeper.** Amend RULE-003 as quoted (bible §5 corrected). Approve RULE-024 (see DEC-11); the "two voices and mute Sabin" census line for CY 312 is canon.
