# Sigils, colours, recognisers and home tones — sheet for the art team and the composer

> **Revision r3 (lead-screenwriter, 2026-11-22 — rulings of `story/decisions_resolved.md`):** accepted as a **working sheet** for the concept artist, composer and sound director (to be confirmed at the style and voice tests; it is not a canon record). Colour corrections for Hadden and Tennant (DEC-61). The saga's title is HOLLOWSTONE.

Author: writer-houses · 2026-11-22 · brief r2 · Canon: v0.2 (house records HS-001…012, HS-090…095) · status: draft
Spoiler level: **public-safe** (no secrets on this sheet). Sigils, colours and words of the great houses are fixed by `houses_overview.md` and canon; everything marked *(proposal)* is new.

## 1. Rules

1. **Main colours are exclusive** (canon): Corvane deep blue · Dask ochre · Ilvane white · Rusk rust red · Quell sea-green · Fennick slate grey · Lantry black · Ashare pearl/teal (extinct) · the Choir undyed linen · Ossari sand-orange. A minor house may use its liege's main colour **plus** its own accent, never another great house's main colour as a field.
2. **Five on-screen recognisers per power:** colour · sigil · costume silhouette · architecture · leitmotif. Soldiers in helmets/masks; banners in house colours (AI crowd consistency).
3. **Danger pairs** (keep apart on screen): ochre (Dask, earthy, matte) vs sand-orange (Ossari, light, dusty) vs brick (Guild, dark red-brown) · sea-green (Quell, yellowish) vs teal (Ashare/the Pretender, bluish) vs slate blue-green (Vanth, historical, greyish) · Ilvane white (cool, silky) vs Choir linen (warm, coarse) vs Quell bone (warm, smooth) · Lantry copper (pinkish, polished) vs Dask bronze (yellow-brown, dull).

## 5. For the composer — the home-tone map

| Spire | Home tone | House theme |
|---|---|---|
| Crown | C | the Helm's fanfare (old Harrow chord) |
| Sluice | C♯, drifting flat | Fennick |
| Sentinel | D | Corvane |
| Pale | E | Ilvane |
| Sheaf | F | Dask |
| Choir | G | the Choir |
| Falls | A | Rusk (Vanth before CY 141) |
| Storm | B | Quell |
| Sunken | (canon: "the Sunken Spire's tone" — the composer and the lead fix it; keep it out of the house themes) | Ashare |

All the living home tones sit in one middle register; canon says relay stones "ring only on the high home-tones". **No house theme should use the low register as its tonic** — leave the low space empty for the mystery (`[SEALED — ask lead]` for why).
These pitches are proposals for the sound director; the bible only fixes that "each house theme is built on its Spire's tone".
