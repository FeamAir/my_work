```

> Words, sigil and colour are proposals (brick orange — check against Ossari sand-orange: brick is red-brown and dark, sand-orange is light and dusty). Dorran Strand (CH-0041) Guildmaster since CY 305 (proposal).

- **What:** the engineers of the Mids — pumps (wind and treadmill), dikes, locks, sluice-gates, the powder mills (with House Wray, HS-040). Founded CY 177 when the Highwater lock-builders and millwrights merged.
- **Power:** machines that work without the ear; jobs; and the **river gauges** — marked posts at every lock from the Polders to the sea, read daily since CY 190. Nobody on the Steps reads them. They become evidence in S7 (SEC-008 is proven by "Dorran's river gauges, the Choir's song logs, and the Vanth river book").
- **Ambition:** a **voice in the Ring** — "pumps vs. songs", the rise of the middle class. The Guild argues the Hill Voice precedent: if a bank can hold a voice without a Spire, so can the men who keep the dikes.
- **Numbers (CY 312):** ~140 treadmill pumps and ~60 wind-pumps in the Highwater Lows; ~2,000 guild masters and journeymen; ~5,000 treadmill walkers on the wheels (paid or debt-bound). RULE-007: ~400 walkers a day undo one hour of a Spire's silence.

**The yearly "breakage" (SEC-028, revealed S5E8).** Before every Still Days, guild journeymen **saw the axle-pins** of old pumps so they snap under load; the sluice-wardens must buy replacement pins and axles (~15 marks a pump) out of the dry-tithe. About 30 pumps "break" each year. In CY 312 all three Ninth Sluice pumps jammed on the flood night (S1E10) — doubling the dead. Corr the Reedmaster pocketed a clean-sawn pin. Corr's line: *"They always break the week before the Still. Funny, that."* (S1E5).

*Upward consequence:* S5E8 the walkers blackmail the Guild with the sawn pin; S5E11 the Guild's barges hold Ninth Sluice on the Night of a Thousand Voices; S6 the walkers' strike prices the election.

**The walkers' secret union** under the Guild (Corr, CH-0048): cells by wheel; signals by the treadmill's rhythm (a stamped code, Hush-Law-legal). Holds the power to stop every pump in Highwater (S6).

```

> "Words" are Silt's fence's line (S1E2), adopted as the syndicate's saying (proposal). Mother Silt (CH-0014) head since ~CY 290 (proposal; coordinate with writer-characters).

- **What:** the Lows' syndicate, run from a barge-palace in the Lows by Mother Silt. Smuggling (echo-shards, saltpeter, foreign silver via Quell's Varra clan), the dry-pledge racket, the rumour network ("the mouths"), the echo-shard black market, men hired out with no questions.
- **Dry-pledges:** sandbag-and-bucket "protection" for a lane in a flood — a household pays ~1 copper a week; the Undertow prices pledges by **leaked drafts of the Tithe Map** (bought from Map Office clerks, House Tolland, HS-022). When the bank wants a district's pledges, the Undertow sells them to the Counting House (Account Nine buys Ninth Sluice's pledges in CY 312) — the Lows' own insurance becomes the bank's collateral.
- **Fake drownings:** the Undertow and Widow Pruitt "drown a debtor on paper" — a name struck off the dry-tithe roll after a flood, a new name in another sluice. Consequence: the Lows' death rolls are unreliable — election arithmetic in S6; the method by which a main hero "dies".
- **Ear-money (SEC-029):** an Undertow **under-boss** buys "returned" children from Gleaner novices and sells them as debt-walkers. Silt does not know until S4; her purge of him (S4E6) exposes the Gleaners.
- **Hiring (SEC-024, sealed):** in CY 314 the Undertow hired out men for a job that Mother Silt never forgives herself. *Who hired them and for what — sealed.* Her **book of sold men** begins there (S4E10) → Tibb (S9) → Pell (S10E11).
- **Upward consequence:** besides the above — when Ninth Sluice was "let go" (CY 312) the Undertow's dry-pledges failed with it; Silt hides Hollis in S2 because the ledger copy is the only weapon she has against the bank that bought her pledges.

## The Listeners (Fennick's)

Informers and secret police of House Fennick (`HS-006-fennick.md`): ~1,500 part-time eyes, ~200 full-time "hares" under Captain Fitch (CH-0053). Grey hooded coats with a yellow cord at the throat, hidden when working; signal by whistles (three-note calls — the whistles of S1E2). Paid per report; **buy eyes** in the Lows (S1E2's stolen tithe box). From CY 312 they enforce "Listener Law" (S4) and fire on the singers of the Thousand Voices (S5E11).

## The Quiet (only what is allowed)

A secret society in the Lows and the Sunk, cells of three ("three keep it, none sings it"), sign: a spiral drawn in mud. Keeps **the old lullabies**. Has lost children to the Gleaners for three centuries. The Oshra line keeps the last verse in it (SEC-003). **Everything else about the Quiet is `[SEALED — ask lead]`** — this file adds no structure, leaders or holdings.

As a *power* (for house politics only): the Quiet owns nothing, votes nothing, and appears in no ledger; its weight is that every Lows mother who still hums is, knowingly or not, the Choir's only source of singers (`HS-013-choir.md`).
