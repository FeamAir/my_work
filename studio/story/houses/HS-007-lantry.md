> Note for the keeper: Lantry holds **no Spire**. Its Conclave voice is the **Hill Voice** — the former second voice of House Harrow, attached since CY 1 to the Crown Spire's surrender (`HS-009-harrow.md`; `power_and_money.md` §1). The head of Lantry is not hereditary: the partners of **the Table** elect a Master or Mistress of the Count every seven years (Ursel elected CY 291, re-elected 298, 305, 312).

## Public history

**From grain-factors to the realm's paper.** Hendrik Lantry was a Highwater grain-factor who lent silver to Dask barges against their cargo. In CY 220 (EV-0252) he and two partner families (Merriden, HS-016; later Orsley, HS-031) opened the **Counting House** on the Steps: a vault, a ledger, and a promise — a Lantry receipt could be redeemed in silver on demand.

## True history

- **SEC-007 — Account Nine.** Lantry issues dry-notes against land it expects to be **let flood**: it pre-sells the future "reclaimed" plots of districts it knows will be sung last, and prints notes against them before the water comes. In CY 312 Account Nine is Ninth Sluice, "to be reclaimed", closing on the third Still Day. By S6 about **70% of all dry-notes are unbacked**. Revealed S6E7. Numbers: `power_and_money.md` §5.
- **SEC-006** (as `saga_arc.md` S6 states it): Hollis's father forged the Dry Charter and was killed by Ursel (EV-0203, EV-0204). Revealed S6E8. Details belong to `writer-characters`; this file only records that the Charter is the bank's legal foundation.
- **SEC-013.** Ursel, with Garrick Dask, ordered Ruel Corvane's murder (S1E8). Revealed S2E11.
- **SEC-015.** Ursel bribed Fennick's steward so that the Sluice Spire was not retuned on Still Day 3, CY 312 — to close Account Nine on time. Revealed S2E6.
- **SEC-016.** In S6, after the crash, Ursel's step-grandson **Piers Lantry** joins Quell salvage-brokers in inventing the Pearl Pretender to sell Lirenne salvage bonds. Revealed S8E6.
- **The Exchange Panic (no new secret — a pattern).** The "lost treasure fleet" rumour of CY 268 was a relay message sold early; Lantry bought before the panic. The audience can be shown this as one line in S6 (Ursel's POV) — it is the same trick Bede Lark plays in CY 312, sixty years later.
- **Account Five (canon, EV-0200).** The reclamation accounts are numbered in order of opening (`power_and_money.md` §5). Account Five is the precursor of Account Nine: in CY 295 Lantry paid for the Sluice Spire's retuning to be delayed, and the Night of Still Water followed (truth recorded in canon EV-0200; prequel P5). Account Nine (CY 312) is the same operation, repeated on the rest of the district.

## Culture and look

- **Never raise the voice.** The Counting House is the quietest building in Highwater: felt floors, ledgers turned with ivory blades, a bell that rings only at the evening count (S1E1) and for a death-tone. A clerk who is trusted "gets a clip" (Hollis's brass ledger-clip).
- **The Table.** The partner families sit at one long black table in Harrow Hall (bought CY 252, now the **Table House**, PL-0071). Every seven years they elect the head. Ursel won in CY 291 over her stepson's claim and has won every election since by knowing each partner's debts.
- **Dress:** black wool and black silk with copper buttons and chains; pince-nez on copper chains (Ursel); clerks in long grey coats with ink-stained cuffs (Hollis), copper ledger-clips as rank badges (brass for juniors, copper for seniors, black iron for the Table).
- **Architecture:** the **Counting House** — black stone, copper-sheathed doors, a great hall of standing desks under clerestory windows, the vault below the waterline of the Steps (the only dry vault below sea level in the realm — a boast); a brass balance over the door that tips with the tides.
- **Household:** clerks (Hollis, Fossett), Merriden notaries, door-wardens; no soldiers — the bank **hires free companies** (Lantern Company, Wet Saints) with its own notes.

## Notes for production

- **Five recognisers:** black + copper; the copper balance with water in one pan; black silk, copper chains, pince-nez, ink-stained grey clerks; black stone hall with copper doors and standing desks; a dry clockwork figure with a coin-drop, tied to no home tone.
- **Sigil drawing:** a copper beam balance, the left pan holding a level surface of blue-grey water, the right pan empty — and **level** (the joke: water weighs the same as nothing). Field black.
- **Colour cautions:** black is Lantry's main colour; Rusk's black is secondary to rust red. Copper must not read as Dask's bronze — keep it pinkish and polished.
- **Leitmotif note:** the only house theme with no Spire home tone — it is built on the Relay's tone-codes (canon): the bank "sings" nothing, it only listens to prices. In S6 the clockwork slows and stops on the night of the fire.
