> Note for the keeper: Quell is not a bloodline. It is a **house of five clans**; the clan-moot (the **Hull Moot**) elects a **Captain-Lord**, who takes the name *Quell* for life or until deposed. ### Public history

**The wreck-singers.** Before Lirenne fell, the barrier islands were the city's outer wall: salt-rakers, fishers, pilots and the **wreck-singers** of Brackwater, who sang the Storm Spire for ships in distress. At the Crowning of the Keepers (~OS 400) the four clans of the inner islands (Rhee, Jutt, Skarra, Tarrow) were sworn together as one warden family under a captain they chose themselves — the outer-isle Varra joined in CY 96, making five; the Keepers called them by the name of their Spire's gift — *quell*, the stilling of the sea.

**The Breaking.** The Chronicle says Quell boats "worked the Sunk for a week after the drowning". Quell signed the Charter in CY 1 and was granted, in CY 3, the **Sunk Charter** (EV-0264): the right of salvage over the whole drowned city of Lirenne and the shallows around it "until the city be raised or the sea give it back". Nobody thought the clause would ever matter.

**Alliances and debts (CY 312).** Natural ally of Corvane at sea (the Pilots' Peace), natural enemy of the Relay. Owes nobody; is owed ~30,000 marks by Corvane (unwritten). Wrecker money funds Corvane — Jaro's secret. In S6 the Tarrow salvage-brokers partner Piers Lantry (SEC-016).

### True history

- **The Sunk Charter has a second edge.** The Charter of the Ring (RULE-009) gives the Sunken Vote to "whoever holds Lirenne". The Sunk Charter gives Quell *salvage* of Lirenne "until the city be raised". Clan lawyers have argued for two centuries over a tavern table that Quell already *holds* Lirenne. No Captain-Lord has dared to say it in the Ring. In S6 the brokers put it on paper — that is what the **Lirenne salvage bonds** sell (`world.md` §7).
- **SEC-016.** The "Pearl Pretender" (S6) is a fake invented by Quell salvage-brokers (Clan Tarrow) together with Piers Lantry, to sell Lirenne salvage bonds after the dry-note crash. Lio believes it. (Revealed S8E6.)
- **The Storm Spire is sung softer.** Ships that did not pay the calm-fee get a thinner song; the Cantors (rented from Pale Crag) are paid a "quiet bonus" by the moot to follow the fee-list, not the storm.

### Culture and look

- **The Hull Moot.** Each Still Days, on the beach under the Storm Spire, the five clan-chiefs sit in an upturned hull. A Captain-Lord can be deposed by three clans of five. Every Captain-Lord keeps changing sides because he must keep three clans happy — Jaro's politics in one sentence.
- **Sound:** the islands whistle, knock and stamp; Quell sailors' names are j/r names (Jaro, Jinna, Rourke). The Shoals are the loudest place where the Hush Law is obeyed.
- **Dress:** sea-green oilskins, bone buttons and bone ear-rings, knitted caps; captains wear a green sash with an iron ring threaded on it (the eel through the ring); wreckers carry hooded lanterns with green glass.
- **Architecture:** **Brackwater** — stilted grey-wood town on sandbars around the Storm Spire, rope bridges, salt pans glittering white; on the outer bar the **false-light towers** (officially "beacons").
- **Faith:** the Lady Below openly; the Choir at the Spire only.

#### The house scheme on three floors — false lights and the soft song
| Floor | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Steps — invents** | the Captain-Lord and the moot | The **calm-fee**: a ship pays at the Brackwater bar to be "sung through". Non-payers get a softer song; some are led onto the bar by **false lights** and "salvaged". |
| **Mids — executes** | rented Cantors (the quiet bonus), salvage-brokers of Clan Tarrow, wreck-masters of Clan Rhee, harbour-clerks who keep the fee-list | brokers value the cargo before the ship is wrecked; Rhee boats are on the water before the cry. |
| **Lows — pays** | crews of wrecked ships; Lows divers who strip wrecks in winter water; island children who keep the false-light fires | |

*Upward consequence:* wrecker silver quietly pays Corvane's fleet (the S2 blockade of Highwater is partly paid by drowned merchant crews); in **S6** Quell brokers and Piers Lantry float the **Lirenne salvage bonds** and a pretender to sell them — splitting the Lows' vote in the S6 election; in **S9** the bondholders pay for the Raising's boats.

### Notes for production

- **Five recognisers:** sea-green + bone; the green eel coiled through an iron ring; oilskins, knitted caps, bone ear-rings, green sashes; grey stilted town, salt pans, beacon towers; a whistled 6/8 figure on B with hull-knocks.
- **Sigil drawing:** a sea-green eel making one full coil through a dark iron ring, head up, mouth closed; field bone (warm off-white). Not a snake: draw the fin ridge.
- **Colour cautions:** sea-green must stay green (with yellow in it). **Never teal** — teal is Ashare's, and in S6 the Pretender's banners are teal: the audience must see the difference between Quell's green and the Pretender's teal on the same barge.
- **Crowds:** oilskin hoods and lanterns at night; boats from above.
