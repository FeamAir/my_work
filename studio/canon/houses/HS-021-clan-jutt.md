- **What:** the richest clan — Brackwater's salt pans. Jinna Quell (CH-0242), deposed Captain-Lord (CY 303), is their elder.
- **Scheme:** Jutt sells salt to the Undertow for the Lows' fish-curing at a "Lows price" and to the Steps at a "Steps price"; the difference finances Jinna's moot campaign to unseat Jaro.
- **Use:** S2 — Jinna tries to depose Jaro at the Hull Moot during the blockade (two Still Days to buy three clans); S9 — Jutt votes Jaro out of the movement.
