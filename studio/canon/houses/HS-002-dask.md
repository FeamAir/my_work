- **Epithet:** the Granary
- **Power:** grain (most of the delta's bread), dike-labour, the polder levies (big, poorly trained).
- **Character:** cold, patient, transactional; marries for land; never forgets a debt. Conquered founding House Moll (EV-0250).
- **Scheme:** cornering grain before flood seasons; "relief bread" at triple price through Undertow middlemen. Consequence: Dask's vote is always for sale for bread or water - to Odo in S1, to Maren in S7; desperate when the polders dry in CY 314.
- **Heads:** Lucan (Helm; dies S01E01); Garrick inherits "everything but one sealed chest" (S01E12). Dask polders flooded deliberately in S10.
