- **What:** the wreck-masters of the outer bar; keepers of the **false-light towers** (officially beacons). Jaro Quell's birth clan; his father Rourke led it.
- **Scheme:** false lights for non-payers of the calm-fee; first on the water at a wreck. Rhee silver is the **wrecker money that funds Corvane** (Jaro's secret, `cast_overview.md`).
- **Use:** S1E4 — Jaro's grin, "Calm is bought"; S2 — the blockade fleet's wages; S9 — Rhee breaks with the movement when Jaro does.
