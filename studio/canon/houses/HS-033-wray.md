- **What:** hereditary keepers of the **Concord Arsenal** and its powder mills in the Highwater Mids, sworn to the Helm's office; they buy saltpeter (from Rusk), mill powder (with Guild mill-wrights), and stamp every barrel with the **Arsenal ring-stamp** — the "Concord stamp".
- **Fact for SEC-024 staging (no culprit):** Arsenal barrels are re-used for years and travel everywhere — Rusk stores, Corvane ships, free companies, smugglers. The stamp says where a barrel was *made*, never whose powder was in it. In S6E4 (the barrel-staves echo) a Wray clerk can explain exactly that to a hero — which is why the staves prove nothing until S7E8.
- **Use:** S3 (powder from Wray mills at the Stair), S4, S6E4.
