- **What:** descended from Thessa Moll and a Dask second son (CY 89). Hold the Reach for the Barrows. **Garrick Dask's mother, Hilde, was a Reach Dask** — Garrick is a quarter Moll by public record.
- **Scheme:** the Reach Dasks quietly run the Kellow knights' grievances to their own advantage: whenever the Barrows weakens, they offer to "keep the Reach quiet" — for land.
- **Use:** S4 — Garrick's cousins want the Reach in the bank foreclosure; S7 (Garrick POV) — the family mirror for the Broken Dike reading; S10 — candidates to inherit Dask after Garrick drowns.
