- **What:** given **Rimgate** (the one terrace fort Rusk kept after the Kite War) in CY 206; in the same year the first Hadden married a **Tekka** clanswoman under the peace — Ossari blood in every generation since. Translators, guides, wardens of the upper road. **Dagny Hadden** married Brenna's son Ardo — the Rusk heir Sindre is part Ossari (`characters/_minor-houses.md`; head of Rusk from CY 313-09, DEC-30).
- **Secret (SEC-039, season-tier):** the Haddens guide the quartermasters' **back-trade** up the **Goat Stair** (PL-0073) — an unguarded goat-path up the escarpment two miles from the Stair. *Upward consequence:* whoever knows the Goat Stair can move men or powder past the Stair. Decided (DEC-40): a small Ossari party uses it in the Battle of the Stair (S3E10) - two faults of the house, not one.
- **Livery (DEC-61):** rust red with **one sand-orange sleeve** — the Exchange of Sleeves (RULE-023) worn as livery; the sigil keeps its sand-orange kite.
- **Use:** S1E4 — a Hadden officer laughing with the hostage boy in his own language; S3 — divided loyalties in the siege; S4 — the river route under occupation.
