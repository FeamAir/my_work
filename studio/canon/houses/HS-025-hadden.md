- **What:** given **Rimgate** (the one terrace fort Rusk kept after the Kite War) in CY 206; in the same year the first Hadden married a **Tekka** clanswoman under the peace — Ossari blood in every generation since. Translators, guides, wardens of the upper road. **Dagny Hadden** married Brenna's son Ardo — the Rusk heir Sindre is part Ossari (`characters/_minor-houses.md`, proposal).
- **Secret (SEC-039, season-tier):** the Haddens guide the quartermasters' **back-trade** up the **Goat Stair** (PL-0073) — an unguarded goat-path up the escarpment two miles from the Stair. *Upward consequence:* whoever knows the Goat Stair can move men or powder past the Stair. Question to the lead whether the Ossari use it in the Battle of the Stair (DEC-40).
- **Use:** S1E4 — a Hadden officer laughing with the hostage boy in his own language; S3 — divided loyalties in the siege; S4 — the river route under occupation.
