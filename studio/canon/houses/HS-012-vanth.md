### Public history

Wardens of the **Falls Spire** at the top of the Red Escarpment, where the Aun falls ~300 m from the plateau. Their seat was the cliff-town that Rusk later rebuilt as **the Stair**; Rusk was their sworn sword-house, keeping the road. Vanth held a voice in the Ring from CY 1.

**The fall (CY 138–141: the Vanth Treason, EV-0150, and the First Upland War, EV-0151 — prequel window).** The Choir found that Vanth was singing the Falls Spire less than the law required. Official version (Choir and Rusk chronicles): *"Vanth was attainted for selling the Stair"* — to the upland clans. The Choir declared it treason; with Ilvane backing, Helm Evander Ilvane's writ named Rusk "the arm of the Concord"; in the **First Upland War** the upland clans fought for Vanth, and lost. Rusk stormed the Stair in CY 141. Vanth was destroyed; its voice and Spire went to Rusk; survivors fled up the escarpment.

**Heads:** Gethin (~OS 690 – CY 28) · Hywel (28–66) · Ithel (66–104) · Tegwen (104–136) · the last Lady of Vanth (136–141, CH-0280 — **name reserved for P4**; lead to choose).

### True history

- **SEC-008 (as it touches Vanth).** The detuned Spires pull river water from the uplands. In CY 138–141 Vanth discovered this and sang the Falls Spire less to let the Aun reach the uplands, and traded with the upland clans (EV-0150); the Choir called it treason and Rusk destroyed them. Revealed S7E10. **Never confirmed in public text.**
- **Survivors.** Vanth survivors were taken in by the Ossari river clan **Edakka** (at the Shelf lakes) and kept a copy of the Vanth **"river book"** — the record of the falls' height against the Spire's song — which Rusk believed it had burned. Kadu's family has kept it since CY 141 (one of the three records of S7E10). Kadu himself is `writer-characters`'.
