> Words, sigil and colour are proposals (brick orange — check against Ossari sand-orange: brick is red-brown and dark, sand-orange is light and dusty). Dorran Strand (CH-0041) Guildmaster since CY 305 (proposal).

- **What:** the engineers of the Mids — pumps (wind and treadmill), dikes, locks, sluice-gates, the powder mills (with House Wray, HS-033). Founded CY 177 when the Highwater lock-builders and millwrights merged.
- **Power:** machines that work without the ear; jobs; and the **river gauges** — marked posts at every lock from the Polders to the sea, read daily since CY 190. Nobody on the Steps reads them. They become evidence in S7 (SEC-008 is proven by "Dorran's river gauges, the Choir's song logs, and the Vanth river book").
- **Ambition:** a **voice in the Ring** — "pumps vs. songs", the rise of the middle class. The Guild argues the Hill Voice precedent: if a bank can hold a voice without a Spire, so can the men who keep the dikes.
- **Numbers (CY 312):** ~140 treadmill pumps and ~60 wind-pumps in the Highwater Lows; ~2,000 guild masters and journeymen; ~5,000 treadmill walkers on the wheels (paid or debt-bound). RULE-007: ~400 walkers a day undo one hour of a Spire's silence.

**The yearly "breakage" (SEC-028, revealed S5E8).** Before every Still Days, guild journeymen **saw the axle-pins** of old pumps so they snap under load; the sluice-wardens must buy replacement pins and axles (~15 marks a pump) out of the dry-tithe. About 30 pumps "break" each year. In CY 312 all three Ninth Sluice pumps jammed on the flood night (S1E10) — doubling the dead. Corr the Reedmaster pocketed a clean-sawn pin. Corr's line: *"They always break the week before the Still. Funny, that."* (S1E5).
