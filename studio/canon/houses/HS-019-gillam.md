- **What:** masters of the Dask grain fleet (~300 barges) and the lock-crews of the Polder canals; loyal Dask men.
- **Scheme — flag-hiring:** the Gillams rent **other houses' seals** for their barges (a Fennick yellow seal costs 20 marks a convoy) so no lock-keeper dares stop them — S1E5's barges under Fennick's seal. *Upward consequence:* in S2E11 the Gillam barges block the canal at the Sluice Gates — one of Maren's three defeats.
- **Use:** S1E5, S2E11; S4 — the barges lie on the mud of a dropping Aun.
