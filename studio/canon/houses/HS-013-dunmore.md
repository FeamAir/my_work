- **What:** hereditary wardens of the Seventh to Twelfth sluices — they work the sluice-gates, keep the dry-tithe rolls of the lower Lows, and hold the keys of the **evacuation gates** to the Mids. Ex-Harrow vassals, Fennick's since CY 251. A small grey house on a stone dike above Ninth Sluice.
- **Scheme — the pass list (three floors, DEC-55):** in every flood the warden's clerk *issues* **gate passes** (5 marks a family) to whoever can pay; the Rusk quartermaster's office strikes *extra* copies; guardsman Ansel Fray (CH-0075) sells them at the gate (S1E4) and works the gate in S1E10. Holders of passes go through first. *Upward consequence:* S1E10 — Kes opens the gate against orders ("Not twice"); S1E12 she is stripped of rank and sent to the Stair — where S3 needs her.
- **Use:** S1 — the warden's clerk counting passes at the gate (a face for the scheme); S2 — the Dunmores collect the dry-tithe from a district that no longer exists ("reclaimed").
