- **Scheme — saltpeter sold twice:** the **gate book** and the **cliff book** (`HS-004-rusk.md`). *Upward consequence:* S3E10–11 the Ossari fire Rusk powder; CY 314: 100 Concord-stamped barrels written off from the exile stock (SEC-024 — buyer sealed).
