- **What:** one of the three founding partner families of the Counting House; hereditary **notaries of the Dry Book** — every dry-note issue carries a Merriden countersignature certifying that deeds of equal value are in the vault. Ursel Lantry was born a Merriden (proposal — coordinate with writer-characters; `characters/_minor-houses.md`).
- **Scheme:** Merriden notaries countersign Account Nine's issues knowing the "reclamation title" behind them is land under water or about to be. Their protection: a private **counter-ledger** of every unbacked issue, kept since CY 295 "against the day".
- **Use:** S1 — the countersigning hand in the private office (Hollis sees the copper ring); S6 — when the bank runs, the Merridens try to sell the counter-ledger to whoever is winning (Maren? Odo's successors? the bondholders?) — an auction scene. Their betrayal speeds Ursel's fall.
