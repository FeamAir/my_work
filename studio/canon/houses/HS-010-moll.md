### Public history

A **sword house**: the dike-levy masters of the eastern river flats, sworn to Lirenne (~OS 400) to raise the labour that built and held dikes. No Spire, no voice in the Ring. Their power was hands — thousands of dike-workers — and the dike-courts that judged who worked how many days.

**The Furrow War (CY 87–88, EV-0250).** In the flood year CY 87 the Moll dikes broke and the Moll polders drowned while the Dask polders held. The Chronicle: *"The lords of Moll cut their own dikes to drown their neighbours, and the sea drowned them instead."* Dask levies marched in to "rebuild the Reach"; Lord **Rawlin Moll** was killed at the dike of Hobb's Gate (CY 88). Helm Galen Corvane sanctioned the conquest. Rawlin's daughter **Thessa Moll** (CH-0276) was married to a Dask second son in CY 89 — the cadet line **Dask of the Reach** (HS-018).

**Heads:** Tobin (~OS 690 – CY 30) · Yorath (30–61) · Rawlin (61–88, CH-0275).

**Who took what:** the Moll polders, its levies and dike-courts → Dask. Moll blood survives in the Dask of the Reach cadets (Garrick Dask's mother was one — `characters/_minor-houses.md`) and in the rebel polder villages around the **Kellow** knights (HS-017), who still keep the straw-and-black ribbons.

### True history

**The Broken Dike (secret SEC-034; see `HS-002-dask.md`).** Moll did not cut its dikes. Dask Cantors sang the Sheaf Spire short over the Reach for three nights in a storm. The Kellow knights' grievance-song says so; the Dask song-log of CY 87 proves it.

### Notes for production

Straw yellow + black; dike-spade and reed; workers' smocks; the clapped work-rhythm. Appears in history montages, the Kellow sub-plot, and the S10 Battle of the Polders (old ribbons on new levies).
