- **What:** the fishing fleet of the Shoals — and, under the Sunk Charter (CY 3), the licensed **salvage-brokers of the Sunk**: they value, auction and register everything dredged from drowned Lirenne.
- **Secret (SEC-016):** in S6 Tarrow brokers, with Piers Lantry, invent the **Pearl Pretender** to sell **Lirenne salvage bonds** (`world.md` §7 §6). Revealed S8E6.
- **Use:** S6E5 — bonds sold from barges in the Lows; S8 — the brokers' books; S9 — the bondholders' committee pays for the Raising's boats.
