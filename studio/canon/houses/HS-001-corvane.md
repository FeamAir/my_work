- **Epithet:** the Sentinel
- **Power:** the navy (most of the Concord fleet), harbour tolls, river-mouth customs.
- **Character:** proud old sea-war house; reformist in rhetoric, ruthless in practice; loves the Lows as a recruiting ground. Absorbed founding House Ostry by marriage (EV-0251).
- **Scheme:** captains sell "storm-berths"; the fleet's powder accounts are padded. Consequence: the guns run dry at the Sluice Gates (EV-0308).
- **Heirloom:** "the Admiral's Pearl" - the head of the house's black pearl ring, hinged and hollow, "won at Lirenne" by the founding admiral. The family knows no more. Custody: world.md §6.
- **Heads:** Ruel (to S01E09); Maren as heir from his death; Lady Ysa is regent of Gullmouth (S1-S2).
