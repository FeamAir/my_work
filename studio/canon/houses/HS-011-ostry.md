### Public history

A **sword house** of the river mouth: harbour forts on the sandbars of the **Ostry Roads**, the lantern towers that guided ships over the Gullmouth bar, and the pilots' guild. Sworn to Lirenne (~OS 400) as "the lantern of the river". No Spire, no voice.

**The end (CY 119–120, EV-0251).** Lord **Weylin Ostry** and his heir were lost at sea in a fog on the Ostry bar, sailing under a Corvane escort. His sister **Elowen Ostry** (CH-0279) inherited and married **Wystan Corvane** (CY 120). The Ostry Roads passed to Corvane "by marriage"; the cadet line **Corvane of the Roads** (HS-027) still keeps the forts and the lantern towers. Elowen's son Rendal Corvane was "the first of both bloods".

**Heads:** Kenver (~OS 695 – CY 31) · Gwilym (31–70) · Callis (70–112) · Weylin (112–119, CH-0278) · Elowen (119–120, then by marriage to Corvane).

### True history

**The fog (secret SEC-035; see `HS-001-corvane.md`).** Wystan Corvane's escort captain "lost" Weylin's ship on the bar on purpose. A family story of the Corvane-of-the-Roads cadets; no living main character knows it. Optional colour for S2 (the fall of Gullmouth: "your house has opened harbours before").

### Notes for production

Dark red + white; lantern towers on black sandbar forts; the three-stroke harbour bell. Appears in Gullmouth establishing shots (the towers still stand) and the S2 fall of Gullmouth.
