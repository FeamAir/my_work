- **What:** wardens of the First to Fourth sluices — the "upper Lows", nearest the Mids, with brick houses and shops. Richer than Dunmore; despise them.
- **Scheme — buying First Song:** every year the Cobbets buy **priority songs** from the Choir for their own four sluices (paid out of the dry-tithe they collect, ~2,000 marks a year), and a copy of the Tithe Map draft from the Map Office (Inchley). Their sluices have not flooded since CY 220.
- **Use:** S1E5 — the contrast street (dry, upper sluices) on Pell and Ione's walk; S5 — the Hum's first circle meets in a Cobbet warehouse that was sung dry while the Ninth drowned.
