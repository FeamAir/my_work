- **What:** stewards of Pale Crag since CY 1 — they run the cloister's lands, the novices' dormitory, the Gleaners' pay, and keep **two rolls**: the **White Roll** (foundling novices "blessed by the Lady") and the **Ash Roll** (true origin, price paid, fate — including children "returned to the Lady").
- **Lady Cressa Ilvane was born a Pallis** (proposal; her parents Tamlyn and Imre Pallis, `characters/_minor-houses.md`). She grew up among the rolls without ever reading the Ash Roll — the stewards' children are taught not to.
- **Use:** S3 (Ione traces the Gleaners — a Pallis clerk is her first lead); S4E6 (ear-money); S5 — Aurin takes the Ash Roll and gives it to Cressa: her own family's handwriting (SEC-005 revealed S5E6).
