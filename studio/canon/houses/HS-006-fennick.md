### Public history

**Clerks of the Relay.** In CY 9 Helm Aldous Harrow chartered **the Relay** (EV-0265): echo-stone stations from Spire to Spire, so that a flood at Gullmouth could be known at Highwater in minutes. He gave the codebooks to his own steward's family, the Fennicks — clerks who could keep a secret. The Fennicks served House Harrow and the Helm's office as **Relay-Masters** for 240 years.

**Alliances and debts (CY 312).** Client of Lantry since the elevation (owes ~60,000 marks and its legitimacy). Enemy of Corvane ("law vs. sea"). Useful to everyone. Old feud with Quell over the islands' refusal of Relay towers.

### True history

- **The elevation was bought (no new secret — mechanics only).** Lantry paid the two swing votes of CY 251 and, in return, Fennick's Relay-Master witnessed the last will of Lord Osbert Harrow that left Harrow's *other* voice, the Hill Voice, to "the holder of his debts" — Lantry (see `HS-009-harrow.md`, secret SEC-036). Fennick and Lantry were born as great powers in the same week, from the same deathbed.
- **The Sluice Spire is under-sung.** Fennick pays its Sluice Spire's Cantors the lowest rent in the realm and books more Cantor-hours than it buys. By CY 312 the Sluice Spire has three Cantors, one of them mute (Sabin) — `HS-090-choir.md`, census.
- **SEC-015.** Ursel Lantry bribed Fennick's steward so that the Sluice Spire was not retuned on Still Day 3, CY 312; Cantor Sabin took the purse. **Odo did not know.** Revealed S2E6 (Hollis matches the purse).
- **Odo did not poison the Helm** — everyone thinks he did (SEC-001 is Ruel's).
- **The relay-keepers' private trade.** Below the lord's sight, keepers sell messages early on their own account. In CY 312 Bede Lark (CH-0052) sold the news of the Tithe Map "to an admiral and a bank, the same news, twice" — which set off both murders of S1.

### Culture and look

- **Ears, not swords.** Fennicks are trained from childhood in code-tones, shorthand and memory games; a Fennick child recites the relay codebook before the Choir's prayers. Zev "will not touch the Relay keys" (S1E2) — a scandal in this house.
- **Dress:** slate-grey robes with sulphur-yellow linings, shaved heads or close-cropped hair for the men of the house (Odo's shaved head), a silver hare's-ear brooch for family; Listeners in grey hooded coats with a yellow cord at the throat — hidden under the collar when working.
- **Architecture:** the **Listening House** — a tall narrow stone house in the Steps with a relay tower on its roof, a thousand pigeon-holes of message slips, a whispering gallery where every word in the hall carries to the master's desk. Relay towers: slim grey towers with echo-stones in iron cradles.
- **Household:** clerks, Relay-Wardens (Tennant), Hearings ushers, the **Grey Hares** (Odo's household guard), the Listeners (Captain Fitch, CH-0053).

#### The house scheme on three floors — delayed messages, sold verdicts, the cheap song
| Floor | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Steps — invents** | the Lord and the Masters of the Relay and the Hearings | Market messages (grain prices, flood warnings, ship arrivals) are **delayed** by an hour for the public and sold "first-tone" to subscribers; Hearings verdicts are **fast-tracked** for a speed fee or **delayed** for a slower one; the Sluice Spire is **under-sung** to save Cantor rent. |
| **Mids — executes** | relay-keepers, Relay-Wardens (Tennant), Hearings clerks, Listeners | keepers hold tones in the stone-cradle until the subscriber's runner leaves; clerks lose files; Listeners buy eyes in the Lows (S1E2's tithe-box puzzle). And, on their own account, keepers **sell messages early** — twice if they can. |
| **Lows — pays** | Ninth Sluice and the lower sluices (the Sluice Spire's districts); Lows litigants with no Mids sponsor | flood warnings reach the Lows last; a commoner cannot testify against a noble without a Mids sponsor; the cheap song lets the water in (CY 295, CY 312). |

*Upward consequence:* one keeper's double sale in CY 312 sets off **both murders of S1** (Ruel learns of the Tithe Map → poisons the Helm; Ursel learns that Ruel knows → has Ruel killed). The under-sung Sluice Spire is why a single bribed retuning (SEC-015) is enough to drown Ninth Sluice.

### Notes for production

- **Five recognisers:** slate grey + sulphur yellow; the grey hare with raised ears; grey robes with yellow linings, shaved heads, the hare's-ear brooch; a narrow stone house crowned by a relay tower, walls of pigeon-holes; a ticking pizzicato over a drifting C-sharp.
- **Sigil drawing:** a seated grey hare in profile, both ears straight up and slightly apart, one eye visible; field sulphur yellow. Keep the yellow acid, not gold.
- **Colour cautions:** slate grey is Fennick's — Corvane silver is a metal accent, never a field; keep Listeners' coats matte grey.
- **Two greys (v0.6, DEC-66):** the house colour (**slate grey**) and the colour of the Helm's office (**storm-grey**, the field of the Concord flag, `world.md` §3.2) are **different greys**: storm-grey is lighter and warmer. From S1E11/S1E12 Fennick banners and the Helm's flag stand side by side (Odo Helm from S1E12) - they must stay tellable in every frame. A Fennick Helm keeps the slate house colour on his person; the storm-grey belongs to the office.
- **Crowds:** Listeners are hooded and faceless by design; relay towers at night with stones glowing faintly.

## The Listeners (approved r3) — writer-houses, 2026-11-22 · merged in v0.3


Informers and secret police of House Fennick (`HS-006-fennick.md`): ~1,500 part-time eyes, ~200 full-time "hares" under Captain Fitch (CH-0053). Grey hooded coats with a yellow cord at the throat, hidden when working; signal by whistles (three-note calls — the whistles of S1E2). Paid per report; **buy eyes** in the Lows (S1E2's stolen tithe box). From CY 312 they enforce "Listener Law" (S4) and fire on the singers of the Thousand Voices (S5E11).
