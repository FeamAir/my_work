- **Epithet:** the Stair
- **Power:** the best standing army (pike-and-shot), the only easy road from the uplands, saltpeter trade.
- **Character:** soldier house, plain-spoken, poor in coin, rich in honour; a founding "sword house" that won its Spire by destroying House Vanth in the First Upland War (EV-0151).
- **Scheme:** quartermasters sell Ossari saltpeter twice. Consequence: the Ossari guns at the Stair fire Rusk powder (S3); a second sale in CY 314 (sealed consequence).
- **After S3:** the Stair falls (EV-0311); Rusk keeps its Conclave vote "in exile" at Highwater - the Spire is lost, the seat is not.
