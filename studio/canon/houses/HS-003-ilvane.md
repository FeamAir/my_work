- **Epithet:** the Songblood
- **Power:** the ear - most Cantors come from Ilvane or are trained at Pale Crag; dominates the Choir; sells "priority songs" in storms.
- **Character:** serene, pious, "keep the blood true"; silver-haired by birth (belief - Ione's silver is a rinse). Head: Lord Aurin; real power: his mother, First Cantor Vesna.
- **Scheme:** the Choir's debts to Lantry are paid by secret sale of storm-priority; gleaning of children paid by the head (SEC-029). Consequence: a worsening Cantor shortage - by S5 Spires sung in shifts by children, by S6 houses hire singers from the Lows.
- **Votes:** controls two votes in practice (Ilvane + Choir); they split in S1 (Aurin defies his mother, S01E12).
- **True history:** the founding legend and the bloodline are the centre of the saga's lie (SEC-003, SEC-005; further: sealed).
