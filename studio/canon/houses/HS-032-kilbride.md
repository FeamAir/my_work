- **What:** descended from Lord Osbert Harrow's unrecognised son (b. CY 240); minor Mids gentry — lawyers in the Hearings.
- **Secret (SEC-036):** they hold a copy of Osbert's **CY 247 will** leaving the Hill Voice to his son's line, not to "the holder of his debts" (`HS-009-harrow.md`).
- **Use:** S6–S7 — when the bank falls, the Purchased Seat is up for grabs; a Kilbride claim in the Hearings (Zev's Hearings, after S6) is a ready-made fight over a vote. Decided (DEC-39): S7E4–E5, Zev's first great case; Piers casts the Purchased Seat from S6E12 and through S7–S9 under challenge; the Hearings never rule - the suit is still pending when the Conclave is dissolved (S9E12).
