### Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** man of 36; lean, loose-limbed, sailor's tan; grins with one side of his mouth.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **tall and lanky**, loose swaying walk as if the floor were a deck; deep brown island skin; **missing the last two fingers of the LEFT hand** (a wrecking rope, at sixteen) — he gestures with that hand on purpose. Silhouette: long coat, long limbs, a lounging lean.
- **Hair:** **shoulder-length dark hair with the ends dyed sea-green**, a **single bone ring braided in on the RIGHT side**.
- **Eyes:** green-brown, laughing.
- **Distinctive mark:** three small tattooed dots under the RIGHT eye (one per wreck he survived — proposal).
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **sea-green long coat** with bone buttons, open over a bare-throated bone-white shirt, salt-stained boots. Main colour: sea-green.
- **Signature item:** a **green-glass wrecker's lantern lens** on a leather cord, worn as a pendant (the false lights of House Quell — he jokes that it is "a reminder to stay honest").
- **Changes:** S9 the lens is missing (he throws it into the sea when he breaks with the movement — proposal); S11 back on.

### Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** easy, lazy tenor; island drawl (long vowels, dropped endings); a laugh in it even when he threatens.
- **Speech habits:** prices everything with a smile; quotes his house words as a joke and means them; switches sides mid-sentence and explains why it was always the same side.
- **Catch-phrase:** "Calm is bought." (S1E4, with a grin)
- **Never says:** "I promise"; "loyal".
- **Sample line:** *"I'm loyal to whoever pays and respects me, Admiral. You're halfway there."*
- **Delivery:** `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 2 in S1 (E4, E12 vote); ≤5 per season after.

### Goals
- **Wants (outer):** the Shoals free of the Relay, the Helm and the bank — unrecorded trade forever.
- **Needs (inner):** to find one thing he will not sell; he finds it in S11.
- **Fears:** being bought cheaply; being on a losing deck.
- **Heroism:** he **comes back** — to the side he left, when it is losing (S11E8).

### Biography
Elected captain-lord by the island clans for luck and nerve. Natural friend of Corvane at sea, natural enemy of the Relay. Quell money — wreckers' money, salvaged from ships lured onto the Shoals by false lights — quietly funds Corvane's refit debts (secret; proposal SEC-031), which is why he will back Ruel and then Maren. In S1 he is the vote-broker Ruel courts (E4) and Quell votes for Corvane (E12); a Quell token is found on Ruel's assassin (E8, a red herring planted against him).
