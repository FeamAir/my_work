### Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** woman of 34; broad-shouldered, compact, heavy forearms; weathered upland skin.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **medium height, broad and compact**; ruddy, weathered upland skin; **stiff RIGHT knee** from a pike-thrust at twenty-two (proposal) — a slight rolling limp she ignores; stands with feet planted like a pike square. ### Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** low, gravelly alto; upland accent — rolled "r", long "o", slow; she rarely raises it because she does not need to.
- **Speech habits:** orders in three words or fewer; dry, sideways humour; calls every soldier under thirty "lad" or "lass" regardless; counts heads compulsively ("How many?").

### Goals
- **Wants (outer):** to bring her people home alive and retire to a dry farm.
- **Needs (inner):** to stop paying for one night with the rest of her life — to forgive the girl at the gate.
- **Fears:** gates; crowds pressing on a bar; being given the same order twice.
- **Heroism:** she **holds the line for others** — at the gate in S1E10 by opening it, at the Stair and at the Hundred Locks by standing.

### Biography
Born on an upland farm in the shadow of the Stair; joined House Rusk's pike at fourteen as the farm could not feed her. At seventeen, a junior guard on the Ninth Sluice evacuation gate on the Night of Still Water (CY 295, EV pending), she **shut the gate on orders** with thousands still behind it (SEC-009). Nobody knows it was her; she has never told anyone. Twenty years' service since: the border, the escarpment, the Ossari skirmishes, the Conclave guard. Kes is a sergeant because she will not take a commission ("officers eat first").

In S1 she commands the Rusk guard of the Conclave delegation in Highwater: the quay at Ruel's rally (E2), the barracks with the hostage boy Esk and his kite (E4), the door of the Ring (E6), the kill of Ruel's assassin on the Steps (E8) and the polder clay on his boots (E9). She catches one of her own guardsmen (Ansel Fray, proposal) selling gate passes and lets him off with a blow (E4). In the flood (E10) she is ordered to keep the same gate shut, sees her own hands on the bar, and opens it. Stripped of her rank, she is sent upriver with Esk.

Kes knows the Ossari better than any Concord officer, and is the first to hear Esk say "your stones drink our rivers" as more than a hostage's insult (SEC-008, from S3).
