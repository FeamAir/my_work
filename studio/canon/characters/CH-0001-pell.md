- Age band: 15 at S1 (teen: no romance, ever); 23 after the skip (S5).

- Signature item: cracked black echo-shard, no bigger than a thumbnail, on a cord at the neck (custody: world.md §6).

- Clothes: mud-grey wading leggings, patched oilskin.

## Voice
- Fast, low, Lows slang; whistles instead of singing (Hush Law).
- Sample: *"Water doesn't care who you are. That's the only fair thing in this city."*

## Goals
- Wants: to get Wick and Nan "up the Steps" before the next flood.
- Hides: she hums Nan's forbidden lullaby, and the shard answers (SEC-002).
- Fears: deep water.
- Heroism: she keeps going back into the water for other people.

## Biography
Mudlark of Ninth Sluice, raised by her grandmother Nan after her mother's death (EV-0261). Finds the shard at the lowest ebb (S01E01). Lead of the saga from Act II (after S04E11). Survives the saga; gives her voice in the finale (S12E12).
