### Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** young woman of 19; tall, lean, sailor-strong; sun on her cheekbones.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **tall, lean, long-legged**; warm brown skin, darker than her father's (her mother's side — writer-houses); quick upright stride, hands behind her back like a captain on deck. Silhouette: tall, short coat, high curly tail.
- **Hair:** **dark umber-brown curls tied high at the crown with a silver cord** — a short, bouncing tail.
- **Eyes:** grey-blue, steady.
- **Distinctive mark:** none in S1; from S2E11 a thin powder-burn scar through the LEFT eyebrow (proposal — the Sluice Gates).
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **deep-blue SHORT jacket** (cut at the hip — never her father's long coat), white shirt, grey breeches, sea boots. Main colour: deep blue (see the report: a Corvane-blue conflict with Ruel in S1E1–E9 only).
- **Signature items:** (1) **brass spyglass on her belt (right hip)**; (2) from **S6E12** her father's **black pearl ring, worn openly on the LEFT hand**. From S1E11 to S6E12 the ring is hidden (never on screen on her).
- **Changes over the saga:** S2 exile: salt-faded coat; S5 admiral's insignia; S6E12 the ring; S7+ Helm's grey-blue mantle over the coat (the director decides the Helm's regalia).

### Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** clear mezzo, crisp, commanding; Gullmouth accent like her father but harder, no warmth wasted.
- **Speech habits:** short sentences; orders, not requests; naval terms used exactly; she never fills a silence. Grows shorter each season.
- **Catch-phrase:** "Hold the line of the ships." (S1–S2) → later "Protect the name." (S1E11, `[ECHO: SEC-010]`)
- **Never says:** "I don't know"; "please" to her own captains; her father's crime aloud.
- **Sample line:** *"I wanted the sea. The sea doesn't vote."* · *"Every crew comes home or I don't."*
- **Delivery for AI (S1):** `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 4: E2 to Ruel ("I wanted the sea"), E9 at his deathbed, E11 reading the page, E12 refusing to swear.

### Goals
- **Wants (S1):** to protect her father's name. (S2–S5) to win the war her father's death started. (S6–S8) to hold the delta together.
- **Needs (inner):** to admit that the name she protects is a lie — which she never quite does.
- **Fears:** losing a crew; being her father; disorder ("the mob").
- **Heroism:** she **never abandons her crews** — and that loyalty is what turns into her danger.

### Biography
Only child of Ruel Corvane; a junior naval officer by choice, raised on decks more than in halls. She wanted the sea, not politics, and is pulled off her ship to be her father's aide during the election (S1E2) — where a Lows boy steals her cadet cap (Wick; pays S9E10). She takes a ledger copy from Hollis to her father (E7) and watches him burn one page. After his murder she stays by him on the *Patient Gull* (E9). In E11 Hollis gives her the page's twin: her father poisoned the Helm (SEC-001). At the wake she slips the black pearl ring from his finger and hides it with the page — her first compromise. Her aunt Ysa confirms her right to cast the Corvane vote "on one condition", spoken off screen (SEC-023; the condition is obedience — pays S2). In the Ring she votes for Corvane's cause, loses on the Sunken Vote, refuses to swear to Odo, and sails with the fleet.
