**Kadu** (30) — Ossari field captain. Main from S10 (the Ossari alliance). Survives. *Function:* first seen in S3 (the lead places the episode) — a field captain with a **key tattoo**; his family has kept the **Vanth "river book"** since CY 141 — one of the three records that prove, side by side with Dorran's gauges and the Choir's song logs, that the detuned song pulls water from the uplands (SEC-008, S7E10); stands beside Pell in hiding (S10). *Secret:* **descends from House Vanth**, destroyed in CY 141 for singing the Falls Spire less (SEC-008 note). *Link:* the oldest grudge in the saga, carried by its calmest man. Proposal: Ottar is his cousin, given as a hostage-child twelve years before Esk. *Look:* compact, scarred, **long black hair in a single rope braid threaded with copper wire**, the **key tattoo on the RIGHT forearm** (design: art team; meaning: lead's S3 outline), sand-orange and dun wind-coat, horn goggles on the brow. *Voice:* slow, low, very few words; Ossari accent with old, formal Concord words nobody uses any more (the book taught him the language). *"My family kept a book for a hundred and eighty years so that one day someone would believe it. Read."*
