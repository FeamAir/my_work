### Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** woman of 62; small, upright, still; hands folded.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **small and very upright**; light-brown skin, fine wrinkles; an arthritic left hip — the stick is needed, not decorative; slow, exact steps, the stick set down first. Silhouette: a small black column with a stick.
- **Hair:** **iron-grey, pinned in a tight coil at the nape with copper pins**.
- **Eyes:** dark, amused, unblinking behind the glass.
- **Distinctive mark:** none; a widow's ring of plain copper on the RIGHT hand.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** **black gown with copper embroidery** at cuffs and collar (Lantry colours). Main colour: black.
- **Signature items:** **pince-nez on a copper chain**; **walking stick with a scale-shaped handle** (a copper balance) — she taps it once when a conversation is over.
- **Changes:** S6 — the coil looser, a single strand loose (the only time); the stick is lost in the fire (E9–E10).

### Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** soft, low, grandmotherly mezzo; slow; perfect Steps diction; she never raises her voice — the bank's words *"All water finds its level"* are her manner.
- **Speech habits:** endearments ("my dear", "child") used as weapons; praises before she cuts; speaks of people as entries ("carried", "settled", "written off").
- **Catch-phrase:** "All water finds its level." / "A clerk who is trusted gets a clip."
- **Never says:** a threat; a number aloud in company; the name Teodor.
- **Sample line:** *"His father had a beautiful hand."* (S1E11, `[ECHO: SEC-006]`) · *"Account Nine closes on the third Still Day."* (S1E3)
- **Delivery for AI:** very still — ideal for static `[MS]`; `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 3 in S1: E1 the clip, E3 Account Nine, E11 "a beautiful hand".

### Goals
- **Wants (outer):** for the bank to outlast every house.
- **Needs (inner):** none she would admit. Her one tenderness is Hollis — the son of the man she drowned.
- **Fears:** disorder in the columns; being remembered as a thief rather than a builder.
- **Heroism:** none; she is courageous only in S6, when she stays in the Counting House as it falls.

### Biography
Head of the Lantry Counting House and holder of the Purchased Seat. She built the bank's modern power on one idea: dry-notes are backed by deeds to dry land, and whoever knows in advance which land will be "let go" can sell it first (Account Nine, SEC-007). In CY 295 the clerk Teodor Kett forged the Dry Charter at the bank's direction; in CY 296 she had him drowned and raised his ten-year-old son as a charity apprentice (SEC-006). She has been genuinely kind to Hollis for sixteen years.

In S1 she buys early news from relay-keeper Bede Lark — that Ruel knows of the Tithe Map — and with Garrick Dask orders Ruel's murder (SEC-013); she bribes the Sluice Spire's retuning to be skipped (SEC-015: Cantor Sabin takes the purse) so that Account Nine "closes" on the third Still Day. The flood comes. Ninth Sluice is declared reclaimed land. In E11 she finds Hollis's desk empty.
