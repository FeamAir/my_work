### Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** man of 22; slight, neat, soft-faced, younger-looking than his age.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** **short and slight**; olive delta skin (Lows-born, unremarked in the Choir); light, silent walk; head always slightly bowed and turned toward Vesna's hands. Silhouette: a small hooded figure half a step behind a veil.
- **Hair:** **ash-brown, close-cropped, with the Voice's tonsure — a shaved band from ear to ear over the crown**.
- **Eyes:** light brown, watchful; he watches hands, not faces.
- **Distinctive mark:** none.
- **Usual clothes and colours:** Choir robe of **undyed linen** (oatmeal), a violet stole on high days. Main colour: undyed linen.
- **Signature item:** a **violet cord knotted round the LEFT wrist** — the First Cantor's colour, the mark of her Voice.
- **Changes:** S8: the violet cord is cut at his arrest.

### Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** soft, even tenor; liturgical cadence; perfectly neutral when he voices Vesna — and, when he speaks for himself (rarely, S2+), a faint Lows vowel slips through.
- **Speech habits:** as the Voice: third person ("The First Cantor says…"), never a contraction; as himself: short, gentle, a little sly.
- **Catch-phrase:** "The First Cantor says…"
- **Never says:** "I" while voicing; a word of the Lows — except once, on purpose (proposal: S2E9, so that fans can catch it).
- **Sample line:** *"The Choir owes the bank its roof."* (S1E8, softened) · as himself: *"I don't lie for her. I only choose which truth reaches you first."*
- **Delivery:** ideal AI voice — mostly `[OS]` behind Vesna; `[LIPSYNC-CU]` ≤2 in S1, ≤5 per season S2–S8.

### Goals
- **Wants (outer):** to stay at the First Cantor's side as long as he can.
- **Needs (inner):** to be someone, not a mouth.
- **Fears:** being caught; being only a voice.
- **Heroism:** he **bends the most powerful voice in the realm, one word at a time**, and dies for it.

### Biography
Officially a foundling of the Choir, gleaned young and raised at Pale Crag; chosen as the First Cantor's Voice for his perfect memory of sign and his flat, trustworthy tone. In truth he is a child of the Lows, **placed in the Choir by the Quiet** (SEC-012), and he sometimes mistranslates Vesna on purpose — softening, delaying, once or twice reversing. In S1E8 he softens "the Choir owes the bank its roof"; in S1E11 he teaches the mute Ione her first signs — the beginning of the series' sign glossary.
