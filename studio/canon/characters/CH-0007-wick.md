> Writer's note (dossier): Born Ninth Sluice (PL pending). Died at the Battle of the Ninth Bridge, S9E10 (CY 324–325; keeper to fix). Main from S5. Relations to Pell (CH-0001), Kes (CH-0004), Maren (CH-0006) live in their lower-ID records.

### Look (story-level, for the art team)
- **Age band and build:** boy of 11 (S1–S4: 11–13), skinny, quick, all knees; after the skip a young man of 19 — tall, rangy, taller than Pell.
- **Silhouette, body and bearing:** S1–S4: **skinny, small, quick**, olive-brown skin, scabbed knees; S5+: **very tall and gangly**, taller than Pell, loose-jointed walk. Proposal: after the beating on the Night of a Thousand Voices (S5) he is **deaf in the LEFT ear** and turns his head to the right to listen. Silhouette: the cap.
- **Hair:** **mouse-brown mop with one tuft at the RIGHT temple that always sticks up** (it survives the soldier's crop in S5).
- **Eyes:** sea-grey like Pell's — big, curious.
- **Distinctive mark:** gap in the front teeth (S1–S4 only; adult teeth by S5).
- **Usual clothes and colours:** S1–S4: **oatmeal hand-knitted jumper** (Pell's old one, sleeves rolled four times), rope belt, bare feet. S5+: city watch **buff-brown coat** with a grey sash.
- **Signature item:** **Maren's deep-blue Corvane cadet cap** with a small silver cormorant badge — stolen S1E2, worn **backwards** as a boy; worn properly, under his watch helmet or on the night watch, from S5. It is on his head when he dies (S9E10) and Maren finds it.
- **Changes:** S5 adult refs; the cap faded almost grey-blue.

### Voice (for the sound team)
- **Voice character:** S1–S4: high, quick, cracking with excitement; Lows accent, thicker than Pell's. S5+: a light tenor, still quick, still laughing.
- **Speech habits:** asks "why?" about everything nobody explains (S1E8 "Why do we cover our mouths?"); narrates what he sees aloud; nicknames everyone (Hollis = "Specs", Kes = "Sarge").
- **Catch-phrase:** "Pell… it's singing back." (S1E1) · later "Not my cap. Finders'."
- **Never says:** that he's scared (he says "I'm cold"); goodbye.
- **Sample line:** *"If the Lady wanted us quiet she'd have made us fish."*
- **Delivery for AI:** child lines mostly `[OS]`/`[MS]` with the cap as identifier; `[LIPSYNC-CU]` max 2 in S1 (E1 cliffhanger, E8 "why do we cover our mouths?").

### Goals
- **Wants (outer):** S1–S4: food, adventure, to be taken seriously by Pell. S5+: to protect Pell from outside the law — he joins the watch so someone inside it is on her side.
- **Needs (inner):** to be his own man, not Pell's little brother — which he becomes only by walking into her cause.
- **Fears:** being left behind; water at night.
- **Heroism:** he **stands between his sister's people and the soldiers** — in S5 and at the Ninth Bridge.

### Biography
Born four years before his mother drowned; he does not remember his father. Grew up in Nan's stilt-house, mudlarking at Pell's heels, the comic eyes of the Lows. On the lowest ebb of CY 312 he is the only one who sees the stone hum back (S1E1). On the Steps day (S1E2) he steals a cadet cap from the stiff young officer at Ruel's side — Maren — and never gives it back. His best friend is Patch, a debt-walker boy on Corr's treadmill. In the flood (E10) he goes over the roofs with Nan; on the ruins (E12) he is the one holding baby Tibb.

After Nan's death he is raised by Pell, the Undertow and the war. In the five-year skip he joins the city watch — the very force that hunts the Hum — as Pell's eyes inside it.
